# Eschatology Framework — Index

Personal theological research project: working out a preterist-leaning eschatology (Revelation 20's millennium as already fulfilled, present age as the "little season") and the historical/speculative questions that grew alongside it (ancient architecture, lost civilizations, cosmology).

This is a **multi-session, multi-tranche project**. Content arrives in chunks (originally drafted in dialogue with ChatGPT, refined here). Each tranche gets sorted into one of four files below and merged — never just appended raw.

[[00c-commission-covenant-investigation]] — the full working-out of the Israel/Gentile commission question: what the Tanakh anticipated before Yeshua's ministry (Genesis 12, Isaiah 42/49), John the Baptist (including the John/Elijah typology tension, held deliberately unresolved between "I am not Elijah" and "he is Elijah"), Yeshua's Israel-focused earthly ministry, the Canaanite/Syrophoenician woman, the Roman centurion, the post-resurrection turn, the Ethiopian eunuch (covenantal status deliberately left undetermined), Cornelius, the Jerusalem council (also read as a live case study of the epistemological cycle operating inside Scripture itself), Paul's explicit Gentile apostleship, the covenant-continuity thread, and — finally — whether/how the commission applies to individual believers today (apostolic mission ≠ spiritual gift ≠ individual charge ≠ readiness to answer ≠ visible conduct, five distinguished categories).

[[00r-what-is-a-covenant]] — **⚙️ epistemological sidebar: what a covenant actually is.** Written after Stage 17 but placed **before** it as **Stage 16.5**, because it is the conceptual machinery Stage 17 needs — the file letter records creation order, the spine records argument order, and neither was falsified to match the other. **The core distinction: the covenant is not identical to the commandments associated with it.** Seven terms held apart (covenant / promise / commandment / obligation / **sign** / blessing / curse). "Shabbat is the covenant" is imprecise — **Shabbat is a covenant *sign***, as are the rainbow and circumcision. **Produced a finding of its own: *berit olam* is attached to the rainbow, circumcision, AND Shabbat — one phrase, three institutions, three different treatments in every tradition.** Establishes the **Covenant Relationship Rule** (Scripture must state the relationship between covenants; it is never inferred from sequence), warranted by **Galatians 3:17**'s 430 years.

[[01a-romans-9-not-all-israel-is-israel]] — **the slogan, read as an argument.** Romans 9:6 is routinely extracted from something that begins five verses earlier — where **Paul's anguish is for "my kinsmen ACCORDING TO THE FLESH," and he calls his unbelieving ethnic kinsmen "Israelites" in the present tense**, decades after Pentecost. **His first example of "Israel within Israel" is not Gentile Christians but Ishmael and Isaac** — election *within* genealogical descent, not its replacement. **Malachi supplies Edom; Genesis 25 says "two nations"** — so individual vs corporate election may itself be a false either/or. **Exodus 33's "mercy on whom I have mercy" comes from after the golden calf**, which reframes the chapter: Israel exists as covenant people *because* Yahweh shows mercy. Contains a **completed grammatical verification** at 9:22–23 — the asymmetry is real (**middle-or-passive participle with no agent vs active verb with God as subject**) **and what it means is explicitly not established**. **Neither extreme survives the chapter: 9:24 destroys the ethnicity-only reading from inside.**

[[00z-recentering-the-map]] — **⚙️ STAGE 21½ — the ORIENTATION STAGE. Where we are, and why the branches were not detours.** Restates the original question and splits it in two — **ministry** (does every believer have the same calling?) and **timing** (has that proclamation already occurred?). **Locks the thesis discipline: preterism stays DOWNSTREAM of the evidence** — *the preceding texts must earn the little-season hypothesis*, not become its lens. Records three factual corrections about Judaism, sects, and the Talmud, and the standing rule that **ancient Israel ≠ the modern State**. Adds **anachronism** and its test, and **the unfalsifiability protection** — *"Satan deceives" is not evidence*. Keeps **Rupes Nigra downstream** to prevent finding-the-map-in-Revelation circularity. And restates the destination at the precision the evidence supports.

[[00y-who-are-the-people-of-god]] — **the oldest question in the project, reopened by Hebrews 4:9's own vocabulary.** Traced from Exodus 6:7's covenant formula forward: **election was never an end in itself** (Genesis 12:3), **Torah itself permits incorporation** (Exodus 12:49's "one law"), and **Isaiah 19:25 calls EGYPT "my people" while Israel remains "my inheritance."** Paul's metaphor is **grafting into an existing tree whose root supports the branches** — so *replacement* is the wrong category, and *"the Church simply is Israel"* is equally premature. **Contains a major translation divergence at Ephesians 2:15** — ESV has Messiah abolishing *the law*; CJB has him destroying *the enmity occasioned by* it. Establishes **Expansion ≠ Erasure** and **Later Inclusion ≠ Rewritten Context** — the second being the rule the whole project started from. **And the finding that vindicated the method: preserving Israel's priority made the Gentile case stronger, not weaker.**

[[00x-hebrews-4-sabbatismos-and-the-rest-that-remains]] — **where Sabbath stops being a dispute about a day and becomes a story from creation forward.** Hebrews argues **chronologically**: Psalm 95 was written *after* Joshua, yet still says "**Today**" — so Joshua's real fulfillment **did not exhaust** the promise. Then it reaches past Joshua to **Genesis 2**, shifts vocabulary from *katapausis* to ***sabbatismos***, and holds **already** (4:3, "we who have believed enter") together with **not yet** (4:11, "strive to enter"). **Contains the project's first Lexical Escalation attempt — which produced a documented GAP rather than an answer**, including a lexicon citation whose section number did not match the primary source. Establishes **Fulfillment ≠ Exhaustion with its bridge requirement** — a control that **cuts against this project's own preterist thesis as much as against futurism**. And 4:9's "**the people of God**" reopens the oldest question in the investigation.

[[00w-sabbath-creation-covenant-sign-shadow-rest]] — **the strongest test the methodology has faced, because Sabbath fits NEITHER "Mosaic, therefore temporary" NOR "creation ordinance, therefore unchanged forever."** Genesis sanctifies the seventh day before Israel exists — **but does not command Adam to keep it.** Exodus grounds it in **creation**; Deuteronomy adds **redemption**; Exodus 31 makes it an explicit **covenant sign with Israel**. **Both facts stand, and neither erases the other.** Isaiah 56 has **foreigners keeping Sabbath**; Yeshua calls Himself its **Lord** and reorients it toward mercy; Hebrews says a ***sabbatismos* remains** — where **ESV reads "Sabbath rest" and CJB reads "Shabbat-keeping,"** diverging along the exact fault line. **Full A/B/C/D classification, with Tier D marking what is unestablished in BOTH directions** — including that Messiah transformed Sabbath into Sunday. Establishes **Removal ≠ Replacement**: *fulfillment must be demonstrated; replacement must be demonstrated separately.*

[[00v-colossians-2-shadow-and-body]] — **where the *pattern → reality* model meets Paul's own word for it — and does not get the answer it wanted.** Begun **before verse 16**, because the paragraph is cosmic victory, not a menu. **The *cheirographon* brake:** the familiar claim *"the Torah was nailed to the cross"* **replaces Paul's noun** — he writes of a handwritten certificate of **debt**, not νόμος. **σκιά → σῶμα is literally shadow → body** — but **verse 17 does not answer the question it raises**: a shadow, on the body's arrival, might become obsolete, or transformed, or *valuable precisely because it reveals the reality's shape*, and **none of those is in the word.** Records the **τῶν μελλόντων** problem — *"things to come," written after the cross.* **Both readings of who-is-judging fail to absorb all the evidence, for the third consecutive stage.** Establishes **Fulfillment ≠ Abolition**, and sets up **Sabbath** as structurally unlike anything tested so far.

[[00u-acts-21-paul-torah-and-the-two-groups]] — **the control case, and the first file written under ADR-0009.** Decades after the council, **"many thousands" of Jewish believers are "all zealous for the law"** — and James, far from endorsing the rumor that Paul taught Jews to *apostatize from Moses*, asks Paul to **publicly demonstrate it false.** Contains the project's **canonical 🔬 Researcher's Commentary**: the Acts 21 sacrifice problem worked through three attempts, including the neat solution **Numbers 6:14 killed** — preserved because the failure exposed that *"sacrifice"* had been flattened into one category. **Two corrections land here**, one of them to this project's own Stage 16 wording. **Reading B of Acts 15:21 downgraded** rather than dropped. Bookmarks ***hypo nomon***. **Continues into Stage 18.9 (§19–29): Numbers 6 read on its own terms**, where **6:9–12 breaks the English category "sin offering"** — a man who merely stood near a corpse owes both a *ḥaṭṭāʾt* and an *asham*, and the text says *he sinned by reason of the dead body*. **"Pay their expenses" kills the theater reading.** Reaches **AD 70 from a second, non-prophetic direction**, and establishes the **Collapsed-Explanation Diagnostic**.

[[00t-acts-15-and-the-four-requirements]] — **where both investigations converged independently.** Circumcision arrived at the Jerusalem Council in Stage 17; food arrived straight back at it in Stage 18.6. **The dispute is *inside* the believing community** — Luke calls the Pharisee party "believers" — and their proposition has two parts: circumcise the Gentiles **and** command them to keep Torah. **Peter's "yoke" held open** against Deuteronomy 30:11, Psalm 19:7, and 1 John 5:3, which describe Yahweh's commandments very differently. **Acts 15:21's *gar* weighed:** Reading A (fellowship requirements) vs Reading B (entry requirements, instruction continues) — **with Reading B explicitly labeled an inference**, plus two further possibilities kept open. **The reductio that settles one thing outright:** the four cannot be exhaustive, or Gentiles were permitted to murder and steal. **Leviticus 17–18's *ger* overlap** examined as a serious hypothesis, with ***ger* added to the glossary and explicitly not flattened into "Gentile Christian."** **The textual asymmetry recorded and let to stand: blood retained, unclean species conspicuously absent — while 15:21 blocks "Moses no longer matters."** Establishes **Covenantal Transmission**.

[[00s-clean-and-unclean-from-noah-to-messiah]] — **the food investigation, opened and deliberately left unresolved.** Traced from **Genesis 7:2** (the distinction predates Sinai) through **Genesis 8:20** (its first explicit use is *sacrificial*) and **Genesis 9:3's real difficulty** — "every moving thing that lives shall be food for you," recorded rather than explained away. Leviticus supplies **its own stated theological reason** (holiness → separation → distinction), so the nutrition-advice reduction contradicts the text. **Mark 7's dispute is identified first — unwashed hands — and Mark 7:8 stands as a control verse.** Then the stage's sharpest finding: **Mark 7:19's Greek is a participle with no stated subject, and ESV, NIV, CJB, and RVR1960 supply four different degrees of interpretation** — RVR1960 alone keeps the participle; CJB alone inserts "ritually." **Peter's own interpretation of the sheet vision (Acts 10:28) concerns *persons*** — but the brake runs both ways, so "therefore no dietary implication" is refused too. Includes the **Acts 11 gap** (Luke never says what was on the table) and **Romans 14's two problems**: the *vegetarian* party Torah never required, and Paul's use of ***koinos* rather than *akathartos***. Establishes the **Contextual Question Principle**, **Parallel-Text Control**, and the **two-axis distinction** for "clean food."

[[00q-circumcision-covenant-sign-and-heart]] — **the second pressure test, and it broke the formula.** Circumcision **predates Moses**, so "that's just Mosaic Law" fails immediately. Genesis 15:6 credits righteousness **two chapters before** Genesis 17's sign — Paul's Romans 4 argument is chronological exegesis of Genesis, not invention. **Torah itself moves circumcision inward** (Deuteronomy 10:16 commands it of the already-circumcised; 30:6 turns it into a promise Yahweh Himself fulfills), and Jeremiah 9:26 indicts "all the house of Israel" as uncircumcised in heart. **The Timothy/Titus control case** resolves Paul's apparent self-contradiction: only contradictory if circumcision itself is the issue. **Romans 2:28 explicitly restrained** — it must not become a shortcut to erase ethnic Israel while claiming to oppose replacement theology. **The finding: sacrifice changed *form*, priesthood changed *order*, circumcision's *sign* ceased functioning as the Gentile boundary — three different transformations. There is no mechanical formula.** Opens the **`OLAM` bookmark** with an explicit brake against resolving it opportunistically.

[[00p-sacrifice-priesthood-and-fulfillment]] — **the first worked example of what *fulfillment* actually means.** Leviticus 16 read **first, on its own terms** (the binding sequencing constraint honored) — two goats, two movements: blood atones, the live goat **bears sins away**; and the mediator who must atone **for himself**. Isaiah 53 concentrating sacrificial categories into a person *before* any New Testament writer. Then Hebrews: **"shadow," "copy," "pattern" → the model is *pattern → reality*, not *falsehood → truth*.** The posture argument — the Levitical priest **stands** daily; Messiah offers once and **sits down**. Yields the definition that reconciles Matthew 5:18 with Hebrews 7:12 by distinguishing **truth** from **administration**. Three table rows filled with explicit apostolic interpretation, each shaped *reality retained, form transformed*. **§18 applies the brake preemptively** — a model that works in one area is a possibility framework, not an automatic answer elsewhere. Includes the **Azazel side branch — 1 Enoch since VERIFIED, with the semantic fork preserved rather than collapsed**, and three recorded translation/text-critical divergences (Isaiah 53:10's conditional, Matthew 26:28's contested "new," Hebrews 7:24's *aparabaton*).

[[00o-new-covenant-what-changed]] — **the What-Changed investigation, begun.** Starts with the prophets, not with Paul and not with any denomination. Jeremiah 31's recipients (**Israel and Judah**) and its explicit "**not like**" the Exodus covenant — which forces the refinement from "one covenant story" to **"one covenantal story containing multiple covenants, culminating in Messiah."** Torah *internalized*, not erased (*natatti et-torati beqirbam*). Ezekiel 36's Ruach **causing** obedience rather than replacing it — a third function added to this vault's own model of the Spirit. Ezekiel 37's dry bones adding **resurrection language** to the semantic family, with the brake stated in the same breath. Luke 22:20 as the **chronological anchor** — the New Covenant is tied to Yeshua's death. **Two rows of the table honestly marked UNRESOLVED** (Ezekiel 37's sanctuary "forevermore"; Jeremiah 31:34's "all shall know me"), and **Zechariah 6:13 downgraded to suggestive after two of three translations read a *separate* priest.** Establishes the **Category Collapse Test's second face** (imported categories — moral/civil/ceremonial is theological taxonomy, not biblical terminology), the **Eleven-Question Protocol** for Torah commands, and the **companion question of purpose**.

[[00n-torah-fulfilled-and-what-changed]] — **Matthew 5:17–18 as a control on Matthew 24:35.** The **two "untils"** ("until heaven and earth pass away" *and* "until all is accomplished"), with their relationship recorded as undetermined rather than silently chosen. *katalyō* explicitly negated; *plēroō* read against Matthew's own prior usage (1:22, 2:15, 2:17, 4:14) — **neither** "fulfill = terminate" **nor** "fulfill = nothing may change." Melchizedek earns entry via Hebrews 7:11–12 arguing from **Psalm 110**, with a lexical brake on *metathesis*. Jeremiah 31 blocking both reductions at once. Hebrews 8:13 cashed — **with a substantial brake on the pre-AD-70 dating inference, including one counter-consideration flagged as unverified.** Establishes the **Category Collapse Test** and the **six-way distinction** (Torah / Mosaic covenant / Levitical priesthood / Temple / sacrificial administration / moral commandments are *not* interchangeable). Heart-circumcision traced back into Torah itself. **Records an unresolved cost of the position: Matthew 5:19.**

[[00m-fig-tree-generation-heaven-and-earth]] — the fig tree (deflated as a decoder by Luke 21:29's "and all the trees"), "this generation" with four interpretations weighed — **including CJB's "this *people*" as recorded contrary evidence** — and "heaven and earth will pass away" traced through Deuteronomy's covenant witnesses, Isaiah 51, and Isaiah 65–66's complication. Establishes the **Anti-Replacement Principle** and **Audience Continuity**.

[[00k-the-gathering-of-the-elect]] — Matthew 24:31's gathering traced to its Tanakh sources: Deuteronomy 30's "uttermost parts of heaven," Isaiah 11 and 43's four-direction gathering, Zechariah 2:6's "four winds" verbatim, and **Isaiah 27:13's great trumpet** announcing the return of the scattered. **Includes the *angeloi* concession** — the lexically-available "human messengers" reading is set aside because Matthew's own "his angels" formula (13:41, 16:27, 25:31) points the other way, even though the rejected reading would have been more convenient. Names the hardest test the model faces: reconciling Isaiah 27, Matthew 24, 1 Corinthians 15, 1 Thessalonians 4, and Revelation 20 without protective exceptions.

[[00j-cosmic-signs-and-intertextual-priority]] — Matthew 24:29's darkened sun, moon, and falling stars, read against the prophets who used the same language for the fall of **Babylon** (Isaiah 13, with the Medes named as the mechanism), **Edom** (Isaiah 34, the source of Revelation 6's rolled-up sky), and **Egypt** (Ezekiel 32) — plus Joel, which Peter applied to his own generation at Pentecost. Establishes the formal rule of **Intertextual Priority**, with its limiting clause. Includes the Ezekiel 11:23 / Mount of Olives hypothesis at "supports."

[[00h-coming-on-the-clouds]] — **the major pressure point.** Daniel 7's Son of Man comes *to the Ancient of Days*, not down to earth. Cloud vocabulary assembled from Exodus (presence), Psalm 18 (deliverance), Isaiah 19 (judgment on Egypt — the load-bearing control), Nahum. Yeshua's use of Daniel 7 + Psalm 110 before the high priest, addressed to His own judges. Revelation 1:7 and Zechariah 12:10. Establishes that "cloud coming can only mean bodily descent" is a claim the Tanakh does not permit — without thereby proving preterism.

[[00i-stars-the-dragon-and-the-cross]] — stars as heavenly beings (Revelation 1:20's explicit key), the third of the stars in Revelation 12 classified as strong inference rather than stated text, the canonical cluster tying Satan's defeat to the cross (John 12, Colossians 2, Hebrews 2, 1 John 3, Revelation 12:11), the torn veil interpreted by Hebrews itself, and Melchizedek as king-priest. Includes the Isaiah 14 caution: its stated referent is Babylon's king, and Satanic application is later typology.

[[00g-matthew-24-and-the-oikoumene]] — **the bridge back into preterism.** Matthew 24:14's "whole world" is *oikoumenē* (inhabited world) — the same word Luke uses for Caesar's census and a first-century famine. Paul's own universal-proclamation language (Colossians 1:5–6, 1:23, Romans 10:18, 16:25–26) and his description of his regional mission as "fulfilled" (Romans 15:19, 23). "This generation" tested against Matthew's own usage. Matthew 23 running directly into Matthew 24. AD 70 as an unavoidable referent. Three hypotheses held open, including the postmillennial/"little season" possibility marked as under-supported rather than suppressed.

[[00f-witness-synthesis]] — **the question that started this whole investigation, answered.** Matthew 28, Mark 16 (with its manuscript problem stated), Acts 1:8, Acts 8:4, Ephesians 4:11, 2 Timothy 4:5, 1 Peter 3:15, Colossians 4:5–6, and 1 Corinthians 12 read together rather than proof-texted. **Both extremes fail** — "only the apostles proclaimed" is eliminated by Acts 8; "every believer holds an identical evangelistic ministry" is eliminated by Ephesians 4 and 1 Corinthians 12. Includes the point where the method corrected its own author's starting position.

[[00e-olive-tree-and-covenant-identity]] — Paul's *theological* explanation of what Acts narrates historically: Romans 9–11 (Israel not rejected, the remnant, the olive tree, the warning against Gentile arrogance, "all Israel will be saved" left genuinely open) and Ephesians 2 (far off brought near, one new humanity, fellow citizens). Includes the deliberate stress-test of "Messianic Israelite" — the label survives as a description of covenant continuity, but is disciplined by the text rather than the reverse. Tanakh precedent from Ruth and Ezekiel 47:21–23.

[[00d-grace-and-stewardship]] — a separate thread from the same sessions: Yahweh as giver before steward, the Spirit as *arrabōn* (guarantee/pledge, not an investment expecting return), stewardship kept distinct from that pledge (the parable of the talents), Ephesians 2:8–10 holding grace and good works in the right order, plus two flagged-for-later word studies (ruach as wind/breath/spirit, and Psalm 8's Hebrew/Greek textual variant on "angels").

## Governing Principle

Keep three altitudes separate at all times, in every entry:

1. **What the text explicitly says** — a direct quote or paraphrase of Scripture, cited by reference.
2. **What I infer from combining passages** — a synthesis or timeline reading. Still a conviction, but labeled as *my present understanding*, not "the Bible says."
3. **What I speculate beyond the text** — historical theories, connections to extrabiblical claims (Tartaria, mud floods, Rupes Nigra, etc.). These live in Hypotheses or Open Questions, never in Convictions, no matter how strongly held.

Collapsing these three into one undifferentiated list is the failure mode this structure exists to prevent — it's what makes a position defensible ("here's what Scripture says, here's what I conclude, here's what I'm still exploring") instead of brittle.

## Cross-Session Continuity

This is the persistence system — no separate tool needed:

- **These four files are the source of truth.** A new session (this vault, ChatGPT, anywhere) starts by reading them, not by re-deriving from memory.
- **`bd remember`** holds a short pointer to current state (which file was last touched, what tranche is pending) — check `bd memories bible` at the start of a session.
- **SESSION-LOG.md** (vault root) gets an entry each session noting what was merged in.
- When a ChatGPT conversation produces a large synthesis (like the one that seeded this framework), paste it in as a new tranche — it gets sorted into the four files, not kept as a fifth transcript file.
