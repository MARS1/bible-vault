# Eschatology Framework — Index

Personal theological research project: working out a preterist-leaning eschatology (Revelation 20's millennium as already fulfilled, present age as the "little season") and the historical/speculative questions that grew alongside it (ancient architecture, lost civilizations, cosmology).

This is a **multi-session, multi-tranche project**. Content arrives in chunks (originally drafted in dialogue with ChatGPT, refined here). Each tranche gets sorted into one of four files below and merged — never just appended raw.

## Read This First

[[00b-biblical-epistemology]] — how we know what we think we know, before getting into what we actually believe. Defines Biblical Epistemology and Contextual Exegesis, the 7-step test every passage should pass before it counts as an established conviction, and the translation/textual-source policy (CJB → ESV → RVR1960, original languages when wording matters). See ADR-0002 and ADR-0003 (`docs/adr/`).

**Manuscript voice (ADR-0004):** when a session produces real explanatory commentary — the "notice the sequence" kind of insight that would actually survive into the eventual book — it gets written into the relevant file immediately, marked as manuscript voice, not deferred to a later drafting pass. See [[00b-biblical-epistemology]] section A for the first worked example.

[[00c-commission-covenant-investigation]] — the full working-out of the Israel/Gentile commission question: what the Tanakh anticipated before Yeshua's ministry (Genesis 12, Isaiah 42/49), John the Baptist (including the John/Elijah typology tension, held deliberately unresolved between "I am not Elijah" and "he is Elijah"), Yeshua's Israel-focused earthly ministry, the Canaanite/Syrophoenician woman, the Roman centurion, the post-resurrection turn, the Ethiopian eunuch (covenantal status deliberately left undetermined), Cornelius, the Jerusalem council (also read as a live case study of the epistemological cycle operating inside Scripture itself), Paul's explicit Gentile apostleship, the covenant-continuity thread, and — finally — whether/how the commission applies to individual believers today (apostolic mission ≠ spiritual gift ≠ individual charge ≠ readiness to answer ≠ visible conduct, five distinguished categories).

[[00k-the-gathering-of-the-elect]] — Matthew 24:31's gathering traced to its Tanakh sources: Deuteronomy 30's "uttermost parts of heaven," Isaiah 11 and 43's four-direction gathering, Zechariah 2:6's "four winds" verbatim, and **Isaiah 27:13's great trumpet** announcing the return of the scattered. **Includes the *angeloi* concession** — the lexically-available "human messengers" reading is set aside because Matthew's own "his angels" formula (13:41, 16:27, 25:31) points the other way, even though the rejected reading would have been more convenient. Names the hardest test the model faces: reconciling Isaiah 27, Matthew 24, 1 Corinthians 15, 1 Thessalonians 4, and Revelation 20 without protective exceptions.

[[00j-cosmic-signs-and-intertextual-priority]] — Matthew 24:29's darkened sun, moon, and falling stars, read against the prophets who used the same language for the fall of **Babylon** (Isaiah 13, with the Medes named as the mechanism), **Edom** (Isaiah 34, the source of Revelation 6's rolled-up sky), and **Egypt** (Ezekiel 32) — plus Joel, which Peter applied to his own generation at Pentecost. Establishes the formal rule of **Intertextual Priority**, with its limiting clause. Includes the Ezekiel 11:23 / Mount of Olives hypothesis at "supports."

[[00h-coming-on-the-clouds]] — **the major pressure point.** Daniel 7's Son of Man comes *to the Ancient of Days*, not down to earth. Cloud vocabulary assembled from Exodus (presence), Psalm 18 (deliverance), Isaiah 19 (judgment on Egypt — the load-bearing control), Nahum. Yeshua's use of Daniel 7 + Psalm 110 before the high priest, addressed to His own judges. Revelation 1:7 and Zechariah 12:10. Establishes that "cloud coming can only mean bodily descent" is a claim the Tanakh does not permit — without thereby proving preterism.

[[00i-stars-the-dragon-and-the-cross]] — stars as heavenly beings (Revelation 1:20's explicit key), the third of the stars in Revelation 12 classified as strong inference rather than stated text, the canonical cluster tying Satan's defeat to the cross (John 12, Colossians 2, Hebrews 2, 1 John 3, Revelation 12:11), the torn veil interpreted by Hebrews itself, and Melchizedek as king-priest. Includes the Isaiah 14 caution: its stated referent is Babylon's king, and Satanic application is later typology.

[[00g-matthew-24-and-the-oikoumene]] — **the bridge back into preterism.** Matthew 24:14's "whole world" is *oikoumenē* (inhabited world) — the same word Luke uses for Caesar's census and a first-century famine. Paul's own universal-proclamation language (Colossians 1:5–6, 1:23, Romans 10:18, 16:25–26) and his description of his regional mission as "fulfilled" (Romans 15:19, 23). "This generation" tested against Matthew's own usage. Matthew 23 running directly into Matthew 24. AD 70 as an unavoidable referent. Three hypotheses held open, including the postmillennial/"little season" possibility marked as under-supported rather than suppressed.

[[00f-witness-synthesis]] — **the question that started this whole investigation, answered.** Matthew 28, Mark 16 (with its manuscript problem stated), Acts 1:8, Acts 8:4, Ephesians 4:11, 2 Timothy 4:5, 1 Peter 3:15, Colossians 4:5–6, and 1 Corinthians 12 read together rather than proof-texted. **Both extremes fail** — "only the apostles proclaimed" is eliminated by Acts 8; "every believer holds an identical evangelistic ministry" is eliminated by Ephesians 4 and 1 Corinthians 12. Includes the point where the method corrected its own author's starting position.

[[00e-olive-tree-and-covenant-identity]] — Paul's *theological* explanation of what Acts narrates historically: Romans 9–11 (Israel not rejected, the remnant, the olive tree, the warning against Gentile arrogance, "all Israel will be saved" left genuinely open) and Ephesians 2 (far off brought near, one new humanity, fellow citizens). Includes the deliberate stress-test of "Messianic Israelite" — the label survives as a description of covenant continuity, but is disciplined by the text rather than the reverse. Tanakh precedent from Ruth and Ezekiel 47:21–23.

[[00d-grace-and-stewardship]] — a separate thread from the same sessions: Yahweh as giver before steward, the Spirit as *arrabōn* (guarantee/pledge, not an investment expecting return), stewardship kept distinct from that pledge (the parable of the talents), Ephesians 2:8–10 holding grace and good works in the right order, plus two flagged-for-later word studies (ruach as wind/breath/spirit, and Psalm 8's Hebrew/Greek textual variant on "angels").

## Governing Principle

Keep three altitudes separate at all times, in every entry:

1. **What the text explicitly says** — a direct quote or paraphrase of Scripture, cited by reference.
2. **What I infer from combining passages** — a synthesis or timeline reading. Still a conviction, but labeled as *my present understanding*, not "the Bible says."
3. **What I speculate beyond the text** — historical theories, connections to extrabiblical claims (Tartaria, mud floods, Rupes Nigra, etc.). These live in Hypotheses or Open Questions, never in Convictions, no matter how strongly held.

Collapsing these three into one undifferentiated list is the failure mode this structure exists to prevent — it's what makes a position defensible ("here's what Scripture says, here's what I conclude, here's what I'm still exploring") instead of brittle.

## Cross-Session Continuity

This is the persistence system — no separate tool needed:

- **These four files are the source of truth.** A new session (this vault, ChatGPT, anywhere) starts by reading them, not by re-deriving from memory.
- **`bd remember`** holds a short pointer to current state (which file was last touched, what tranche is pending) — check `bd memories bible` at the start of a session.
