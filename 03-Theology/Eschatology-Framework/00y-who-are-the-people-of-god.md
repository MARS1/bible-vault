# Stage 21 — Who Are "the People of God"?

> **Hebrews 4:9: a *sabbatismos* remains for τῷ λαῷ τοῦ θεοῦ — "the people of God."**
>
> ### **Four substitutions are available, and the methodology permits none of them until the text earns it:** ❌ *"that means Christians"* · ❌ *"that means Israel"* · ❌ *"that means the Church replacing Israel"* · ❌ *"that means ethnic Israelites and nobody else."*

**So we begin much farther back.**

---

## 1. "People of God" Begins as Unmistakably Israelite Covenant Language

> **Exodus 6:7** — ESV: "**I will take you to be my people, and I will be your God**, and you shall know that I am the LORD your God." CJB: "I will take you as my people, and I will be your God."

> **Leviticus 26:12** — ESV: "And I will walk among you and **will be your God, and you shall be my people**."

**Jeremiah, Ezekiel, and Zechariah repeat it. It becomes one of Scripture's great covenant formulas:**

> ### **I will be their God, and they shall be My people.**

> ### **So we do not get to begin with "people of God means anybody religious." It has covenantal content, and its primary biblical referent is ISRAEL. That is the starting point.**

---

## 2. But Israel's Election Was Never an End in Itself

> **Genesis 12:3** — ESV: "I will bless those who bless you… and **in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed**." CJB: "**by you all the families of the earth will be blessed**."

**Two truths are present from very early:**

| **Particularity** | Yahweh chooses Abraham → Isaac → Jacob → Israel |
| **Universality** | **through that chosen line, the nations are to be blessed** |

> ### **These are not competing ideas. The particular election is the MEANS through which blessing extends outward.**
>
> **We already watched this pattern in Acts ([[00c-commission-covenant-investigation]]). Now we are finding its roots.**

---

## 3. Torah Itself Already Permits the Foreigner to Join

**Otherwise someone could claim Gentile inclusion was a Pauline invention. It was not.**

> **Exodus 12:48** *(indexed)* — the foreigner who keeps Passover, with his males circumcised, "**shall be as a native of the land**."

> **Exodus 12:49** — ESV: "**There shall be one law for the native and for the stranger who sojourns among you.**" CJB: "**The same teaching is to apply equally to the citizen and to the foreigner living among you.**"

> ### **Even under Torah: genealogy and covenant membership are RELATED BUT NOT IDENTICAL CATEGORIES.**
>
> **Someone not born ethnically Israelite could be incorporated into Israel's covenantal community.** *(Sixth appearance of the *ger* thread — Exodus 12:48/49 · Leviticus 17–18 · Numbers 15:15–16 · Exodus 20:10 · Isaiah 56:6–7 · here.)*

---

## 4. Rahab and Ruth Make It Concrete

**Rahab: a Canaanite of Jericho. Ruth: a Moabite.**

> **Ruth 1:16** *(indexed)* — "**Your people shall be my people, and your God my God.**"

**Note the sequence: not merely *"I personally believe your theology"* but ***your people → my people*** *and* ***your God → my God***.** **And Ruth enters the lineage to David, and thence to Messiah.**

> ### **So the biblical story itself refuses the absolute equation *Israel = biological ancestry and nothing else*.**
>
> ### ⚖️ **And the opposite brake, immediately: it does NOT follow that biological Israel becomes irrelevant. Both errors are available.** *(And Ruth is still called "the Moabite" afterward — [[00q-circumcision-covenant-sign-and-heart]] §29. **Incorporation without erasure of origin.**)*

---

## 5. 🔴 Isaiah 19 — Yahweh Calls EGYPT "My People"

> **Isaiah 56:3** *(indexed)* — the foreigner who joins himself to Yahweh must not say "**the LORD will surely separate me from his people**." **Almost answering our question directly.**

**Then Isaiah 19 goes further than anything yet encountered:**

> **Isaiah 19:24** — ESV: "In that day **Israel will be the third with Egypt and Assyria**, a blessing in the midst of the earth." CJB: "Isra'el will be **a third partner with Egypt and Ashur**."

> **Isaiah 19:25** — ESV: "**Blessed be Egypt my people, and Assyria the work of my hands, and Israel my inheritance.**" CJB: "**Blessed be Egypt my people, Ashur the work of my hands and Isra'el my heritage.**"

> ### **Yahweh applies "MY PEOPLE" to EGYPT. Historic oppressor of Israel.**
>
> ### ⚖️ **And note what does NOT happen: Israel does not disappear. Israel remains distinctly "my inheritance."**
>
> ### **This is prophetic evidence that Yahweh's people-category can EXPAND to encompass nations WITHOUT ERASING Israel. A major control text.**

---

## 6. Hosea — "Not My People" → "My People"

> **Hosea 1:9** — ESV: "Call his name **Not My People**, for you are not my people." CJB: "Name him **Lo-'Ammi** [not-my-people]."

> **Hosea 1:10** — ESV: "**And in the place where it was said to them, 'You are not my people,' it shall be said to them, 'Children of the living God.'**"

> **Hosea 2:23** — ESV: "**I will say to Not My People, 'You are my people'; and he shall say, 'You are my God.'**"

> ### ❗ **Contextually, Hosea is speaking about ISRAEL. That must not be skipped.**

> **📌 FIFTH CONFIRMED VERSIFICATION OFFSET: CJB numbers ESV's Hosea 1:10 as 2:1, and ESV's 2:23 as 2:21.** *(Direction confirmed; CJB text of 2:23 was not retrieved in this pass — flagged rather than approximated.)* Prior offsets: Jeremiah 31 · Zechariah 2:6 · Psalms superscription · Hosea 2:11→2:13.

**Then something remarkable: BOTH Paul and Peter reuse Hosea's language — and not identically.**

---

## 7. Peter Applies Israel's Covenant Vocabulary

> **1 Peter 2:9** — ESV: "But you are **a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for his own possession**." CJB: "a chosen people, **the King's *cohanim***, a holy nation, a people for God to possess!"

**That language is Exodus 19 — spoken at Sinai, to ISRAEL: *kingdom of priests, holy nation*.**

> **1 Peter 2:10** — ESV: "**Once you were not a people, but now you are God's people**; once you had not received mercy, but now you have received mercy." **That is Hosea.**

### ⚠️ But who is Peter's audience? Genuinely contested

**Peter addresses "elect exiles of the Dispersion" across Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, Bithynia. "Dispersion" can sound explicitly Jewish.** **Yet:**

> **1 Peter 4:3** — ESV: "the time that is past suffices for **doing what the Gentiles want to do**, living in sensuality… **and lawless idolatry**." CJB: "living the way **the pagans** want you to live… **forbidden idol-worship**."

**That sounds Gentile.** **The composition and rhetorical identity of Peter's audience is genuinely debated, and this project does not resolve it casually.**

> ### **The undeniable observation: Peter applies ISRAEL'S COVENANT VOCABULARY to Messiah-followers living among the nations.** **How ethnically mixed that audience was remains a separate historical question.**

---

## 8. Paul Does Something Similar in Romans 9

> **Romans 9:24** — ESV: "even us whom he has called, **not from the Jews only but also from the Gentiles**?"

> **Romans 9:25–26** — ESV: "'**Those who were not my people I will call "my people"**'… 'there they will be called **sons of the living God**.'"

**That is Hosea — whose original historical context concerned ISRAEL — deployed inside an argument explicitly involving JEWS + GENTILES.**

> ### **This is precisely an intertextual question. And the answer is NOT "because Paul doesn't care about context."**
>
> **HYPOTHESIS (Tier B):** Paul may understand **the restoration of Israel and the inclusion of the nations as CONVERGING within Messiah.** **That needs Romans 9–11 as a whole — which is the next stage.**

---

## 9. The Olive Tree

> **Romans 11:17** — ESV: "But if some of the branches were broken off, and you, **although a wild olive shoot, were grafted in among the others** and now **share in the nourishing root** of the olive tree." CJB: "you — a wild olive — were grafted in among them and have become **equal sharers in the rich root**."

> ### **Notice what Paul does NOT describe: Yahweh did not chop down Israel's tree and plant a Gentile tree called Christianity.**
> ### **The wild branches are grafted into an EXISTING tree. That is a massive distinction.**

> **Romans 11:18** — ESV: "**do not be arrogant toward the branches**. If you are, remember **it is not you who support the root, but the root that supports you**." CJB: "don't boast as if you were better than the branches!"

> ### **Almost devastating to triumphalist replacement theology. Gentiles are not depicted as replacing the ancient root — they are BENEFICIARIES of it.**

**And Paul says natural branches can be grafted in *again*. So biological Israel remains within his concern.**

---

## 10. A Better Category Than "Replacement"

**Theological labels smuggle conclusions. "Replacement theology," crudely: *Israel failed → Church replaced Israel*.**

**Romans 11's imagery is different:** **continuity + pruning + grafting + restoration.** **One tree · natural branches · wild branches · branches removed · branches grafted · natural branches capable of restoration · root remains.**

> ### **So avoid ❌ "Church replaces Israel." But ALSO avoid prematurely asserting ❌ "Church simply IS Israel" without qualification.**
> ### **Paul supplies a more precise metaphor: GRAFTING. Use his vocabulary before inventing ours.**

---

## 11. Ephesians 2 — the Architectural Version

> **Ephesians 2:12** *(indexed)* — Gentiles were "**alienated from the commonwealth of Israel**" and "**strangers to the covenants of promise**."

> ### **Their original problem was not "you weren't Christians." They were alienated from ISRAEL and from THE COVENANTS.**

> **Ephesians 2:13** — ESV: "But now in Christ Jesus **you who once were far off have been brought near** by the blood of Christ."

**far → near. NOT: Israel abolished → unrelated entity created.**

> **Ephesians 2:14** — ESV: "**he himself is our peace, who has made us both one** and has broken down in his flesh **the dividing wall of hostility**." CJB: "he has made us both one and has broken down the ***m'chitzah*** which divided us."

> **Ephesians 2:19** *(indexed)* — "**no longer strangers and aliens, but… fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God**."

**Incorporation language throughout: strangers → citizens · aliens → household · far → near · two → one.**

> ### **Direction is not "Israel becomes irrelevant." It is "those formerly outside are brought inside through Messiah." Matches Romans 11 extraordinarily well.**

---

## 12. Cornelius, Revisited

**Acts 10:28 — "God has shown me that I should not call any *person* common or unclean." The Ruach falls. Acts 11:18 — "to the Gentiles also God has granted repentance that leads to life."**

**At the time we refused to leap from that into a developed covenant theology** ([[00s-clean-and-unclean-from-noah-to-messiah]] §20). **Now Romans and Ephesians supply the theology Acts had not yet articulated:** Gentiles are not merely *permitted to stand near Israel* — they become **grafted branches · fellow citizens · members of the household · sharers in covenantal blessing, through Messiah.**

> ### **This is why following Acts sequentially BEFORE Paul was worth the effort. We watched the historical event before letting later theology explain its significance.**

---

## 13. 🔴 A MAJOR TRANSLATION DIVERGENCE — Ephesians 2:15

**This is the sharpest finding in the stage, and it lands directly on the continuity/discontinuity fault line.**

| Version | Ephesians 2:15 |
|---|---|
| **ESV** | "**by abolishing the law of commandments expressed in ordinances**, that he might create in himself one new man in place of the two" |
| **CJB** | "**by destroying in his own body the ENMITY OCCASIONED BY the *Torah*, with its commands set forth in the form of ordinances.** He did this in order to create… **a single new humanity**" |

> ### **ESV: Messiah abolishes THE LAW.**
> ### **CJB: Messiah destroys THE ENMITY OCCASIONED BY the Torah.**
>
> ### **Those are not the same claim. One removes the law; the other removes the hostility attached to it — and v.14 has just named "the dividing wall of HOSTILITY" as the thing broken down.**

> **⚖️ Interest noted in both directions and settling nothing:** CJB is a Messianic Jewish translation with a stake in the narrower reading; ESV is a traditional-Christian translation with a stake in the broader one. **Neither observation settles the Greek.**
>
> **What CAN be said now:** **the immediate context (v.14's *dividing wall of hostility*, v.16's reconciliation "killing the hostility") makes hostility the paragraph's recurring object — which is a contextual datum favoring the narrower reading, though not a lexical demonstration.**
>
> ### **Requires Greek work per ADR-0003 and the Lexical Escalation rule. Filed to [[04-open-questions]].** *(Same category as Mark 7:19's parenthesis, Hebrews 4:9's* sabbatismos, *and Colossians 2:14's* cheirographon *— the fourth passage where an English rendering has been quoted to this project as though it were the verse.)*

---

## 14. ❗ The Brake — Romans 11:25–29

> **Romans 11:25** — ESV: "**Lest you be wise in your own sight**… a partial hardening has come upon Israel, **until the fullness of the Gentiles has come in**."

> **Romans 11:26** — ESV: "**And in this way all Israel will be saved.**"

**Greek: πᾶς Ἰσραήλ — *pas Israēl*. One of the most disputed phrases in Romans. Candidates:** ethnic/national Israel · the elect within Israel · the total covenant people including Jews and Gentiles · a future restoration of Jewish Israel · variations. **NOT settled by theological instinct.** *(Already open as [[04-open-questions]] item 14.)*

**But 28–29 absolutely prevent one conclusion:**

> **Romans 11:28** — ESV: "As regards election, **they are beloved for the sake of their forefathers**."
> **Romans 11:29** — ESV: "**For the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable.**" CJB: "**God's free gifts and his calling are irrevocable.**"

> ### **A very strong brake against simplistic replacement. Ethnic Israel did not cease to matter to Paul.**

---

## 15. Jeremiah 31 Becomes Indispensable

**What is the New Covenant actually called?**

> **Jeremiah 31:31** *(indexed)* — "I will make a new covenant **with the house of Israel and the house of Judah**."

**Not "with an unrelated Gentile institution." Yet Gentiles unquestionably participate in New Covenant blessing.**

> ### **So something must explain the relationship — and Romans 11 / Ephesians 2 supply a textually grounded model:**
> ### **Gentiles are brought into covenantal blessing THROUGH ISRAEL'S MESSIAH, rather than receiving an unrelated covenant detached from Israel's promises.**
>
> **Which means the binary "Old covenant = Jews; New Covenant = Christians" is increasingly inadequate.**

---

## 16. And Abraham Becomes More Important Than Moses Here

> **Galatians 3:8** *(indexed)* — Scripture "**preached the gospel beforehand to Abraham**" — *"in you shall all the nations be blessed."* **Paul calls Genesis 12's promise *gospel beforehand*.**

> **Galatians 3:29** *(indexed)* — "**if you are Christ's, then you are Abraham's offspring, heirs according to promise**."

> ### 🚧 **Precision: Paul does NOT say Gentiles biologically become descendants of Jacob.** He is speaking of **inheritance in the Abrahamic promise through Messiah.**

> ### **Consequence: Gentile inclusion is NOT a Plan B caused by Israel's failure. It was embedded in the Abrahamic promise from the beginning.**

---

## 17. "Messianic Israelite," Revisited a Third Time

**The term was adopted, then disciplined twice** ([[00e-olive-tree-and-covenant-identity]]; [[01-convictions]] B.5). **The evidence now suggests continuing to resist a premature identity label.**

> ### **The strongest available vocabulary is Paul's own:**
> **Abraham's offspring according to promise · grafted into the olive tree · brought near · fellow citizens · members of Yahweh's household · one new humanity in Messiah · people of God.**
>
> ### **These have the enormous advantage of being *Paul's phrases*. We do not need to improve them yet.**

---

## 18. Back to Hebrews 4:9

> **"There remains a Sabbath-rest for the people of God."**

> ### **After the above, the phrase cannot responsibly be read as obviously meaning *ethnic Israel exclusively*. Nor may Israel be erased from it and *Gentile Christianity* written in.**

> ### **STRONGEST CURRENT SYNTHESIS (Tier B):**
> ### **Yahweh's people remain rooted in the covenant story of Israel, centered in Israel's Messiah, with believing Gentiles incorporated into that people through Messiah — rather than establishing a rival people alongside or in place of Israel.**

**Note the wording carefully. It preserves Israel's priority · Gentile inclusion · Messiah as the means · one people · continuity — *without pretending every identity question is solved*.**

---

## 19. And This Answers the Original Question From an Unexpected Direction

**The investigation began asking where Scripture establishes the mission to the nations, rather than assuming every commission was originally universal.**

**Here is the traced trajectory:**

**promise to Abraham — nations blessed** → **Israel chosen** → **foreigners may join Yahweh** → **prophets anticipate the nations** → **Messiah comes first to Israel** → **Israelite apostles proclaim Messiah** → **Samaritans receive** → **Ethiopian eunuch receives** → **Cornelius receives the Ruach** → **Jerusalem recognizes Gentile repentance** → **Acts 15 refuses circumcision as the entry requirement** → **Paul explains grafting** → **Gentiles brought near** → **one covenant people around Israel's Messiah.**

> ### **Vastly stronger than quoting "Go make disciples of all nations" — not because that verse is unimportant, but because the whole covenantal trajectory underneath it has now been traced.**

## 20. ⚖️ And the Counterevidence, Because an Elegant Model Is Exactly When to Look Hardest

**Still genuinely open:**

- **If Jew and Gentile become "one," why does Paul continue distinguishing them?** *(Acts 21:25 — [[00u-acts-21-paul-torah-and-the-two-groups]] §10.)*
- **If Gentiles are grafted into Israel's covenantal tree, which Torah obligations follow?**
- **Why does Acts 15 distinguish requirements for Gentiles?**
- **What is "the Israel of God"?** — **Galatians 6:16**, ESV: "**peace and mercy be upon them, and upon the Israel of God**." CJB: "**and upon the Isra'el of God!**" *(Does *kai* here mean "and also upon" a distinct group, or "namely"? Unresolved.)*
- **What does "all Israel will be saved" mean?**
- **Does Revelation distinguish Israel's tribes from the nations even in its final imagery?**

> **Revelation 21:12–14** — ESV: "**twelve gates**… on the gates **the names of the twelve tribes of the sons of Israel** were inscribed… And the wall of the city had **twelve foundations**, and on them **the twelve names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb**."

> ### **What does that architecture itself communicate about covenant continuity — twelve tribes as gates, twelve apostles as foundations, in one city?**
>
> ### **These remain open. We do not erase them because the current model is elegant. An elegant model is precisely when to look hardest for evidence capable of breaking it.**

## 21. 🆕 Two Formal Rules

### **Expansion Does Not Require Erasure**

> **When Scripture extends a covenantal title, promise, blessing, or identity beyond its earlier recipients, do not assume the original recipient has thereby been displaced.**
> **Test whether the later text depicts REPLACEMENT, INCORPORATION, EXPANSION, FULFILLMENT, GRAFTING, or some other relationship.**
> ### **Prefer the relationship explicitly supplied by the text over a theological label imposed upon it.**

**Worked: Isaiah 19:25 calls Egypt "my people" *while Israel remains "my inheritance."* Romans 11 grafts wild branches into an existing tree *whose root supports them.***

### **Later Inclusion Must Not Rewrite Earlier Context**

> **The fact that Scripture later extends a promise or mission does not authorize retroactively pretending an earlier passage originally addressed a broader audience than its historical context supports.**
> ### **Preserve the original audience first; then trace the textual bridge by which Scripture itself expands the scope.**

> ### **This second rule is exactly what started this entire project.**

**Both added to [[00b-biblical-epistemology]] and [[00-methodology-current]].**

## ✍️ Manuscript Voice — Context Made the Gospel Larger, Not Smaller

> **Prose:** The investigation had begun with a suspicion about context. I did not want to take instructions given to Israelite apostles and quietly universalize them merely because later Christian tradition had taught me to read them that way. If Yeshua said He was sent to the lost sheep of Israel, then Israel had to be allowed to remain in the sentence. If John baptized Israelites, that mattered. If the apostles initially proclaimed Messiah within Israel, that mattered too. **Context could not be sacrificed simply because I already believed the nations were included.**
>
> But something unexpected happened as the context widened. **The case for the nations became stronger rather than weaker.** Abraham had been promised that all families of the earth would be blessed through him. Torah already knew the foreigner who joined himself to Yahweh. **Isaiah could astonishingly call Egypt "My people."** Yeshua came to Israel, yet Gentiles appeared at the edges of the Gospel narratives. Acts then showed the boundary opening in real time — Samaria, the Ethiopian, Cornelius — until Paul finally supplied the theological vocabulary: **wild branches grafted into an ancient tree, strangers brought near, foreigners becoming fellow citizens.**
>
> I had worried that contextual rigor might trap the good news inside Israel. **Instead, Israel turned out to be the very historical and covenantal path through which the blessing reached the nations.** The gospel did not become universal by ceasing to be Israel's story. It became universal through Israel's Messiah, exactly as the promise to Abraham had anticipated. **Context had not made the gospel smaller. It had shown me why it could become so large.**

## 22. Next — Romans 9, in Sequence

> ### **Romans 9–11 is ONE argument, and it is routinely pulled apart to prove opposite systems.**

**The dangerous slogans that live there:** *"Not all who are descended from Israel belong to Israel"* · *"Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated"* · *"He has mercy on whomever he wills"* · *"vessels of wrath"* · *"wild olive branches"* · *"until the fullness of the Gentiles"* · ***"all Israel will be saved."***

**The question that governs everything downstream:**

> **Romans 9:6** — ESV: "**For not all who are descended from Israel belong to Israel.**" CJB: "**For not everyone from Isra'el is truly part of Isra'el.**"

> ### **Does Paul there redefine Israel as "the Church"? Or is he making a distinction WITHIN Israel concerning promise, election, and faithful remnant?**
>
> ### **If we get that wrong, everything downstream is contaminated — including Romans 11.**

**Method: Romans 9 FIRST, in sequence, WITHOUT letting Romans 11's conclusion predetermine what Romans 9 is allowed to say.** **Then see whether Paul's own argument carries us from Israel → remnant → Gentiles → grafting → all Israel.**

**If it does, the first enormous arc of this investigation may be approaching its close.**
