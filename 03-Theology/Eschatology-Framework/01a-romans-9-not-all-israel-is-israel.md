# Stage 22 — Romans 9: "Not All Who Are From Israel Are Israel"

> ### **METHOD LOCKED (from Stage 21½): we do NOT read Romans 9 backward through Romans 11, through Calvinism, through replacement theology, through dispensationalism, or through our own developing model. Paul's argument develops on its own.**

**The famous sentence at 9:6 is routinely extracted from an argument that begins five verses earlier. Those verses matter enormously.**

---

## 1. Paul Begins With Anguish — Not Rejection

> **Romans 9:2–3** — ESV: "I have **great sorrow and unceasing anguish** in my heart. For I could wish that **I myself were accursed and cut off from Christ** for the sake of my brothers, **my kinsmen according to the flesh**." CJB: "my grief is so great, the pain in my heart so constant… **my brothers, my own flesh and blood**."

**Greek: κατὰ σάρκα — *kata sarka*, according to the flesh. Explicitly PHYSICAL KINSHIP.**

> ### **So before verse 6 arrives, we already have a control: Paul has not forgotten biological Israel. His entire argument is GENERATED BY ANGUISH OVER BIOLOGICAL ISRAEL.**
>
> ### **That is a problem for the reading "physical Israel doesn't matter anymore because the Church is the real Israel" — a problem that exists *before* we reach the verse used to support it.**

---

## 2. And He Calls Them Israelites — Present Tense

> **Romans 9:4** — ESV: "**They ARE Israelites**, and to them belong the adoption, the glory, the covenants, the giving of the law, the worship, and the promises." CJB: "**the people of Isra'el!**"

**Greek: Ἰσραηλῖται — *Israēlitai*.** **Not "they used to be." Not "until Messiah arrived."**

> ### **Paul writes AFTER the death, resurrection, ascension, Pentecost, and Gentile inclusion — and calls his UNBELIEVING ethnic kinsmen *Israelites*.**
>
> ### **Whatever covenantal change Messiah brought, Paul has not reassigned the word so that unbelieving Jews can no longer hold it. A brake on one overreading of 9:6, established from 9:4.**

---

## 3. The Inventory — and a Plural That Matters

> **Romans 9:4–5** — ESV: "to them belong **the adoption, the glory, the covenants, the giving of the law, the worship, and the promises**. To them belong **the patriarchs**, and **from their race, according to the flesh, is the Christ**."

**Paul does not speak contemptuously of Israel's covenantal history. He piles honour on honour — and Messiah Himself comes *kata sarka* from them.**

> ### 🔍 **Note the plural: "the covenants" — Greek διαθῆκαι *diathēkai*.**
>
> **Paul's own plural supports the Stage 15 refinement** ([[00r-what-is-a-covenant]]): **one covenantal story containing multiple covenants** — Abrahamic · Sinaitic/Mosaic · Davidic · the New promised through Jeremiah — **not "Old Covenant → failed → New Covenant."** *(Independent confirmation of a refinement reached seven stages earlier from a different direction.)*

---

## 4. Only NOW Does the Problem Appear

**If Israel possesses all of that, and Messiah came from Israel, and yet many Israelites do not receive Him — a devastating question follows:**

> ### **Has Yahweh's word failed?**

> **Romans 9:6** — ESV: "**But it is not as though the word of God has failed.** For not all who are descended from Israel belong to Israel."

> ### ❗ **Paul's immediate problem is NOT "who is the Church?" It is: HOW CAN YAHWEH'S PROMISES TO ISRAEL REMAIN TRUSTWORTHY WHEN SO MANY ISRAELITES STAND OUTSIDE THEIR MESSIANIC FULFILMENT?**
>
> ### **9:6 is an ANSWER TO THE APPARENT FAILURE OF YAHWEH'S PROMISE. Keep that context locked.**

---

## 5. The Greek Makes the Wordplay Visible

> **οὐ γὰρ πάντες οἱ ἐξ Ἰσραὴλ οὗτοι Ἰσραήλ**
> **Very literally: "For not all those FROM Israel — these are Israel."**

**CJB: "For not everyone from Isra'el is truly part of Isra'el."**

**Paul uses *Israel* TWICE. Deliberately.**

| **Israel₁** | those who are **from** Israel |
| **Israel₂** | those who constitute "Israel" in the sense relevant to Yahweh's promise |

> ### **The question is: what distinguishes Israel₂ from Israel₁?**
> ### **And this is exactly where readers jump too quickly.**

---

## 6. Paul Answers With Isaac — Not Gentile Christians

> **Romans 9:7** — ESV: "and not all are children of Abraham because they are his offspring, but '**Through Isaac shall your offspring be named**.'"

> **Romans 9:8** — ESV: "**it is not the children of the flesh who are the children of God, but the children of the promise** are counted as offspring." CJB: "not the physical children… but the children the promise refers to."

> ### **Who is being contrasted? ISHMAEL AND ISAAC. Both biologically Abraham's.**

**Verse 8 sounds like something that could include Gentiles — and elsewhere Paul does connect Gentiles to Abrahamic promise (Galatians 3:29).** **But HERE his first example is not Jew vs Gentile. It is Ishmael vs Isaac.**

> ### **Paul's first demonstration: BIOLOGICAL DESCENT FROM ABRAHAM HAS NEVER, BY ITSELF, DETERMINED THE LINE THROUGH WHICH THE COVENANT PROMISE PROCEEDS.**
>
> ### **That is considerably more precise than "Israel now means the Church" — which Paul has not said.**

---

## 7. Then He Closes the Escape Route

**Objection available: Ishmael and Isaac had different mothers — Hagar and Sarah. Perhaps *that* is the distinction.**

> **Romans 9:10** — ESV: "when Rebekah had conceived children **by one man, our forefather Isaac**." CJB: "**both her children were conceived in a single act** with Yitz'chak."

**Same father. Same mother. Twins.**

> **Romans 9:11** — ESV: "**though they were not yet born and had done nothing either good or bad** — in order that God's purpose of election might continue, not because of works but because of him who calls."

> **Romans 9:12** — "**The older will serve the younger.**"

**The argument has narrowed dramatically: Abraham → Isaac not Ishmael → Jacob not Esau → Israel.**

> ### **So 9:6 initially concerns ELECTION WITHIN GENEALOGICAL DESCENT. Not the replacement of genealogical descent.**

---

## 8. ⚠️ Malachi — Intertextual Priority Becomes Crucial

> **Romans 9:13** — ESV: "**Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated.**"

**Reading Romans 9 alone, a modern reader may imagine Yahweh arbitrarily loving one unborn individual and emotionally hating another.**

**But Paul is quoting Malachi 1:2–3 — written long after Jacob and Esau died:**

> **Malachi 1:2–3** — ESV: "'I have loved you,' says the LORD. But you say, 'How have you loved us?' '**Is not Esau Jacob's brother?**' declares the LORD. 'Yet I have loved Jacob but Esau I have hated.'"

**And Malachi explicitly connects them with their PEOPLES — Esau corresponds to EDOM; the surrounding verses speak of Esau's hill country and the people with whom Yahweh is angry.**

> ### **So Paul's quotation carries a CORPORATE/NATIONAL HISTORICAL context, not merely an individual psychological one.**
>
> ### ⚖️ **This does NOT prove individual election is irrelevant to Romans 9. It prevents pretending Malachi's original context does not exist.**
>
> **Exactly why the Intertextual Priority rule was built.**

---

## 9. And Genesis 25 Says "Two Nations"

> **Genesis 25:23** — ESV: "**Two nations are in your womb, and two peoples from within you shall be divided**; the one shall be stronger than the other, **the older shall serve the younger**." CJB: "There are two nations in your womb. From birth they will be **two rival peoples**."

**Jacob becomes Israel. Esau becomes Edom.**

> ### **So Paul's examples simultaneously involve INDIVIDUAL PERSONS and CORPORATE COVENANTAL LINES.**
>
> **Is Romans 9 about individuals? Yes — Paul names them. Is it about peoples? Yes — the source texts explicitly say *nations*.**
>
> ### **Therefore INDIVIDUAL vs CORPORATE ELECTION may itself be a FALSE EITHER/OR.** *(Another binary failing — the pattern named at [[00z-recentering-the-map]] §1.)*

---

## 10. 🔬 Researcher's Commentary — Why Three Evidence Types Cannot Be Collapsed

> **Preserved per ADR-0009 and ADR-0010. This section IS the worked demonstration of the never-collapse rule.**

| Question asked | Evidence type | What it yields |
|---|---|---|
| *What does "Esau" mean?* | **lexical** | **nothing that solves Romans 9** |
| *What is Paul's immediate context?* | **contextual** | he is discussing Israel and Yahweh's promise |
| *Where did the quotation come from?* | **intertextual** | **Malachi supplies EDOM; Genesis supplies TWO NATIONS** |

> ### **The intertextual evidence MATERIALLY ALTERS how much we are entitled to infer from Paul's words — and neither lexical nor contextual work would have produced it.**
>
> **If these three were merged into an undifferentiated "evidence," the Edom/nations dimension would simply be invisible. This is the case ADR-0010's *never collapse evidence types* clause exists to protect.**

## 11. The Objection Paul Anticipates

> **Romans 9:14** — ESV: "**Is there injustice on God's part? By no means!**"

**He asks it because his own argument sounds unfair. Then he quotes:**

> **Romans 9:15** — "**I will have mercy on whom I have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I have compassion.**"

**That is Exodus 33:19 — and the context is decisive:**

> **Exodus 33:19** — ESV: "I will make all my goodness pass before you and will proclaim before you my name 'The LORD.' And **I will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, and will show mercy on whom I will show mercy**."

> ### ❗ **WHAT IS HAPPENING IN EXODUS 33? THE GOLDEN CALF HAS JUST HAPPENED.**
>
> **Israel has catastrophically violated covenant. Moses intercedes. Yahweh's very presence among Israel is at issue. Moses asks to see His glory. And Yahweh proclaims His sovereign mercy.**

---

## 12. Which Means Israel Itself Survived by Mercy

**Paul is explaining why Yahweh's word has not failed despite Israel's present unbelief — and he reaches back to a moment when Israel had ALREADY committed a covenant-breaking offence grave enough to jeopardize the relationship.**

**What preserved the story? Not Israel's entitlement. YAHWEH'S MERCY.**

> ### **So Romans 9 is not simply "God enjoys deciding whom to condemn." The quotation Paul selects comes from a historical moment demonstrating that ISRAEL EXISTS AS COVENANT PEOPLE BECAUSE YAHWEH EXERCISES MERCY.**
>
> **A contextual finding that reframes the whole chapter — and it is available only by asking where the quotation came from.**

---

## 13. Pharaoh — and the Hardening Both Ways

> **Romans 9:17** — ESV: "'**For this very purpose I have raised you up, that I might show my power in you**, and that my name might be proclaimed in all the earth.'"

> **Romans 9:18** — ESV: "So then **he has mercy on whomever he wills, and he hardens whomever he wills**."

> ### ⚖️ **Did Yahweh harden Pharaoh? YES. Does Exodus also say Pharaoh hardened his own heart? YES.**
>
> **Across the Exodus narrative the language varies: Pharaoh's heart is hardened · Pharaoh hardens his heart · Yahweh hardens Pharaoh's heart.**
>
> ### **Scripture again refuses the binary "divine sovereignty OR human responsibility." The narrative contains both — and we do not use Romans 9 to erase the Exodus account Paul is quoting.**

---

## 14. Pharaoh Is Not a Random Example — Look at the Structure

| Example | What it concerns |
|---|---|
| **Isaac / Ishmael** | covenant line |
| **Jacob / Esau** | covenant line **and nations** |
| **Moses / Israel after the golden calf** | **mercy** |
| **Pharaoh / Egypt** | hardening and judgment |

> ### **ALL of these are events from the FORMATION OF ISRAEL'S COVENANT IDENTITY.**
>
> ### **Paul is not assembling random proof texts about abstract predestination. He is RETELLING ISRAEL'S OWN SCRIPTURAL HISTORY to explain Israel's present crisis.**

> **MARKED AS A MAJOR OBSERVATION: *Romans 9's election argument is embedded in Israel's covenant history before it becomes a systematic-theological abstraction.***
>
> **That does not tell us what systematic theology may legitimately derive. It tells us the ORDER OF INTERPRETATION: exegesis first, theology afterward.**

---

## 15. The Potter and the Clay — and Where the Image Comes From

> **Romans 9:20–21** — ESV: "Will what is molded say to its molder, '**Why have you made me like this?**' Has the potter no right over the clay, to make out of the same lump **one vessel for honorable use and another for dishonorable use**?"

**Creator/creature sovereignty is certainly present. But the methodology asks: where does the image come from?**

> **Jeremiah 18:6** — ESV: "**Like the clay in the potter's hand, so are you in my hand, O HOUSE OF ISRAEL.**"

> ### **The potter/clay imagery has an EXPLICIT COVENANTAL/NATIONAL APPLICATION TO ISRAEL.**
>
> **And Jeremiah 18 immediately continues by describing nations whose announced judgment CAN CHANGE when they repent, and nations whose promised good can be withheld when they do evil.**
>
> ### **That does not make Romans 9 simple. It makes it LESS simple.**

---

## 16. ⚖️ Which Requires a Brake in Both Directions

> ❌ **"Potter and clay proves every individual's eternal destiny was immutably determined before birth."** — **Not from the metaphor alone.**
> ❌ **"Potter and clay has nothing whatsoever to do with individual salvation."** — **Also too strong.**

> ### ✅ **What CAN be established: Paul uses prophetic imagery whose scriptural background concerns Yahweh's sovereign authority over COVENANT PEOPLES AND NATIONS, particularly Israel.**
>
> **The precise relationship between that corporate sovereignty and individual salvation must still be determined from Paul's whole argument.**
>
> ### **Do not ask a metaphor to prove more than its source context permits.**

---

## 17. ✅ GRAMMATICAL VERIFICATION COMPLETED — Romans 9:22–23

> **This was an assigned lexical/grammatical task under the Lexical Escalation rule. It was carried out, and the asymmetry is REAL and verifiable.**

> **Romans 9:22** — ESV: "vessels of wrath **prepared for destruction**." **Greek: κατηρτισμένα — *katērtismena*.**
> **PARSING: perfect participle, MIDDLE OR PASSIVE, accusative neuter plural. NO STATED AGENT.**

> **Romans 9:23** — ESV: "vessels of mercy, **which he has prepared beforehand** for glory." **Greek: προητοίμασεν — *proētoimasen*.**
> **PARSING: aorist indicative ACTIVE, 3rd person singular. GOD IS THE SUBJECT, explicitly.**

> ### ✅ **ESTABLISHED (grammatical): Paul does NOT use parallel constructions. Verse 23 has an active verb with God as subject. Verse 22 has a participle that is morphologically ambiguous between middle and passive, and names no agent.**
>
> ### ⚠️ **NOT ESTABLISHED: what that asymmetry MEANS.** The middle voice would suggest the vessels *fitted themselves*; the passive would suggest they *were fitted*; and Greek perfect middle/passive forms are frequently identical, so **the ambiguity may be morphological accident rather than authorial intent.**

> ### 🔬 **AND A NEVER-COLLAPSE FLAG, recorded because it was nearly missed:**
> **The interlinear resource consulted added the editorial comment that "the passive sense dominates."** **That is an INTERPRETIVE GLOSS, not part of the parsing.** **The parsing says *middle or passive*. The gloss says *passive*.** **We record the parsing as established and the gloss as one commentator's reading** — exactly the distinction ADR-0010 exists to protect.

> **🔴 AND THE TRANSLATIONS SPLIT ALONG THE SAME LINE:** **ESV "prepared for destruction"** (agent-neutral, leaves the ambiguity) vs **CJB "people who deserved punishment and were **ripe for** destruction"** (reads the **middle** sense — they became so). **Fifth passage where an English rendering has been quoted to this project as though it were the verse.**

**Filed to [[04-open-questions]]. Requires consultation of a critical Greek grammar on perfect middle/passive ambiguity — the parsing alone does not resolve authorial intent.**

## 18. Then Romans 9 Refuses to Stay Inside Ethnic Israel

> **Romans 9:24** *(indexed)* — "even us whom he has called, **not from the Jews only but also from the Gentiles**."

**Paul's argument now explicitly expands — and immediately he quotes Hosea's "those who were not my people I will call 'my people.'"**

> ### **This is NOT a random Gentile proof text. Paul has spent the chapter establishing promise · election · mercy · hardening — and NOW calling from Jews AND Gentiles. That is the transition.**

---

## 19. ⚠️ The Intertextual Complication — Held Open

**Hosea originally speaks about ISRAEL. His "not my people" are covenant-breaking Israelites promised restoration.** **Yet Paul invokes that language while discussing Jews + Gentiles.**

**Possibilities, none selected:**

1. Paul sees Hosea's **restoration pattern** as applicable to Gentile inclusion.
2. Paul quotes Hosea concerning **restored Israel** while his larger sentence encompasses both.
3. Paul understands **Israel's restoration and Gentile inclusion as participating in ONE larger Messianic restoration.**
4. Other possibilities exist.

> ### **WE DO NOT CHOOSE YET — because Romans 11 is waiting, and we promised not to let Romans 11 dictate Romans 9 prematurely.** **Open intertextual question** ([[04-open-questions]]).

## 20. Isaiah Brings In the Remnant

> **Romans 9:27** — ESV: "Though the number of the sons of Israel be as the sand of the sea, **only a remnant of them will be saved**."

**Now we are much closer to understanding 9:6, because Paul has introduced ISRAEL and REMNANT.**

> ### **This may not be "ethnic Israel versus non-Israelite Church" at all. It may be ISRAEL AS A HISTORICAL PEOPLE WITHIN WHOM YAHWEH PRESERVES A FAITHFUL/ELECT REMNANT — a category deeply rooted in the prophets.**
>
> **Still not settled until Romans 11. But the remnant category is now explicit, and it was supplied by Paul, not by us.**

---

## 21. And the Immediate Crisis Is Named — Faith and a Stumbling Stone

> **Romans 9:30–31** — ESV: "**Gentiles who did not pursue righteousness have attained it**, that is, a righteousness that is by faith… **Israel who pursued a law that would lead to righteousness did not succeed**." CJB: "Isra'el, even though they kept pursuing a Torah that offers righteousness, did not reach what the Torah offers."

> **Romans 9:32–33** — ESV: "They did not pursue it **by faith**, but as if it were based on works. **They have stumbled over the stumbling stone**… '**whoever believes in him will not be put to shame**.'" CJB: "not as being grounded in trusting but as if it were grounded in **doing legalistic works**."

> **🔍 Note CJB's "legalistic works" is an interpretive rendering where ESV reads "works" — recorded as a translation datum, not adopted.**

> ### **Romans 9 places FAITH IN MESSIAH directly inside the Israel question.**
>
> **Ethnic descent is not meaningless — Paul honoured it in verses 1–5. Torah is not meaningless — Paul named it among Israel's privileges. But NEITHER ancestry NOR possession of Torah automatically produces the promised outcome. MESSIAH IS THE CRISIS POINT.**

---

## 22. So What Does 9:6 Mean — Provisionally?

### ✅ Established

**Paul distinguishes those who are "from Israel" from Israel in the sense relevant to Yahweh's promise.** His first examples are **Isaac/Ishmael and Jacob/Esau**, demonstrating that **physical descent alone has never determined the covenant line.** Later he introduces a **remnant of Israel**, and **Gentiles called alongside Jews**.

### ❌ NOT established

> **Romans 9:6 does NOT by itself establish "Israel now means the Gentile Church."**

### ❌ ALSO not established

> **Romans 9:6 does NOT mean "only biological Israel matters and Gentiles remain outside."** **Romans 9 destroys that reading by verse 24 — from inside the same chapter.**

> ### **Neither extreme survives the chapter intact — and this is NOT a compromise. Paul is building his own category, and we have not finished hearing it.**

## 23. Why Romans 9–11 Must Remain One Argument

| **Romans 9** | Has Yahweh's word to Israel failed? — **promise · election · remnant · mercy** |
| **Romans 10** | Then why is Israel presently stumbling? — **Messiah · faith · hearing/proclamation** |
| **Romans 11** | Has Yahweh therefore rejected His people? — **"By no means!"** then remnant · hardening · Gentiles · **olive tree · grafting · fullness · all Israel · irrevocable calling** |

> ### ❗ **And note what sits in the middle of Romans 10: HEARING AND PROCLAMATION. Which is the original question of this entire project.**
>
> **We cannot stop at 9:6 and build a position from it. Paul is not finished.**

## ✍️ Manuscript Voice — "Not All Israel Is Israel" Did Not Mean What the Slogan Suggested

> **Prose:** I had heard Paul's sentence so often that I thought I already knew what it meant: *"Not all who are descended from Israel are Israel."* Read alone, it seemed almost designed to settle the identity question. Perhaps biological Israel had simply given way to another Israel defined entirely by faith. **But Paul would not let the sentence remain alone.**
>
> He began the chapter grieving for his own kinsmen according to the flesh — **and still called them Israelites**. He then named their adoption, covenants, Torah, worship, promises and patriarchs before identifying Messiah Himself as coming from them according to the flesh. **Whatever distinction Paul was about to make within Israel, he had not begun by declaring physical Israel meaningless.**
>
> Then his examples surprised me. **He did not explain "Israel within Israel" by immediately pointing to Gentile Christians. He pointed backward:** Abraham had Ishmael and Isaac; Isaac had Esau and Jacob. **Biological descent had always been real without being sufficient to determine the covenant line.** Then the prophets supplied another category: **the remnant.** Only later did the Gentiles explicitly enter Paul's argument.
>
> **The famous sentence therefore ceased to be an answer and became a question:** what kind of Israel does Paul mean when he distinguishes Israel from those who descend from Israel? I could no longer permit a theological slogan to answer before Paul finished his sentence across Romans 9, 10 and 11.

> ### 🆕 **And the methodological line worth keeping separately:**
> > ### **A familiar verse is not necessarily a familiar argument.**

## 24. Next — Romans 10, and the Text That Could Falsify the Original Position

> ### **Our investigation began with: WHO IS COMMANDED TO PREACH?**
>
> **Romans 10 contains: "How are they to believe in him of whom they have never heard? And how are they to hear without someone preaching?"**
>
> ### **That is one of the strongest texts anyone could bring AGAINST the original position. We do not avoid it. We run directly at it.**

**The questions to ask:** what is Paul saying · **to whom** · **about whom** · and does *"how beautiful are the feet of those who preach the good news"* establish **a universal evangelistic office for every believer** — or **something considerably more specific inside Paul's argument about Israel**?
