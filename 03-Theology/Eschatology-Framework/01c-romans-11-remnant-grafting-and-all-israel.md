# Stage 24 — Romans 11: Remnant, Grafting, and "All Israel"

> ### **This closes the Romans 9–11 arc, and with it the "Who are the people of God?" branch that Hebrews 4 opened at Stage 20.**

**Method carried in (Stage 21½):** Romans 11 is not read backward through later systematics. *"All Israel"* is not resolved before Paul's own argument resolves it — and where he leaves it open, it stays open.

---

## 1. Paul Asks the Question Directly — and Answers It

> **Romans 11:1** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "I ask, then, **has God rejected his people? By no means!** For I myself am an Israelite, a descendant of Abraham, a member of the tribe of Benjamin."

> **Romans 11:2** — "**God has not rejected his people whom he foreknew.**"

**Paul's first move is to present himself as evidence** — ethnically and covenantally an Israelite, and a believer.

> ### ❗ **THIS IS THE CANONICAL CONTROL ON ROMANS 9:6.** *"Not all who are from Israel are Israel"* (Stage 22) is not the opening sentence of Israel's cancellation. **Two chapters later Paul asks that exact question explicitly and answers it "by no means."**

**Tier A (Text).** Not an inference — Paul poses the question in his own words.

---

## 2. Elijah and the Remnant

> **Romans 11:3–4** — ESV: "'Lord, they have killed your prophets… and **I alone am left**'… But what is God's reply to him? '**I have kept for myself seven thousand men** who have not bowed the knee to Baal.'" *(1 Kings 19:18)*

> **Romans 11:5** — "**So too at the present time there is a remnant, chosen by grace.**"

**The category Romans 9 introduced (Stage 22 §, the *remnant* of 9:27) is now named and applied to Paul's own moment.**

> ### **Not "Israel replaced." Israel CONTAINS a remnant preserved by grace.**

**Note the shape of the Elijah precedent:** the prophet's perception was that faithfulness had vanished entirely; **Yahweh's reply was that it had not, and that Elijah could not see it.** That is a methodological datum as much as a historical one.

---

## 3. Grace and Works — In Context

> **Romans 11:6** — ESV: "But if it is by grace, it is no longer on the basis of works; otherwise grace would no longer be grace."

**Routinely extracted as a free-floating slogan. In context it explains WHY THE REMNANT EXISTS** — covenant standing is not secured by boasting or merit.

**Brake:** this does not make obedience irrelevant. Romans 10:16 (Stage 23 §10) had Paul speaking of **obeying** the gospel and treating unbelief and disobedience as the same failure. **Both stand.** *(Pattern established across Stages 19–23: grace + faith + obedience, none reduced into the others.)*

---

## 4. Hardening — and Immediately, Its Limits

> **Romans 11:7** — ESV: "Israel failed to obtain what it was seeking. The elect obtained it, but **the rest were hardened**."

Severe. **But Paul qualifies it twice, and both qualifications are his own:**

| Qualifier | Where | Force |
|---|---|---|
| **partial** | 11:25 — **ἀπὸ μέρους** *apo merous* | not total |
| **until** | 11:25 — **ἄχρι οὗ** *achri hou* | not obviously permanent |

**Both verified by interlinear retrieval — see §13.**

---

## 5. "Did They Stumble in Order That They Might Fall?"

> **Romans 11:11** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "So I ask, **did they stumble in order that they might fall? By no means!** Rather, through their trespass salvation has come to the Gentiles, **so as to make Israel jealous**."

**Paul's second explicit "by no means" in the chapter.** The jealousy motif planted at Romans 10:19 (Deuteronomy 32:21, Stage 23 §15) now detonates exactly as flagged.

> ### **Gentile inclusion is NOT framed as: Israel falls → Gentiles replace them → story over. It is framed as part of a restorative strategy directed BACK at Israel.**

---

## 6. Paul Expects Something Beyond the Present Remnant

> **Romans 11:12** — ESV: "if their trespass means riches for the world… **how much more will their full inclusion mean!**"

> **Romans 11:15** — ESV: "For if their rejection means the reconciliation of the world, **what will their acceptance mean but life from the dead?**"

**11:15 was not in the tranche and is added here from the retrieved text** — it strengthens the point independently: Paul contemplates an **acceptance**, and describes it in resurrection language.

> ### **This forecloses one reading outright: Paul does NOT treat Israel's present unbelief as the final state of Israel as Israel.**

**NOT ESTABLISHED** — whether "full inclusion" / "acceptance" means a future mass conversion, a first-century restoration, cumulative salvation across history, or something else. **Open question q69.**

⚠️ **And "life from the dead" (11:15) must not be quietly cashed as a resurrection-timing datum for the eschatological framework.** It may be metaphorical, may be literal, may be both. **Flagged, not used. Open question q70.**

---

## 7. Audience Shift, Explicitly Marked

> **Romans 11:13–14** — ESV: "**Now I am speaking to you Gentiles.** Inasmuch then as I am an apostle to the Gentiles, I magnify my ministry in order somehow to **make my fellow Jews jealous, and thus save some of them**."

**Paul marks the audience shift himself** — which matters, because everything from here to 11:32 is addressed to Gentile believers and is largely a **warning to them**.

> ### **Paul's Gentile mission and his concern for Israel are not rival projects. They are one project.** *Expansion without erasure* — the same rule established at Stage 21.

---

## 8. The Olive Tree — Incorporation, Not Substitution

> **Romans 11:16–17** — ESV: "if **the root is holy, so are the branches**. But if some of the branches were broken off, and you, **although a wild olive shoot, were grafted in among the others** and now **share in the nourishing root** of the olive tree…"

**What Paul does NOT say, and the absence is load-bearing:**
- He does not say the Gentile believer becomes a **second tree**
- He does not say the old tree is **destroyed** and a Gentile tree replaces it
- He does not say the branches broken off are **replaced in kind**

> ### **Wild branches are grafted INTO AN EXISTING CULTIVATED TREE. That is incorporation.**

---

## 9. But What Is the Root? — Deliberately Not Settled

**Candidates:** Abraham and the patriarchal promises · the covenant promises generally · Israel's covenantal root · Messiah · some combination.

**Romans 11 never says "the root is Christ."** It says Gentiles **share in the nourishing root**, and that **the root supports you** (11:18).

**Contextual lean, recorded as contextual not lexical:** the Abrahamic/patriarchal framing of Romans 9–11 — and especially 11:28's *"beloved for the sake of their forefathers"* — weighs toward the patriarchal-promise reading.

> ### **That is a lean. The referent is preserved as an open exegetical question — q72 — because the project's own eventual identity language depends on it and that is exactly the place to be most careful.**

---

## 10. The Warning to Gentiles — Symmetrical and Severe

> **Romans 11:18** — ESV: "**do not be arrogant toward the branches.** If you are, remember **it is not you who support the root, but the root that supports you**."

> **Romans 11:19–21** — ESV: "Then you will say, 'Branches were broken off so that I might be grafted in.' **That is true.** They were broken off because of their unbelief, **but you stand fast through faith. So do not become proud, but fear.** For if God did not spare the natural branches, **neither will he spare you**."

> **Romans 11:22** — ESV: "Note then **the kindness and the severity of God**… **provided you continue in his kindness. Otherwise you too will be cut off.**"

> ### ❗ **GENTILE INCLUSION CREATES NO BASIS WHATSOEVER FOR CONTEMPT TOWARD ISRAEL. The same principle that removed unbelieving branches can remove arrogant grafted ones. Paul says so in as many words.**

**Note 11:19: Paul grants the Gentile's premise — "that is true" — and then refuses the conclusion drawn from it.** The facts are conceded; the arrogance is not.

---

## 11. Faith Is the Dividing Issue

> **Romans 11:20** — "They were broken off **because of their unbelief**, but you stand fast **through faith**."

**Not ethnicity alone. Not Torah possession alone. Not Gentile identity alone.**

And because faith is not treated as bare intellectual assent elsewhere in this investigation *(Romans 10:16's "obeyed the gospel," Hebrews' unbelief↔disobedience — Stages 19, 20, 23)*, **obedience remains part of its lived expression.**

---

## 12. Natural Branches Can Be Grafted Back — Into *Their Own* Tree

> **Romans 11:23** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "**And even they, if they do not continue in their unbelief, will be grafted in**, for God has the power to graft them in again."

> **Romans 11:24** — ESV: "how much more will these, the natural branches, be grafted back into **their own olive tree**."

> ### ❗❗ **"THEIR OWN OLIVE TREE."** Paul's own phrase, about currently-unbelieving Israel, in the middle of the metaphor. **This makes it very difficult to argue the olive tree denotes a Gentile institution that replaced Israel** — the tree is described as belonging to the natural branches even while they are off it.

**Tier A.** The possessive is in the text.

---

## 13. The "Mystery" — Two Qualifiers, Both Verified

> **Romans 11:25** — ESV: "**Lest you be wise in your own sight**, I do not want you to be unaware of this mystery, brothers: **a partial hardening has come upon Israel, until the fullness of the Gentiles has come in.**"

**Note first: the mystery is introduced as an ANTIDOTE TO GENTILE ARROGANCE, not as a prophecy chart.** Paul says why he is telling them.

### Greek — VERIFIED BY INTERLINEAR RETRIEVAL

| English | Greek | Strong's |
|---|---|---|
| "partial" | **ἀπὸ μέρους** *apo merous* | 575 + 3313 |
| "until" | **ἄχρι οὗ** *achri hou* | 891 + 3739 |
| "the fullness of the Gentiles" | **τὸ πλήρωμα τῶν ἐθνῶν** *to plērōma tōn ethnōn* | 4138 + 1484 |

**ἀπὸ μέρους — the hardening is explicitly PARTIAL, not total.**
**ἄχρι οὗ — "until." A temporal limit is stated.**

> ### **BRAKE: *achri hou* marks a terminus; it does NOT by itself specify what happens after it.** "Until X" does not automatically entail "and then not-Y." **The word establishes that Paul framed the hardening as bounded — nothing more, and this project does not extract more from it.**

**πλήρωμα τῶν ἐθνῶν — NOT ESTABLISHED** whether this denotes a number, a covenantal fullness, the completion of Gentile mission, representative inclusion of the nations, or something else. **Open question q73 — and it touches the original proclamation question directly**, since "the fullness of the nations coming in" is scope language of exactly the kind Stage 23's Universal-Language Scope Test now governs.

## 14. 🛑 Romans 11:26 — THREE-WAY TEXTUAL DIVERGENCE, AND PAUL MATCHES NEITHER SOURCE

> **Romans 11:26** — ESV: "**And in this way all Israel will be saved**, as it is written, '**The Deliverer will come from Zion**, he will banish ungodliness from Jacob.'"

### First: οὕτως is an ADVERB — VERIFIED

**Greek: καὶ οὕτως πᾶς Ἰσραὴλ σωθήσεται.** **οὕτως — *houtōs*, Strong's 3779, ADVERB.** "Thus / in this way / in this manner."

> ### ❗ **CHRONOLOGY CANNOT BE IMPORTED FROM THE ENGLISH WORD "so."** Paul may be saying *"and in this MANNER all Israel will be saved"* rather than *"and AFTER the Gentile fullness, THEN all Israel is saved."* **The adverb does not settle the verse — but it removes the sequential reading's claim to be the obvious one.**

### Second — and this is the finding: the Zion preposition

**Retrieval of all three witnesses:**

| Witness | Text | Preposition | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MT Hebrew** (Sefaria, verified) | וּבָ֤א **לְצִיּוֹן֙** גּוֹאֵ֔ל | **לְ** *l'* | **TO** Zion |
| **LXX** (Elpenor, verified) | καὶ ἥξει **ἕνεκεν Σιὼν** ὁ ῥυόμενος | **ἕνεκεν** *heneken* | **FOR THE SAKE OF** Zion |
| **Paul, Rom 11:26** (interlinear, verified) | Ἥξει **ἐκ Σιὼν** ὁ Ῥυόμενος | **ἐκ** *ek* | **OUT OF / FROM** Zion |

> ### ❗❗ **THREE WITNESSES, THREE DIFFERENT PREPOSITIONS, THREE DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS. PAUL MATCHES NEITHER THE HEBREW NOR THE SEPTUAGINT.**

**This is a different and stronger phenomenon than Stage 23.** At Romans 10:18 Paul quoted the LXX **verbatim** (Stage 23 §13). Here he matches **no** extant witness examined.

**Consequences, stated as constraints rather than conclusions:**
- **Any argument resting on the DIRECTION of the Deliverer's coming — to Zion, from Zion, for Zion — is resting on a preposition that differs across every witness.** The project may not build on it.
- **This is a SOURCE-TEXT divergence (Hebrew / Greek / Paul), NOT a translation divergence (ESV / CJB / RVR1960).** Different category, different count, **and the two tallies may not be merged** — see §17.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** whether Paul used a variant Greek text now lost, quoted from memory, deliberately reshaped, or conflated Isaiah 59:20 with another passage. **Open question q74.**

### Third: the object is Jacob

> **Romans 11:26b–27** — "he will banish ungodliness **from Jacob**"; "and this will be my covenant with them **when I take away their sins**." *(Isaiah 59:20–21, with covenant language resembling Isaiah 27:9)*

> ### **If "all Israel" meant exclusively the combined Jew–Gentile Church, it is difficult to explain why Paul immediately glosses it with a prophecy about removing ungodliness from JACOB.**

**That does not eliminate broader covenant-people readings — but it places substantial weight on Israel/Jacob remaining central.** Strong **intertextual** evidence — **recorded as intertextual, not lexical.**

## 15. "All Israel" — Four Readings, None Selected

| | Reading |
|---|---|
| **A** | **Ethnic/national Israel collectively** — a future or climactic restoration |
| **B** | **The elect remnant of Israel across history** — the totality of saved Israelites |
| **C** | **The total covenant people** — Jew + Gentile together |
| **D** | **A corporate-remnant formulation** — Israel in its promised, elect fullness rather than every ethnic Israelite without exception |

> ### **NOT SELECTED. Open question q75 — and it is deliberately left open even though the project's own identity language would be easier if one were chosen.**

**What the evidence does constrain:** the *Jacob* gloss (§14) weighs against a reading that empties Israel of ethnic reference; *"their own olive tree"* (§12) weighs against replacement; *"broken off because of unbelief"* (§11) weighs against ethnicity-alone.

## 16. The Statement Replacement Models Must Reckon With

> **Romans 11:28** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "**As regards the gospel, they are enemies for your sake. But as regards election, they are beloved for the sake of their forefathers.**"

> **Romans 11:29** — ESV: "For **the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable**."

### Greek — VERIFIED BY INTERLINEAR RETRIEVAL

**ἀμεταμέλητα — *ametamelēta*, Strong's 278.** Adjective, nominative neuter plural. **"Not to be repented of / not regretted / irrevocable."** Sentence-initial for emphasis: *ἀμεταμέλητα γὰρ τὰ χαρίσματα καὶ ἡ κλῆσις τοῦ Θεοῦ.*

> ### **Paul holds BOTH simultaneously, of the SAME PEOPLE, in adjacent clauses: presently "enemies as regards the gospel" AND "beloved as regards election."**

**No all-or-nothing model survives 11:28–29 intact.** Two truths, held together, about one group.

## 17. Divergence Tallies — Kept Separate, Deliberately

**Stage 23 established a running count of TRANSLATION divergences (English witnesses rendering the same Greek differently). Stage 24 introduces a SOURCE-TEXT divergence (Hebrew, Greek, and Paul differing at the level of the text itself).**

| Category | Count | Instances |
|---|---|---|
| **Translation divergence** (ESV/CJB/RVR1960) | **8** | Mark 7:19 · Col 2:14 · Heb 4:9 · Eph 2:15 · Rom 9:22 · Rom 10:4 · Rom 10:15 · Rom 10:18 |
| **Source-text divergence** (MT/LXX/NT author) | **2** | Ps 19:5 *qav*/*phthongos* (Stage 23) · **Isa 59:20 *l'*/*heneken*/*ek* (Stage 24 — three-way)** |

> ### ❗ **MERGING THESE INTO "TEN DIVERGENCES" WOULD BE AN EVIDENCE-TYPE COLLAPSE (ADR-0010).** A translator choosing between senses of one Greek word and an author quoting a source text that reads differently in every witness are **not the same phenomenon** and do not support the same conclusions. **Counted separately, permanently.**

⚠️ **CJB was NOT retrieved for Romans 11 this pass.** No translation-divergence claim is made for this chapter in either direction. **Open question q71.**

## 18. Mercy Is the Terminus

> **Romans 11:30–32** — ESV: "just as you were at one time disobedient… but now have received mercy because of their disobedience, so they too have now been disobedient **in order that by the mercy shown to you they also may now receive mercy**. For **God has consigned all to disobedience, that he may have mercy on all**."

**The argument terminates in neither ethnic boasting nor theological boasting nor deterministic pride. It terminates in mercy.**

> ### **And the arc is coherent across all three chapters: Romans 9 grounded election in Exodus 33:19 — "I will have mercy on whom I have mercy" (Stage 22). Romans 11 ends with mercy on all. Paul opened and closed the same way.**

## 19. 📐 Paul's Own Epistemology — This Belongs in the Methodology

> **Romans 11:33** — ESV: "Oh, **the depth of the riches and wisdom and knowledge of God!** How **unsearchable** are his judgments and how **inscrutable** his ways!"

> **Romans 11:34–36** — "For who has known the mind of the Lord, or who has been his counselor?… For from him and through him and to him are all things."

**Paul has just spent three dense chapters reasoning at maximum intensity** — Torah, Prophets, Writings, history, logic, intertextuality, rhetorical questions, an extended metaphor, and an explicit "mystery."

**And he ends in worship, not in a completed system.**

> ### **THIS IS THE POSTURE THIS PROJECT TRIED TO ESTABLISH AT [[00b-biblical-epistemology]] BEFORE IT KNEW PAUL MODELED IT.** Rigorous investigation does not terminate in *"I have Yahweh fully figured out."* It terminates in **what has been revealed held confidently, and the depths acknowledged as depths.**
>
> **Rigor and reverence are not opposites — and Romans 11:33 arrives AFTER the exegesis, not instead of it.** The humility is earned, not a substitute for the work.

**Added to the epistemology as a worked biblical precedent.**

## 20. Has Yahweh Replaced Israel?

**Based on Romans 11: no simple replacement model survives.** Paul explicitly states, in his own words:

| # | Paul's statement | Verse |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Yahweh has **not rejected** His people | 11:1–2 |
| 2 | A **remnant** exists, chosen by grace | 11:5 |
| 3 | The stumbling is **not a final fall** | 11:11 |
| 4 | The hardening is **partial** (*apo merous*) | 11:25 |
| 5 | The hardening runs **until** (*achri hou*) | 11:25 |
| 6 | Gentiles are **grafted into an existing tree** | 11:17 |
| 7 | Gentiles are **commanded not to boast**, and can themselves be cut off | 11:18–22 |
| 8 | Natural branches **can be grafted back** — into **their own** tree | 11:23–24 |
| 9 | Israel remains **beloved as regards election** | 11:28 |
| 10 | Yahweh's calling is **irrevocable** (*ametamelēta*) | 11:29 |

### ⚖️ And the counterweight, with equal force

**Ethnic descent alone does not guarantee participation in the saving fullness of the promise.** Branches were broken off **because of unbelief** (11:20). Faith is the dividing issue.

> ### **Ethnicity matters — and ethnicity does not substitute for faith in Messiah. Distinction without separation. Neither half may be dropped to make a cleaner system.**

## 21. Does the Church "Become Israel"?

**Romans 11 does not use that formulation.** Paul supplies his own vocabulary instead: **wild branches grafted into a cultivated tree, sharing a root that supports them.**

> ### **METHODOLOGICAL RULE — USE PAUL'S METAPHOR BEFORE INVENTING A LABEL.**
>
> **Grafting is currently more textually precise than either "replacement" or simple identity-collapse.** The metaphor preserves distinctions — natural branches, wild branches, root, pruning, restoration — that **every available one-word label erases.**

---

## 22. What This Does to "Messianic Israelite"

**It does not make the term wrong. It reinforces the caution already established** (Stages 17, 21).

If used personally, it should mean something like:

> *"Through Yeshua HaMashiach I understand myself as **grafted into** the covenantal people rooted in Israel's promises, sharing the nourishment of that root **through faith** — **without** claiming biological descent from Jacob, and **without** claiming that Gentile believers erase Israel's distinct historical identity."*

> ### **The label remains downstream. Romans 11's metaphor is stronger than any terminology this project could adopt — and that ordering (text → synthesis → label) is the Thesis Discipline applied to identity rather than to eschatology.**

## 23. The Original Proclamation Question Gets Another Layer

**Paul's Gentile ministry has an Israel-facing purpose** (11:13–14). So proclamation among the nations is not *"convert every nation because Israel is finished."* It sits inside a covenantal sequence:

**Israel's stumbling → Gentile inclusion → Israel provoked to jealousy → mercy/restoration**

> ### **This may matter at Matthew 24. A first-century proclamation whose stated purpose includes provoking Israel is a different object than a generic mandate to evangelize all nations — and the two would be tested differently.**

**Recorded as a consideration, not an argument. Open question q73 carries the *plērōma* half of it.**

## 24. Romans 9–11 as One Argument

| Chapter | Question | Paul's answer |
|---|---|---|
| **9** | Has Yahweh's **word** failed? | **No.** Promise has always operated through election, mercy, and remnant |
| **10** | Then **why is Israel stumbling**? | Messiah is the stumbling stone; righteousness is by faith; **proclamation occurred**; many did not obey |
| **11** | Has Israel therefore been **rejected**? | **No.** Remnant remains · hardening partial and bounded · Gentiles grafted in · arrogance forbidden · natural branches may return · **calling irrevocable** |

> ### ❗ **THE CANONICAL CONTROL, STATED PLAINLY: Romans 9:6 cannot be responsibly interpreted without Romans 11:1.** *"Not all who are from Israel are Israel"* cannot be turned into *"God rejected Israel"* — **because Paul asks that exact question two chapters later and answers "by no means."**

## 25. ✍️ Manuscript Voice — Paul Would Not Let Either Side Boast

> **By the end of Romans 11, the argument had become remarkably difficult to conscript for either of the simple systems I had inherited. Paul would not allow ethnic descent to become a substitute for faith: natural branches had been broken off through unbelief. Yet he was equally unwilling to let believing Gentiles interpret their inclusion as Israel's replacement. They were wild branches, grafted into a tree they had not planted, nourished by a root that supported them rather than the other way around.**
>
> **The warning was therefore symmetrical. Israel could not boast in ancestry apart from faith, and Gentiles could not boast in faith as though grace had made them superior to Israel. Both depended upon Yahweh's mercy.**
>
> **That finally made Romans 9:6 look different. "Not all who are from Israel are Israel" was not the opening sentence of Israel's cancellation. By Romans 11 Paul explicitly denied that Yahweh had rejected His people. The distinction within Israel was real; the remnant was real; Gentile incorporation was real; Israel's hardening was real; and Israel's continuing election was real. Paul refused to flatten any of them.**
>
> **The olive tree became more useful than the labels. There was one rooted covenantal story, natural branches, wild branches, pruning, grafting and the possibility of restoration. The moment I tried to replace that living image with a slogan, I seemed to lose something Paul had deliberately preserved.**

And:

> **The argument ended where the methodology had begun: humility. After three chapters of exegesis, history and theological reasoning, Paul did not announce that he had exhausted Yahweh's mind. He worshiped: "Oh, the depth." Rigorous interpretation and reverence were not opposites. The deeper the reasoning went, the more appropriate humility became.**

## 26. 🔬 Researcher's Commentary

**The Isaiah 59:20 check was run because Stage 23 had just been burned by one.** Having found that Romans 10:18's *oikoumenē* was inherited rather than chosen, the obvious next discipline was to check whether Romans 11:26's quotation behaved the same way. **It did not — it behaved worse.** Paul matched neither the Hebrew nor the Septuagint.

**Two stages, two quotations, two entirely different relationships to the source.** Had either been generalized from the other, the result would have been wrong:
- Generalizing Stage 23 forward → *"Paul quotes the LXX"* → **false at 11:26**
- Generalizing Stage 24 backward → *"Paul reshapes his sources"* → **false at 10:18, where he is verbatim**

> ### **The lesson is narrower and more useful than either generalization: QUOTATION BEHAVIOR MUST BE CHECKED PER QUOTATION. It is not a property of the author.**

**On what was deliberately not done:** four readings of "all Israel" were listed and **none selected**, even though this project's own identity language (*Messianic Israelite*) would be simpler to justify under reading C or D. **Selecting one here would have been the Thesis Discipline failing in the one place it is hardest to notice — where the convenient answer is also genuinely defensible.**

**CJB was not retrieved for Romans 11** (q71). Recorded rather than silently omitted, because Stage 23 found three CJB divergences in a single chapter and their absence here is **unknown, not established as absent.**

## 27. Two New Methodological Rules

Both added to `00-methodology-current.md` and `00b-biblical-epistemology.md`.

### Rule — Question–Answer Canonical Control

> **When a biblical author later asks EXPLICITLY the theological question that readers infer from an earlier statement, the author's own answer exerts strong interpretive control over that inference.**
>
> **Worked instance:** Romans 9:6's *"not all who are from Israel are Israel"* must be read consistently with Romans 11:1, where Paul asks *"has God rejected his people?"* and answers **"by no means."** **An inference drawn from an earlier verse may not survive the author's own later denial of that inference.**

### Rule — Metaphor Preservation

> **When an author supplies a sustained metaphor to describe a complex theological relationship, PRESERVE THE METAPHOR'S DISTINCTIONS before replacing it with a later theological label.**
>
> Labels such as *"replacement," "continuity," "spiritual Israel," "the Church is Israel"* may obscure distinctions the author deliberately preserved. **Paul's olive tree distinguishes root, natural branches, wild branches, breaking off, grafting in, and grafting back — six moving parts that every available one-word label collapses.**
>
> **Corollary:** if a label cannot reproduce the metaphor's distinctions, the label is lossy and the metaphor stays.
