# Stage 31 — Daniel 12: Resurrection, Distress, Sealing, and "the Time of the End"

> ### **Delayed deliberately until it could be entered WITHOUT being used as ammunition for or against the little-season hypothesis. This is the final major Tanakh pressure test before Revelation.**

**First rule for this chapter:**

> ### ❗ **DO NOT DETACH DANIEL 12 FROM DANIEL 11 merely because Daniel 12 contains famous resurrection language.** Daniel 12:1's *"at that time"* takes its immediate referent from the conflict just described. **Daniel 11 may eventually need its own depth; the internal structure of Daniel 12 can still be mapped now.**

---

## 1. Michael, and Daniel's People

> **Daniel 12:1** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "At that time shall arise **Michael, the great prince who has charge of your people**. And there shall be **a time of trouble, such as never has been since there was a nation till that time**. But at that time **your people shall be delivered, everyone whose name shall be found written in the book**."

**Michael is tied specifically to *Daniel's people*. Israel's covenantal crisis is in the foreground from the first clause.**

### The Matthew echo — VERIFIED

> **Matthew 24:21** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "For then there will be **great tribulation, such as has not been from the beginning of the world until now**, no, and never will be."

> ### **A very strong intertextual connection. Matthew appears to be drawing on Daniel 12 — so whatever Daniel's distress is, Matthew 24 expects the reader to remember it.**

**And note the pairing inside 12:1: distress AND deliverance of the written-in-the-book people — the remnant/elect pattern this project has met repeatedly** *(Stages 22, 24)*. **Reminiscent of later book-of-life imagery — but Revelation is not brought in yet.**

## 2. The Resurrection Text

> **Daniel 12:2** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "And **many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake**, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt."

**Hebrew: יְשֵׁנֵי אַדְמַת־עָפָר — *yeshenê admat-ʿafar*, roughly "sleepers of the dust-ground."**

> ### **NOT naturally read as "spiritually sleepy living Israelites." Symbolic dimensions may exist; BODILY RESURRECTION CARRIES SUBSTANTIAL PRIMA FACIE WEIGHT.** *(Taxonomy: **A / E** — Stage 29.)*

---

## 3. ⚠️ CORRECTION — "Many" Does Not Establish "Not All"

**Hebrew: רַבִּים — *rabbim*, "many"** *(verified Stage 27)*.

**This project recorded that datum at Stages 27 and 29 as "recorded, not argued from." That restraint was correct, and now the reason must be stated explicitly:**

> ### ❗ **BIBLICAL "MANY" CAN FUNCTION AS A BROAD COLLECTIVE RATHER THAN MATHEMATICALLY MEANING "SOME BUT DEFINITELY NOT ALL."**
>
> ### **SO THE INFERENCE — *"Daniel says many, therefore perhaps only Revelation 20's first-resurrection martyrs rise"* — IS TOO QUICK. POSSIBLE. NOT ESTABLISHED BY THE WORD *RABBIM* ALONE.**
>
> ### **That is exactly the lexical overreach this project exists to avoid — and it would have been an overreach in the hypothesis's favour.**

**Daniel 12:2 therefore leaves the SCOPE question more open than an English arithmetic reading suggests. Open question q110, superseding the narrower framing carried since Stage 27.**

---

## 4. The Shining — and the Matthew 13 Link

> **Daniel 12:3** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "And **those who are wise shall shine like the brightness of the sky above**; and those who turn many to righteousness, **like the stars forever and ever**."

> **Matthew 13:43** *(verified Stage 27)*: "Then the righteous will **shine like the sun** in the kingdom of their Father."

> ### ❗ **THEREFORE MATTHEW'S HARVEST-AT-THE-CONSUMMATION LANGUAGE IS NOT GENERIC JUDGMENT IMAGERY. It taps Daniel's sequence: DISTRESS → DELIVERANCE → RESURRECTION → RIGHTEOUS SHINING.**
>
> ### **DANIEL 12 IS THEREFORE ONE OF THE STRONGEST PRESSURE TEXTS AGAINST ANY READING OF MATTHEW 13 THAT REDUCES THE HARVEST TO THE DESTRUCTION OF JERUSALEM AS AN ADMINISTRATIVE EVENT. Something more eschatological is in play.**

## 5. 🔑 THE SEALING — Stated Twice

> **Daniel 12:4** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "But you, Daniel, **shut up the words and seal the book, until the time of the end**."

> **Daniel 12:9** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "Go your way, Daniel, for **the words are shut up and sealed until the time of the end**."

**Twice, at both ends of the closing section. Daniel receives prophecy whose fulfilment is far enough from him that sealing is commanded.**

> ### **This becomes the bridge to Revelation — see §13.**

## 6. ⚠️ ANACHRONISM TEST — "Run to and Fro" Is Not Cars and Internet

> **Daniel 12:4b** — "**Many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall increase.**"

### Hebrew — VERIFIED via Sefaria

> **יְשֹׁטְט֥וּ — *yeshoteṭu*** — rendered by Revised JPS as "**range far and wide**"
> **הַדָּֽעַת — *ha-daʿat*** — "the knowledge"

**Commonly modernized into airplanes, travel, and information technology.**

> ### ❗ **EXPLICITLY REJECTED: Daniel 12:4 = cars, airplanes, the internet — unless lexical and contextual evidence actually earns it.**
>
> ### **The verb may involve ranging about or searching THROUGH THE BOOK, and "the knowledge" may concern understanding THE PROPHECY rather than technological knowledge in general — note the definite article, and note that 12:4 sits immediately after the sealing command and immediately before Daniel says he does not understand.**

**A textbook application of the Anachronism Test (Stage 21½). Open question q111.**

## 7. "How Long?" — and the Completion Marker

> **Daniel 12:6** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "**How long shall it be till the end of these wonders?**"

> **Daniel 12:7** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "he raised his right hand and his left hand toward heaven and swore by him who lives forever that it would be for **a time, times, and half a time**, and that **when the shattering of the power of the holy people comes to an end all these things would be finished**."

> ### 🔑 **THE COMPLETION MARKER IS NOT A CALENDAR DATE. IT IS AN EVENT: *the shattering of the power of the holy people.*** And **"the holy people" in Daniel's context is Daniel's people** — which places the prophecy firmly inside Israel's covenantal story.

**Potentially highly relevant to first-century Jerusalem/Temple chronology — ⚠️ AND PRECISION IS REQUIRED BEFORE IDENTIFYING IT WITH AD 70. Open question q112.**

### "Time, times, and half a time"

**Also appears in Daniel 7:25.** Later associated in apocalyptic interpretation with 3½ times · 42 months · 1,260 days.

> ### 🚧 **ESSENTIAL AT REVELATION — AND WE MAY NOT ASSUME EVERY 3½-TYPE PERIOD ACROSS DANIEL AND REVELATION IS AUTOMATICALLY THE SAME HISTORICAL INTERVAL. SHARED APOCALYPTIC NUMBER → INTERTEXTUAL QUESTION, NOT AUTOMATIC IDENTITY.** *(Same brake as the trumpets, q100.)*

## 8. 📐 "I Heard, But I Did Not Understand"

> **Daniel 12:8** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "**I heard, but I did not understand.** Then I said, 'O my lord, what shall be the outcome of these things?'"

**And the answer is not an explanation — it is 12:9's restatement that the words are sealed until the time of the end.**

> ### ❗ **THE PROPHET HIMSELF RECEIVES THE REVELATION AND STILL DOES NOT UNDERSTAND THE CHRONOLOGY. A profound epistemological control, and it belongs in [[00b-biblical-epistemology]].**
>
> ### **Some realities become intelligible only in their appointed time. That resonates exactly with what this project has been building: HUMILITY WITHOUT ABANDONING INQUIRY — the same shape as Romans 11:33, where the humility arrives AFTER the reasoning.**

## 9. Understanding Is Tied to Purification

> **Daniel 12:10** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "**Many shall purify themselves and make themselves white and be refined**, but the wicked shall act wickedly. And **none of the wicked shall understand, but those who are wise shall understand**."

> ### **Understanding is NOT presented as intellectual capacity alone. Daniel ties discernment to covenantal faithfulness: purification · wisdom · understanding — against wickedness and lack of understanding.**
>
> ### 🚧 **BRAKE: this does NOT mean only morally perfect people understand prophecy — and it must not become a device for dismissing disagreement as wickedness. Recorded as a genuine biblical datum with that guard attached.**

**Added to the epistemology.**

## 10. 🔑 The Temple Marker — the Prophecy Is Not Floating in Abstraction

> **Daniel 12:11** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "And from the time that **the regular burnt offering is taken away** and **the abomination that makes desolate is set up**, there shall be **1,290 days**."

> ### ❗❗ **AN EXPLICITLY TEMPLE/SACRIFICIAL CHRONOLOGICAL MARKER. The prophecy is anchored to REGULAR OFFERING REMOVED and ABOMINATION/DESOLATION — which reconnects it to Daniel 9, Daniel 11, Matthew 24, and Jerusalem/Temple history.**
>
> ### **THAT MAKES A FIRST-CENTURY HISTORICAL HORIZON MORE PLAUSIBLE THAN A READING COMPLETELY DETACHED FROM JERUSALEM. Not proven — but the textual architecture DEMANDS Temple-history investigation.**

## 11. ⚠️ 1,290 and 1,335 — No Numerology Fishing

> **Daniel 12:12** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "**Blessed is he who waits and arrives at the 1,335 days.**"

**1,290 days · 1,335 days · difference: 45 days.**

> ### ❗ **WE DO NOT INVENT WHAT THE 45 DAYS REPRESENT. This is exactly the kind of prophecy where interpreters begin matching dates in history until something fits — and that violates the method.**

## 12. Daniel's Own Promise — Rest, Then Stand

> **Daniel 12:13** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "But go your way till the end. And **you shall rest and shall stand in your allotted place at the end of the days**."

> ### ❗ **DANIEL RESTS — AND LATER STANDS. That very naturally reads as death followed by resurrection/vindication.**
>
> ### **THE CHAPTER OPENS WITH SLEEPERS AWAKENING FROM DUST AND CLOSES WITH DANIEL HIMSELF PROMISED THAT AFTER RESTING HE WILL STAND. That bookending STRENGTHENS the bodily-resurrection reading.**

## 13. 🔑🔑 THE SEAL–NEARNESS CONTRAST — Verbal, Not Merely Thematic

**Both sides now verified by retrieval.**

| | Text | Command | Stated reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Daniel 12:4** | "shut up the words and **seal the book**" | **SEAL** | "**until the time of the end**" |
| **Daniel 12:9** | "the words are shut up and **sealed**" | **SEAL** | "**until the time of the end**" |
| **Revelation 22:10** | "**Do not seal up** the words of the prophecy of this book" | **DO NOT SEAL** | "**for the time is near**" |

**And Revelation's opening frames itself the same way:**

> **Revelation 1:1** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "The revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave him to show to his servants **the things that must soon take place**."
> **Revelation 1:3** — **VERIFIED**: "Blessed is the one who reads aloud the words of this prophecy… **for the time is near**."

> ### ❗❗ **THIS IS DIRECT VERBAL OPPOSITION, NOT A THEMATIC RESEMBLANCE. Daniel is commanded to seal BECAUSE the time of the end is not yet. John is commanded NOT to seal BECAUSE the time is near.**
>
> ### **AT MINIMUM: REVELATION PRESENTS ITSELF AS STANDING MUCH CLOSER TO ITS FULFILMENT HORIZON THAN DANIEL DID.**

**⚠️ WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH:** that every event in Revelation occurred immediately · the SCOPE of "near" · the referent of "soon."

> ### **The books themselves contrast REMOTENESS and NEARNESS. That is the finding — and it becomes the FIRST MAJOR CONTROL when Revelation opens.** *(Formalized as the Seal–Nearness Control, §17.)*

## 14. Yeshua's Use of Daniel — the First-Century Anchor

> **Matthew 24:15** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "So when you see **the abomination of desolation spoken of by the prophet Daniel**, standing in the holy place (**let the reader understand**)…"

**Then Matthew 24:21's unprecedented tribulation — Daniel 12:1's language.**

> ### **Yeshua is not quoting Daniel ornamentally. He REACTIVATES Daniel's time-of-end imagery in relation to His own audience's crisis, and tells them to RECOGNIZE it. A very significant first-century anchor.**

### And Luke translates the imagery historically — VERIFIED

> **Luke 21:20–22** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "But when you see **Jerusalem surrounded by armies**, then know that **its desolation has come near**… for these are **days of vengeance, to fulfill all that is written**."

> ### 🔑 **AN ENORMOUS SYNOPTIC CONTROL. Matthew gives "abomination of desolation"; LUKE GIVES "JERUSALEM SURROUNDED BY ARMIES." That strongly supports a historical Jerusalem-siege referent for at least part of the Daniel/Matthew desolation language.**

**⚠️ "to fulfill all that is written" — CONTEXT MATTERS. This is not "all prophecy everywhere." But Luke plainly interprets Jerusalem's crisis in scriptural-fulfilment terms.**

### 🆕 And Luke 21:24 contains an "until" that must not be over-read

> **Luke 21:24** — **VERIFIED**: "Jerusalem will be trampled underfoot by the Gentiles, **until the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled**."

> ### **The UNTIL-REVERSAL CAUTION (Stage 26) applies: this guarantees the trampling THROUGH that point; it does not by itself establish what follows.** **AND it may be evidence of STAGING inside Luke 21 itself — which bears directly on q106. Recorded, not resolved.**

## 15. ⚖️ Where This Leaves the Chronology

**The chain:**

> **Daniel → Temple/desolation → Yeshua applies Daniel to the Jerusalem crisis → Luke: Jerusalem surrounded by armies → "this generation"** *(Luke 21:32)*

> ### **VERY STRONG EVIDENCE FOR SUBSTANTIAL FIRST-CENTURY FULFILMENT.**

**And the unresolved core:**

> ### ❗ **HOW MUCH OF DANIEL 12'S RESURRECTION HORIZON IS INCLUDED IN THAT SAME FULFILMENT?**

### ✅ Strong
- Daniel 12 is deeply tied to **Israel, Temple crisis, distress, and time-of-end language**
- Yeshua **applies Danielic desolation/distress language** to a crisis His generation is told to recognize
- Luke **identifies Jerusalem's encirclement** as desolation/vengeance fulfilment
- Daniel 12's **resurrection language appears bodily and final-sounding**, and the chapter bookends it (12:2 and 12:13)

### ❗ Therefore a first-century reading of Daniel 12 must do ONE of:
1. **demonstrate staged fulfilment**
2. **demonstrate prophetic compression** *(with the Brake — evidence required, not convenience)*
3. **explain resurrection in another textually defensible way**

> ### **IT CANNOT SIMPLY DECLARE THE WHOLE CHAPTER FULFILLED IN AD 70 WITHOUT ADDRESSING RESURRECTION.**

**Does Daniel 12 itself contain signs of temporal staging? Possibly — the 1,290/1,335 sequence · Daniel's "rest and stand at the end" · the distance between the immediate trouble and the final standing. NEEDS DEEPER ANALYSIS. Not asserted.**

## 16. 🏷️ And This Clarifies the Label Question Further

> ### **A standard PARTIAL-PRETERIST reading can say: Daniel/Matthew's Jerusalem-judgment elements were fulfilled in the first century, while final bodily resurrection remains future.**
>
> ### **THAT IS TEXTUALLY EASIER than a model in which Revelation 20's first resurrection AND millennium are also past.**

**The author's Post-Millennial Little-Season Hypothesis therefore goes beyond standard partial preterism — exactly as clarified at Stage 30 §9 — and needs ADDITIONAL evidence from Revelation to justify a past millennium.**

### The specific test that follows

**If Revelation 20's first resurrection is bodily resurrection of martyrs, and the millennium is already past, then there must have been a historical first resurrection prior to or at the start of the proposed millennial period — reconcilable with Daniel 12 · John 5 · Luke 20 · 1 Corinthians 15 · 1 Thessalonians 4.**

**If Revelation instead DEFINES the first resurrection differently, that definition must come FROM REVELATION.**

> ### ❗❗ **EITHER WAY: REVELATION HAS TO DO THE WORK. WE MAY NOT PREDEFINE "FIRST RESURRECTION" TO SAVE THE HYPOTHESIS.** *(This is q98 sharpened.)*

## 17. New Methodological Rule — Seal–Nearness Control

> **When one prophetic text explicitly commands SEALING because fulfilment lies ahead, and a later text invoking the same prophetic tradition explicitly FORBIDS sealing because the time is near, treat the contrast as SIGNIFICANT TEMPORAL EVIDENCE.**
>
> ### **Determine the SCOPE of the nearness FROM THE LATER TEXT before overriding it with an external chronology.**

**Warrant: Daniel 12:4, 12:9 against Revelation 1:1, 1:3, 22:10 — verified verbal opposition, with the reason stated on both sides.**

> ### **Note what the rule does NOT license: it does not settle how much is near, or what "soon" refers to. It establishes that the nearness claim is the LATER TEXT'S OWN, and must be read before an outside chronology is imposed on it.**

## 18. ✍️ Manuscript Voice — Daniel Put Resurrection Beside Jerusalem

> **Daniel 12 created exactly the difficulty I had hoped the methodology would not allow me to avoid. The chapter placed resurrection language beside a crisis that Yeshua later connected directly to Jerusalem. Daniel spoke of a time of unparalleled distress, the shattering of the holy people, the removal of the regular offering and the abomination of desolation. Yeshua told His own generation to recognize Daniel's desolation; Luke translated the warning into Jerusalem surrounded by armies. The first-century anchor was difficult to deny.**
>
> **But Daniel placed sleepers awakening from the dust in the same prophetic horizon. He ended by telling Daniel himself that he would rest and later stand in his allotted place at the end of the days. I could not preserve the first-century judgment merely by deleting the resurrection from the chapter.**
>
> **The result was not a conclusion but a sharper burden. Either Daniel compressed events separated in history, or resurrection unfolded in stages, or I was misunderstanding the chronological relationship entirely. Any interval had to be earned from the texts rather than inserted because my hypothesis needed one.**
>
> **Then another contrast emerged. Daniel was told to seal the book because the time of the end still lay ahead of him. Revelation would later command the opposite: do not seal the prophecy, because the time is near. For the first time, the path into Revelation felt not merely permissible but demanded by the text itself.**

## 19. 🔬 Researcher's Commentary

**The seal contrast turned out to be stronger than expected in two ways.** First, **Daniel states it TWICE** — 12:4 and 12:9 — bracketing the closing section. Second, **the opposition is verbal, not thematic**: seal / do not seal, with a stated timing reason attached to each. **This is not a resemblance being noticed; it is two texts using the same command in opposite directions for opposite stated reasons.** After thirty stages of refusing to build on similarity, it is worth naming what makes this different.

**A correction ran against the hypothesis's favour.** The *rabbim* datum has been carried since Stage 27 as "recorded, not argued from." **This stage makes explicit why it can never be more than that: biblical "many" can function as a broad collective, so it does not establish "not all."** The inference — *Daniel's "many" might be Revelation's first-resurrection group* — **is attractive precisely because it would help. It is now formally blocked (q110).**

**The anachronism check on 12:4 mattered.** *"Many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall increase"* is routinely modernized into travel and information technology. **Hebrew: יְשֹׁטְטוּ, rendered "range far and wide," and הַדָּעַת with the definite article — sitting immediately after the sealing command and immediately before Daniel says he does not understand.** The contextual reading (searching the book, understanding the prophecy) is at minimum a live competitor, and **the technological reading has not been earned.**

**Daniel 12:8 is the quiet finding.** *"I heard, but I did not understand."* **The prophet who received the vision did not grasp its chronology, and the answer he received was not an explanation but a restatement that the words were sealed.** For a project thirty-one stages into building a chronology, that verse is worth more than most of the numbers around it.
