# Side Branch 35½ — "If They Say, He Is in the Wilderness" — and the Provenance of the Investigation

> ### **Two things arrived together and belong in TWO DIFFERENT EVIDENTIARY CATEGORIES. The first is not evidence for the thesis at all — it is the PROVENANCE of the investigation. The second is a real textual observation, banked until the relevant chronology is reached.**

# PART ONE — Researcher's Provenance: A Question That Took Twenty-Five Years

## 1. What this section is, and what it is NOT

> ### ❗ **IT MAY NOT BE CONVERTED INTO: *"I have been thinking about Revelation for twenty-five years, therefore my interpretation is correct."* THAT WOULD VIOLATE EVERYTHING THIS PROJECT HAS BUILT.**

**But as intellectual autobiography it matters enormously — because the finished work should not pretend somebody sat down one afternoon with an eschatological position and assembled proof texts for it.**

## 2. The recollection, recorded with its own uncertainty preserved

**Roughly 2001.** The author was in college and living in Washington, DC. A classmate — **Corey, who went by "Lord Creator," making hip hop at the time** — asked whether he had read Revelation.

**He had not. He says he barely knew what Revelation was.**

**Then he read it.**

⚠️ **The author himself could not pin the date precisely and worked through several personal markers aloud trying to. THAT UNCERTAINTY IS PRESERVED RATHER THAN SMOOTHED — approximately 2001, ~25 years before this investigation, is what the record should say.**

## 3. What followed was NOT twenty-five years of continuous scholarship

**And saying so plainly is part of the honesty:**

> **questions → life → faith → periods of greater and lesser engagement → things learned → things forgotten → convictions challenged → Scripture revisited → more questions**

**In the author's own framing:** hot and cold · spiritual milk before spiritual meat · **but always coming back to it.**

## 4. 🔑 On the tools — stated exactly, in both directions

> ### **THE LANGUAGE MODELS CHANGED WHAT BECAME PRACTICALLY POSSIBLE.** Comparing passages · preserving corrections · inspecting Greek and Hebrew · chasing intertexts · maintaining an epistemological framework · testing hypotheses · keeping an archive of this size at this speed. **Pretending otherwise would be dishonest.**
>
> ### **AND: THE TOOLS ACCELERATED THE INVESTIGATION. THEY DID NOT MANUFACTURE THE JOURNEY THAT PRODUCED THE QUESTIONS.**

**Both halves are needed. The first without the second overclaims for the author; the second without the first overclaims against the tools.**

## 5. And this is exactly why provenance matters methodologically

**When a passage suddenly returns to memory — Jonah · Daniel · Acts 16 · the two at the mill · Zechariah · something taught years ago · a verse that cannot quite be located — THAT IS NOT EVIDENCE MERELY BECAUSE IT WAS REMEMBERED.**

**This project's own record shows all three outcomes:**

| | Worked instance |
|---|---|
| **Recollection was wrong** | "Satan is called the son of perdition" — **John 17:12 is JUDAS** (34½) |
| **Recollection was partly wrong** | the Sukkot/rain objection — **real text, misremembered details, and a missing identity step** (Stage 30) |
| **Recollection was astonishingly relevant** | **Jonah's "belly of Sheol"** (32½) · **the two at the mill** (30½) · **Revelation 20:3's nations** (27½) |

> ### **THE MEMORIES GENERATE QUESTIONS. THE METHODOLOGY JUDGES THEM.**

## 6. ⚠️ And a brake on the beautiful verse itself

> **1 Corinthians 1:27–31** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "God chose **what is foolish in the world to shame the wise**; God chose what is weak in the world to shame the strong… **so that no human being might boast in the presence of God**… '**Let the one who boasts, boast in the Lord**.'"

> ### ❗ **NOTE THE STATED PURPOSE CLAUSE: *so that no human being might boast.* THE VERSE IS ABOUT BOASTING-PREVENTION.**
>
> ### **IT DOES NOT MEAN: lack of scholarship = superior interpretation. NOR: scholars = people Yah intends to shame.**

**And Proverbs points the other way on effort:**

> **Proverbs 2:1–6** — **VERIFIED**: "if you call out for insight and raise your voice for understanding, **if you seek it like silver and search for it as for hidden treasures**, then you will understand the fear of the Lord… **For the Lord gives wisdom.**"
> **Proverbs 4:5–7** — **VERIFIED**: "**Get wisdom; get insight**… The beginning of wisdom is this: **Get wisdom**, and whatever you get, get insight."

> ### **THE BIBLICAL POSTURE IS THEREFORE: LEARN RELENTLESSLY WITHOUT ALLOWING LEARNING TO BECOME GROUNDS FOR BOASTING.** Both clauses. Neither cancels the other. *(Note Proverbs 2:6 holds them together — you SEARCH like for hidden treasure, AND the LORD GIVES.)*

## 7. 🔑 NEW EPISTEMOLOGICAL RULE — Prompting ≠ Proof

> **A thought, recollection, intuition, dream, conviction, or perceived spiritual prompting may LEGITIMATELY GENERATE a research question — but it does NOT establish the answer. The proposed connection must subsequently survive the same textual, contextual, intertextual, historical, lexical and falsification controls applied to every other hypothesis.**

> ### **This leaves Yah completely free to have prompted something WITHOUT making divine prompting an evidentiary shortcut.**
>
> ### **IT MEANS THE THOUGHTS DO NOT HAVE TO BE SUPPRESSED — AND DO NOT HAVE TO BE CANONIZED. BRING THEM TO THE TEXT.**

**Consistent with the project's founding epigraph (ADR-0001): *do not quench the Spirit* — and *test everything; hold fast what is good.* Neither clause is permitted to cancel the other.** **Added to [[00b-biblical-epistemology]].**

# PART TWO — Side Branch 35½: The Localized-Messiah Warning

## 8. The text

> **Matthew 24:23–27** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "if anyone says to you, '**Look, here is the Christ!**' or '**There he is!**' do not believe it. For **false christs and false prophets will arise and perform great signs and wonders**, so as to lead astray, if possible, even the elect. **See, I have told you beforehand.** So, if they say to you, '**Look, he is in the wilderness**,' **do not go out**. If they say, '**Look, he is in the inner rooms**,' **do not believe it**. For **as the lightning comes from the east and shines as far as the west, so will be the coming of the Son of Man**."

## 9. What it establishes

**Someone says: he's HERE · he's THERE · he's hiding in the WILDERNESS · he's inside a private INNER ROOM. Yeshua says: DON'T CHASE THOSE CLAIMS.**

> ### 🔑 **HIS FOLLOWERS WERE APPARENTLY NOT SUPPOSED TO IDENTIFY THE *PAROUSIA* BY SOMEBODY GIVING THEM MESSIAH'S PHYSICAL COORDINATES.**
>
> ### **PHYSICAL-LOCATION CLAIMS ARE NOT RELIABLE EVIDENCE OF HIS PROMISED COMING. The contrast Yeshua draws is with lightning visible east to west — something no one has to be TOLD about.**

## 10. 🆕 And verse 28 closes a loop from Side Branch 30½

> **Matthew 24:28** — **VERIFIED**: "**Wherever the corpse is, there the vultures will gather.**"

> ### ❗ **THAT IS THE SAME LINE LUKE 17:37 GIVES AS THE ANSWER TO THE DISCIPLES' QUESTION "WHERE, LORD?" AFTER THE ONE-TAKEN-ONE-LEFT SAYINGS.**
>
> ### **In Luke it answers "where?"; in Matthew it follows immediately after the *parousia* statement. THE SAME SAYING SITS IN BOTH CONTEXTS — which is a real datum for q105 (are the taken/left sayings the same event as Paul's gathering?) and it was NOT visible until Matthew 24:23–28 was retrieved as a block.**

**Open question q142.**

## 11. This reconnects to the cloud study

**Daniel 7: the Son of Man on the clouds approaches the ANCIENT OF DAYS — heavenly movement.** **Revelation 5: the slain-yet-standing Lamb approaches the throne and receives the scroll.** **Revelation 3:21: "I also conquered and SAT DOWN with my Father on his throne."**

**The developing picture:**

> **resurrection → exaltation → heavenly enthronement → authority → judgment/coming language**

> ### **Considerably more sophisticated than "Yeshua leaves earth, waits somewhere for millennia, physically descends, and then Revelation starts." THE TEXT IS FORCING APART SEVERAL CONCEPTS THAT ARE OFTEN COLLAPSED.**

## 12. "Coming" ≠ automatically "changing physical location"

**Isaiah 19:1** — Yah **riding on a swift cloud and coming to Egypt** — established earlier as divine judgment rather than requiring Yah to become visibly localized in Egypt. **Daniel 7** — cloud movement TOWARD Yah. **Matthew 24** — Son-of-Man coming language. **Revelation** — repeatedly "I am coming."

> ### **THEREFORE "COMING = PHYSICAL RELOCATION FROM POINT A TO POINT B" CANNOT SERVE AS AN AUTOMATIC DICTIONARY DEFINITION. Sometimes physical arrival may indeed be meant. CONTEXT DECIDES.** *(Coming-Language Control, Stage 33.)*

## 13. 🔑 NEW RULE — Anti-Inversion Brake

**The danger runs both ways.** Futurist literalism might say *coming ALWAYS means physically descending to a geographical location.* **We correctly reject that as an automatic rule — and could then accidentally create the opposite rigid rule: *coming NEVER involves physical presence.***

> ### **SAME METHODOLOGICAL ERROR. DIFFERENT CONCLUSION. WE WON'T DO THAT.**

> **ANTI-INVERSION BRAKE: Correcting an overgeneralized interpretation does NOT justify adopting its categorical opposite. If "coming" does not ALWAYS mean physical relocation, that does not establish that it NEVER does.**

**Companion to the Anti-Replacement Principle (disproving A does not establish B) — this is its rigid-dictionary form.**

## 14. ⚠️ AND THE INFERENCE THAT MUST NOT BE DRAWN

**The proposal floated: *therefore the millennial reign must have occurred somewhere physically inaccessible to ordinary humanity.***

> ### ❗❗ **MATTHEW 24 DOES NOT ESTABLISH THAT.**
>
> ### **WHAT IT ESTABLISHES IS NARROWER AND MORE INTERESTING: physical-location claims are not reliable evidence of His promised coming.**
>
> ### **WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH: the location or mode of the millennial reign · that Messiah would be physically hidden somewhere · Rupes Nigra · that the *parousia* was invisible.**

## 15. The prior questions that must come first

**Before asking *where was the millennium?*:**

| # | Prior question |
|---|---|
| 1 | **What does Revelation 20 actually say happens during the thousand years?** |
| 2 | **Who participates?** |
| 3 | **Are the participants described bodily, spiritually, resurrected, transformed, heavenly, earthly, or some combination?** |
| 4 | **What exactly does "reigned with Messiah" mean there?** |
| 5 | **🔑 Does Revelation 20 specify the GEOGRAPHICAL LOCATION of that reign?** |

> ### ❗ **IF REVELATION DOESN'T TELL US WHERE, WE CANNOT FILL THAT BLANK MERELY BECAUSE A PROPOSED GEOGRAPHY WOULD SOLVE THE PROBLEM.**
>
> ### **That is where the Rupes Nigra hypothesis must remain disciplined. Ancient maps or traditions could potentially become historical evidence for WHAT PEOPLE BELIEVED about geography. THEY CANNOT SUPPLY A LOCATION REVELATION NEVER IDENTIFIES unless an independent evidentiary chain establishes the connection.** *(See [[07-deferred-historical-register]] H9, H12.)*

## 16. "Inaccessible" is not one category — it is at least six

**Suppose Revelation 20 eventually indicates the saints existed in some condition inaccessible to ordinary people. That still would not necessarily mean GEOGRAPHICALLY inaccessible.**

**Logically distinct possibilities:** spatial separation · heavenly/earthly separation · spiritual visibility/invisibility · transformed bodily condition · symbolic visionary representation · ordinary geographical separation · **or something not yet anticipated.**

> ### **REVELATION MUST NARROW THESE. Open question q143.**

## 17. ⚖️ But one thing already keeps the hypothesis interesting

**Revelation 20:8 says Satan goes out to deceive "the nations that are at the four corners of the earth."**

> ### **WHATEVER WE ULTIMATELY CONCLUDE, NATIONS APPARENTLY EXIST TO BE DECEIVED.**

**So a simplistic model — *millennium = Messiah and resurrected saints are the only humans remaining anywhere* — immediately encounters a problem the text itself creates.**

> ### 🔑 **THAT IS WHY THE EARLIER REALIZATION MATTERED (Side Branch 27½): the intuition that ordinary human populations may coexist with the millennial reign IS NOT MERELY EXTERNAL SPECULATION. REVELATION 20 ITSELF WILL FORCE THE RELATIONSHIP TO BE INVESTIGATED. THAT IS DIFFERENT.**

## 18. How this observation is recorded — in its disciplined form

### **Localized-Messiah Warning**

**Matthew 24 explicitly warns Yeshua's disciples not to authenticate claims about His *parousia* merely because someone reports Messiah at a secret or localized earthly place — "wilderness," "inner rooms," "here," or "there."**

| | |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Establishes** | physical-location claims are NOT reliable evidence of His promised coming |
| ❌ **Does NOT establish** | the location or mode of the millennial reign |
| ❌ **Does NOT establish** | that Messiah would be physically hidden somewhere |
| ❌ **Does NOT establish** | Rupes Nigra |
| ❌ **Does NOT establish** | that the *parousia* was invisible |

## 19. ✍️ Manuscript Voice — The Questions Predated the Tools

> **At some point I realized that this investigation had not begun when I acquired the tools capable of conducting it. My memory placed its beginning roughly twenty-five years earlier, when someone asked whether I had read Revelation and I had barely known what he meant. I read it, but I did not understand where that reading would eventually lead. What followed was not twenty-five years of continuous scholarship. It was a much less orderly history of belief, doubt, lessons, mistakes, periods of spiritual milk and periods of heavier food, passages remembered imperfectly, and questions that refused to disappear.**
>
> **The language models changed what became practically possible. I could suddenly pursue intertexts, lexical questions, historical claims and competing interpretations at a scale I could not reasonably have maintained alone. But they had not lived the years that generated the questions. And my having lived those years did not make my answers authoritative either. That distinction became important: experience could generate the investigation without governing its conclusions.**
>
> **The same was true of thoughts that seemed suddenly to return to memory. Some had produced remarkable connections; others had required correction. I therefore did not need to decide beforehand whether every recollection had been prompted by the Spirit or produced by my own associative memory. I could receive the question without granting it authority. Prompting was not proof. The thought could enter the investigation, and Scripture could be allowed to keep it, refine it, or kill it.**
>
> **That posture made unusual questions less dangerous rather than more dangerous. I no longer needed to fear asking them because I was no longer obligated to defend them.**

> ### 📌 **THAT LAST SENTENCE BELONGS VERY CLOSE TO THE METHODOLOGICAL HEART OF THE UNABRIDGED RESEARCH EDITION.**

## 20. 🔬 Researcher's Commentary

**Two categories arrived in one breath, and separating them is the whole exercise.** The twenty-five-year provenance is **not evidence** — and it is **not worthless either**. It explains why the questions exist without licensing any answer. **Recorded as intellectual autobiography, filed away from the argument.**

**The brake on 1 Corinthians 1:27 was worth applying to a verse the author invoked about himself.** Retrieval surfaced the **stated purpose clause** — *"so that no human being might boast"* — which is boasting-prevention, **not a claim that ignorance interprets better.** And Proverbs pushes the other way: *seek it like silver · search as for hidden treasure · get wisdom.* **Proverbs 2:6 holds both: you search, and the LORD gives.**

**Matthew 24:28 was the unplanned find.** *"Wherever the corpse is, there the vultures will gather"* is **the same saying Luke 17:37 uses to answer "Where, Lord?"** after the one-taken-one-left sayings. **Visible only because 24:23–28 was retrieved as a block rather than stopping at the lightning verse.** It feeds q105 directly.

**And the inference was declined at its most attractive moment.** The proposal — *the millennial reign must have been somewhere physically inaccessible* — arrived immediately after a genuine textual observation, which is exactly when an unearned extension is hardest to notice. **Matthew 24 establishes that location-claims don't authenticate the *parousia*. It says nothing about where the millennium was.** The **Anti-Inversion Brake** exists because the correction itself could have become a new rigid rule.

**Not retrieved (q144):** Isaiah 19:1 *(verified in earlier stages)* · Revelation 20:8 verbatim *(verified at Side Branch 27½)* · Luke 17:37 Greek *(sōma vs ptōma)* · Matthew 24:28 Greek.
