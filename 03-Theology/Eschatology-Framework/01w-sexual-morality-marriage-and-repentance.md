# Side Study 37½ — Sexual Morality, Marriage, and Repentance

> # 🔒 **PRIVATE — UNABRIDGED RESEARCH RECORD ONLY**
>
> ### **Preserved in full per ADR-0010. NOT manuscript voice. The personal provenance stays in this file; only findings the author later chooses may travel into any published edition.**

## 1. Why this branch exists, and where it came from

**Trigger: Revelation 9:20–21.** The trumpets end with survivors who **did not repent** — of murders, sorceries, **sexual immorality**, thefts, and the worship of idols.

> ### ❗ **AT THAT POINT THE INVESTIGATION STOPPED BEING MERELY ESCHATOLOGICAL — because the author turned the interpretive method INWARD, rather than treating *porneia* as an abstract category applied to other people.**

**Recorded in the author's own framing:** he wants the study to arrive not only at the original hypothesis but at **where he has been following Scripture incorrectly, and what needs to change** — because *"obedience and lack of repentance is the recurring theme in many ways."*

> ### **THAT IS THE METHODOLOGY DOING WHAT IT WAS BUILT FOR. If it is worth anything, eventually it has to be allowed to investigate US, not only Revelation.**

---

## 2. The question, correctly framed

**NOT:** *am I a bad person because I have desired women?*
**NOT:** *were the women treated kindly?*

> ### **BUT: WHAT SEXUAL BOUNDARIES DOES YAH ACTUALLY ESTABLISH, AND WHERE DOES MY CONDUCT STAND RELATIVE TO THEM?**

**That framing is better because it prevents BOTH self-condemnation AND self-justification from deciding the answer beforehand.**

---

## 3. 🔑 A rule the author supplied himself — and it governs this study

> **SOMEONE ELSE'S FAILURE TOWARD ME DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY REDEFINE WHAT OBEDIENCE REQUIRES FROM ME.**

**He stated it before any text was consulted, against his own interest. It stands as this branch's governing control.**

## 5. Vocabulary that is currently blended — and must be untangled

| Greek | Sense |
|---|---|
| **μοιχεία — *moicheia*** | adultery — particularly violation of the marriage bond |
| **πορνεία — *porneia*** | sexual immorality — **broader and more difficult** |
| **πόρνη — *pornē*** | prostitute |
| **πόρνος — *pornos*** | sexually immoral person, with contextual nuance |

**Plus the Torah's own categories and prohibitions, which are not identical to later English Christian vocabulary.**

> ### **TWO SHORTCUTS BOTH REFUSED: assuming *porneia* simply means "sex before marriage" — AND assuming that if an act is not technically adultery, Scripture therefore permits it.**

**Open question q150.**

---

## 6. ✅ Adultery IS narrower than sexual immorality — the intuition is partly right

**Adultery particularly concerns violation of the marriage bond.** And **Yeshua intensifies the examination** (Matthew 5:27–28): a man looking at a woman **in order to lust after her** has already committed adultery in his heart.

> ### 🚧 **THAT DOES NOT MEAN NOTICING AN ATTRACTIVE WOMAN = ADULTERY. Sexual attraction is not identical to the deliberate cultivation of lust.**
>
> ### **Scripture does NOT command anyone to become incapable of recognizing or experiencing sexual attraction. THE QUESTION IS WHAT IS DONE WITH THE DESIRE. That is different.**

*(⚠️ Matthew 5:27–30 not retrieved this pass — q154.)*

---

## 7. 🔑 Prostitution — and Paul gives a DIRECT test, not a generic word study

> **1 Corinthians 6:13, 15–16, 18–20** — ESV, **partially retrieved**: "**The body is not meant for sexual immorality, but for the Lord**"… "your bodies are **members of Christ**?"… "**The two will become one flesh**"… "**Flee from sexual immorality**"… "your body is a **temple of the Holy Spirit** within you"… "**you were bought with a price. So glorify God in your body.**"

**Paul's objection is NOT that she is being mistreated · NOT that it is illegal · NOT that she is being cheated financially. HIS OBJECTION CONCERNS THE SEXUAL UNION ITSELF — and he grounds it in Genesis: the two become one flesh.**

> ### ❗❗ **THEREFORE: EVEN IF THE WOMAN IS AN ADULT, CONSENTS, IS TREATED COMPASSIONATELY, AND THE TRANSACTION IS LAWFUL WHERE IT OCCURS — PAUL STILL TREATS THE SEXUAL JOINING AS MORALLY SIGNIFICANT.**
>
> ### **KINDNESS CANNOT TRANSFORM AN ACT SCRIPTURE PROHIBITS INTO AN OBEDIENT ACT.**
>
> ### **AND CONVERSELY: RECOGNIZING THE ACT AS SIN DOES NOT REQUIRE DEHUMANIZING THE WOMAN INVOLVED. THOSE ARE INDEPENDENT MORAL QUESTIONS.**

**This is the branch's strongest preliminary finding. ⚠️ Requires full verbatim re-retrieval and sequential exegesis — q154.**

## 8. ⚠️ Marriage — the "not in a church" question does NOT resolve as hoped

**A genuine study is needed: Scripture does not present modern church ceremonies, government licences, rings, or contemporary wedding customs as the universal mechanism by which marriage comes into existence.**

> ### 🚧 **BUT BE EXTREMELY CAUTIOUS ABOUT CONCLUDING "we did not marry in a church, therefore it was not a marriage before Yah."**
>
> **He regarded her as his wife. She regarded him as her husband. A household and family were established.** Whether every aspect met biblical ideals is a **separate** question — **but the absence of a church wedding alone is NOT a strong biblical basis for concluding no marriage existed.**

**Open question q151 — what biblically CONSTITUTES marriage?**

---

## 9. 🔑 Her conduct and his must be investigated SEPARATELY

**Suppose, for argument, the author's assessment of his wife's conduct is entirely correct — withheld affection, poor treatment, abandonment of the shared home, failure in marital responsibilities.**

**Those questions matter. They answer: WHAT WAS HER RESPONSIBILITY?**

> ### ❗ **THEY DO NOT ANSWER: WHAT SEXUAL CONDUCT BECAME PERMISSIBLE FOR HIM AFTERWARD. That is a separate question — exactly as the author himself recognized.**

**To be examined in sequence:** Matthew 5 · Matthew 19 · Mark 10 · Luke 16 · Romans 7 · 1 Corinthians 7 · and the relevant Torah material.

> ### **BECAUSE SEPARATION · DIVORCE · ABANDONMENT · ADULTERY · REMARRIAGE · AND SEXUAL RELATIONS ARE NOT INTERCHANGEABLE BIBLICAL CATEGORIES. Open question q152.**

## 10. Paul's "better to marry than burn" — and what it does NOT say

**1 Corinthians 7:9: if they cannot exercise self-control, they should marry, for it is better to marry than to burn with passion.** *(Not retrieved — q154.)*

> ### **PAUL'S PROPOSED OUTLET FOR UNCONTROLLED SEXUAL DESIRE IS MARRIAGE. NOT PROSTITUTION UNTIL MARRIAGE BECOMES PRACTICAL.**

**The author's own half-joking question — *"what am I supposed to do, ask the prostitute to marry me?"* — actually EXPOSES the category distinction rather than defeating it.**

> ### **Paul is not saying "marry anybody so that sex becomes technically permissible." MARRIAGE IS A COVENANTAL UNION, NOT A PERMISSION SLIP ATTACHED TO SEXUAL ACCESS.** **His sexual ethic does not reduce to *libido + legal spouse = problem solved*.**

---

## 11. 🔑 Solomon CANNOT function as permission — and Scripture removes the structure itself

> **Deuteronomy 17:17** — ESV, **VERIFIED**, of Israel's king: "**he shall not acquire many wives for himself, LEST HIS HEART TURN AWAY**, nor shall he acquire for himself excessive silver and gold."

**And 1 Kings 11 presents Solomon's 700 wives and 300 concubines as part of his DOWNFALL — his wives turned his heart toward other gods.** *(Not retrieved — q154.)*

> ### ❗❗ **THE SEQUENCE IS ALMOST CHILLING:**
>
> **COMMAND: the king must not multiply wives, LEST HIS HEART TURN AWAY.**
> **SOLOMON: multiplies wives.**
> **RESULT: his heart turns away.**
>
> ### **SO IF SOLOMON'S SEXUAL ABUNDANCE HAD BEEN FUNCTIONING AS A PERMISSION STRUCTURE IN THE CONSCIENCE, SCRIPTURE ITSELF REMOVES THAT STRUCTURE.**

**And that is exactly what this investigation was built to be capable of doing: CORRECTING US RATHER THAN PROTECTING US.**

---

## 12. ⚠️ "I did it with love" does not settle the moral category

**The appeal to 1 Corinthians 13 is understandable and does not work exegetically.** When Paul says gifts, knowledge and sacrifice without love amount to nothing, **he is not teaching that love makes otherwise prohibited conduct righteous.**

**In Paul's own definition, love "does not rejoice at wrongdoing, but rejoices with the truth."**

> ### **BIBLICAL LOVE AND BIBLICAL OBEDIENCE CANNOT ULTIMATELY BE MADE OPPONENTS.**
>
> ### **"WAS I LOVING?" IS A LEGITIMATE MORAL QUESTION. SO IS "WAS THE ACT ITSELF PERMITTED?" ONE CANNOT ERASE THE OTHER.**

---

## 13. 🔑 The 144,000 "virgins" — and why Stage 36's restraint now matters personally

**The concern arose directly: does Revelation 14:4's virginity language mean those who have had sex occupy an inferior position?**

> ### ❗ **REVELATION DOES NOT SAY THAT. And this is precisely why the identification was NOT closed prematurely at Stage 36 (q148).**

**And Scripture supplies a hard constraint:**

> **Hebrews 13:4** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "**Let marriage be held in honor among all, and let the marriage bed be undefiled.**"

> ### **THEREFORE SCRIPTURE CANNOT COHERENTLY TEACH THAT HAVING SEX ITSELF CONTAMINATES A PERSON. Whatever Revelation 14 means by virginity, IT MUST COEXIST WITH SCRIPTURE'S AFFIRMATION OF FAITHFUL MARITAL SEXUALITY. That already constrains the possible interpretations.**

### ⚠️ And "defiled with women" must be handled carefully

**Revelation 14:4 does NOT teach that women contaminate men.** If literal virginity is intended, we would need to understand **why that particular group is consecrated that way.** If symbolic purity is intended, **we must establish it from Revelation's own imagery.** If both operate together, **we need evidence.**

> ### ❗ **WE MAY ABSOLUTELY NOT DERIVE "women/sex = dirty." Genesis begins with sexual union inside the male-female marriage relationship and commands fruitfulness. SOMETHING SUBTLER IS HAPPENING IN REVELATION 14. Open question q153.**

## 14. 🔑🔑 The question underneath everything — and Revelation 9's actual criterion

**Stated plainly by the author: *I don't want to be separated from Yah because of what I've done.***

> ### ❗ **NOTICE WHAT REVELATION 9 ACTUALLY CONDEMNED: *THEY DID NOT REPENT.* THAT IS DIFFERENT FROM *THEY HAD COMMITTED SINS.***
>
> ### **If Revelation's criterion were "anyone who has committed sexual sin is irretrievably condemned," THEN REPENTANCE WOULD BE IRRELEVANT. Revelation specifically emphasizes their REFUSAL to turn.**

**And Paul makes it explicit.** 1 Corinthians 6:9–10 gives a severe list including the sexually immoral and adulterers — **and then 6:11 says:**

> **"you were washed, you were sanctified, you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ."** — **VERIFIED**

**Preceded by the phrase the author most needs to see, which the fragmentary retrieval did not return and which must be re-verified (q154): *"AND SUCH WERE SOME OF YOU."***

> ### **WERE. THESE ARE NOT PEOPLE WHOSE BIOGRAPHIES CONTAIN NO SEXUAL SIN. THEY ARE PEOPLE WHOSE FORMER CONDUCT DOES NOT GET THE FINAL WORD.**

## 15. Repentance is not pretending the past did not happen

**The author's own statement is very close to the biblical posture:**

> **"I don't want to deny what I actually did, because then I can't repent of it."**

> ### **EXACTLY. Repentance does not require rewriting *"actually, I never enjoyed it."* If something was enjoyed, pretending otherwise is not repentance.**

**Three failures, all refused:**

| ❌ | |
|---|---|
| **"I treated everyone kindly, therefore it was not sinful"** | kindness is not permission |
| **"Actually I never enjoyed it"** | falsification is not repentance |
| **"I sinned sexually, therefore Yah wants nothing more to do with me"** | contradicts the very framework Revelation 9 assumes |

> ### **THE BIBLICAL MOVEMENT: TRUTH → CONFESSION → REPENTANCE → CHANGED DIRECTION → CONTINUED OBEDIENCE.**
>
> ### **AND SOMETIMES THE DIFFICULT PART IS PRECISELY: *I still desire something I have concluded I should not do.* OBEDIENCE IS NOT ONLY OBEDIENCE WHEN DESIRE DISAPPEARS FIRST.**

---

## 16. 🔑 Three things that are NOT the same moral event

> ### **ATTRACTION → CULTIVATED FANTASY → SEXUAL CONDUCT**

**Paul's language of SELF-CONTROL implies something important: DESIRE CAN REMAIN PRESENT WHILE BEHAVIOUR CHANGES. Otherwise self-control would not be necessary.**

**So the response is NOT:**

> ~~"I must become a person who never finds women sexually attractive."~~

**It is:**

> ### **"I DO NOT USE THE EXISTENCE OF THE DESIRE AS PERMISSION TO SATISFY IT IN WHATEVER WAY IS AVAILABLE."**
>
> ### **A VERY DIFFERENT TASK — and an achievable one, which the other framing is not.**

**⚠️ And the pornography alternative does not resolve it either.** If the question is obedience, *"I could use pornography instead"* substitutes one outlet for another. **Yeshua's teaching on intentionally lustful looking and Paul's sexual ethic require investigating the DESIRE → ATTENTION → FANTASY → CONDUCT sequence, not merely relocating the endpoint.**

## 17. Personal provenance — recorded because the pattern is real, not as excuse or accusation

**Long-conditioned association among sexual desire, early exposure to pornography, hurt, loneliness, touch, and eventually transactional sex.** Exposure came young, through peers, before first sexual experience; an early relationship ended in betrayal; a pattern followed.

> ### ⚠️ **UNDERSTANDING THAT HISTORY MAY EXPLAIN WHY TEMPTATION HAS PARTICULAR FORCE. IT DOES NOT CONDEMN AND IT DOES NOT EXCUSE.**
>
> ### **DO NOT CONVERT BIOGRAPHY INTO EITHER AN EXCUSE OR AN ACCUSATION. That brake is stated because both directions are available and both are wrong.**

**Also recorded:** present circumstances — separated roughly two years, living abroad, prostitution locally legal, no current relationship being formed, and deliberate avoidance of looking at women as an attempted discipline. **Legality is explicitly NOT being treated as permission by the author himself.**

**And a genuinely good sign, recorded as such:** he reports resisting far more than before, and the resistance is *not* motivated primarily by fear of punishment.

## 18. 🔑 Post-baptism conduct — a real question, answered by NEITHER reassurance NOR condemnation

**Baptism reported around 2012–2013; sexual conduct afterward. This raises a distinct theological question that must NOT be resolved by a frightening phrase detached from its context.**

> ### ❗ **DO NOT CONCLUDE TONIGHT: "I sinned after baptism, therefore baptism failed or I have exhausted Yeshua's mercy."**

**The New Testament was written TO COMMUNITIES OF BAPTIZED BELIEVERS who still had to be warned, corrected, told to confess, to repent, to forgive one another, and to practise self-control.**

> ### **AND SIMULTANEOUSLY: GRACE IS NOT PERMISSION TO DELIBERATELY CONTINUE SOMETHING ONCE ONE BECOMES CONVINCED IT IS SIN.**
>
> ### **BOTH SIDES HAVE TO SURVIVE THE STUDY.**

**To examine in sequence:** Romans 6 · 1 Corinthians 5–7 · Hebrews 4, 6, 10, 12 · 1 John 1–3 · and Yeshua's teaching in context. **Distinguish: confession · repentance · grace · sanctification · deliberate persistence · stumbling · apostasy.**

> ### **DO NOT INFER FROM "SIN AFTER BAPTISM" EITHER AUTOMATIC LOSS OF SALVATION OR AUTOMATIC HARMLESSNESS. Open question q153.**

**The author described Yeshua as truly great, perfect in both being and example — and explicitly said he was not saying it merely so that he would be spared.**

> **Hebrews 4:15–16** — ESV, **VERIFIED**: "we do not have a high priest who is unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but one who **in every respect has been tempted as we are, yet without sin**. **Let us then with confidence draw near to the throne of grace, that we may receive mercy and find grace to help in time of need.**"

> ### ❗ **NOTE THE LOGIC: YESHUA'S HAVING BEEN TEMPTED YET WITHOUT SIN IS GIVEN AS THE REASON TO APPROACH — NOT THE REASON TO HIDE.**

> ### **HIS PERFECTION IS SIMULTANEOUSLY THE STANDARD ADMIRED AND THE GROUND ON WHICH THE STRUGGLING PERSON IS TOLD TO DRAW NEAR.**
>
> ### **The Christian answer to repeated temptation is NOT meant to be merely terror that the King will punish. "MERCY AND GRACE TO HELP IN TIME OF NEED" IS THE STATED PURPOSE.**

## 21. 🔬 Researcher's Commentary

**This branch exists because the method was turned inward, and that was the author's own decision.** Revelation 9 named sexual immorality among the things its survivors would not repent of — and rather than reading that as a description of other people, **he asked whether his own understanding of marriage, adultery, prostitution, lust, separation and repentance had been biblically sound.**

**Two structures were removed rather than defended.** **Solomon** had been functioning, by the author's own account, as a kind of permission structure — and **Deuteronomy 17:17 dismantles it in the king's own case, with 1 Kings 11 recording precisely the outcome the command warned about.** And the appeal to **love** could not convert a prohibited act into an obedient one, because Paul's own definition of love *"does not rejoice at wrongdoing."*

**One structure was NOT removed, and it matters.** *"We did not marry in a church"* is not a strong biblical basis for concluding no marriage existed — **which makes the question harder, not easier, and the author was not permitted the easier answer in either direction.**

**And the single most important observation is Revelation 9's own criterion.** It condemns those who **did not repent** — not simply those who had sinned. **A person asking "where am I wrong, and what needs to change?" is in a different posture from the one Revelation 9 describes, where judgment comes, truth confronts, and they refuse to turn.** That is a textual observation, not reassurance.

The insight may eventually belong in the book; the sexual history does not have to.**
