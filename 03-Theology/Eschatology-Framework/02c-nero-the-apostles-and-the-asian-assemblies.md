# QUESTION 1 — When did Nero's persecution actually occur?

## 🟡 STATUS: PARTIALLY ANSWERED — primary source retrieved, and it is LESS helpful to the hypothesis than expected

**Tacitus, *Annals* 15.44 — retrieved (English translation, Church & Brodribb via Wikisource; ⚠️ NOT the Latin):**

> **"…all human efforts, all the lavish gifts of the emperor, and the propitiations of the gods, did not banish the sinister belief that THE CONFLAGRATION WAS THE RESULT OF AN ORDER. Consequently, to get rid of the report, NERO FASTENED THE GUILT AND INFLICTED THE MOST EXQUISITE TORTURES on a class hated for their abominations, called CHRISTIANS by the populace. CHRISTUS, from whom the name had its origin, SUFFERED THE EXTREME PENALTY DURING THE REIGN OF TIBERIUS AT THE HANDS OF ONE OF OUR PROCURATORS, PONTIUS PILATUS, and a most mischievous superstition, thus checked for the moment, again broke out not only in JUDAEA, the first source of the evil, but even in ROME…"**

**Tacitus places the fire itself at *Annals* 15.38–43. The passage continues with executions including crucifixion and burning.**

### ✅ What this genuinely establishes

| | |
|---|---|
| **A persecution of Christians under Nero is attested by a non-Christian Roman historian** | not a Christian legend requiring Christian sources to sustain it |
| **Its trigger was the fire and the rumour that Nero ordered it** | a specific, datable political event |
| **Messiah's execution under Pilate in Tiberius's reign is independently attested** | a Tier B/C anchor for the whole timeline |
| **The movement had reached Rome itself by this point** | relevant to Stage 25's "had the message already gone out?" |

### 🛑 And now the three things that CONSTRAIN us — which is why the branch was worth running

> ### ❗❗ **1. TACITUS GIVES NO DURATION AND NO END DATE.**
>
> **He describes the persecution's OCCASION and its CRUELTY. He does not say how long it lasted. THE 42-MONTH TEST CANNOT BE SATISFIED FROM TACITUS.** **The commonly cited "64 to 68" is built by pairing the fire with Nero's death — that is a CONSTRUCTED interval, not a reported one.**
>
> ### 🛑 **THIS IS EXACTLY THE ROUNDING q170 FORBIDS. We must not take a persecution with an attested START and an UNATTESTED END, attach the emperor's death as its terminus because that yields roughly three and a half years, and then present the result as historical convergence.**

> ### ❗ **2. TACITUS DESCRIBES A PERSECUTION *AT ROME*.**
>
> **His account is set in the city, arising from a city fire, punishing a class blamed for it. HE DOES NOT DESCRIBE AN EMPIRE-WIDE ADMINISTRATIVE PROGRAM.**
>
> ### **REVELATION 13:7 SAYS AUTHORITY WAS GIVEN THE BEAST "OVER EVERY TRIBE AND PEOPLE AND LANGUAGE AND NATION," AND 13:16 SAYS "ALL, BOTH SMALL AND GREAT, BOTH RICH AND POOR, BOTH FREE AND SLAVE."** **⚠️ Stage 40 §7 already refused to shrink that phrase to fit a candidate. THAT REFUSAL NOW HAS A PRICE, AND WE PAY IT HERE RATHER THAN QUIETLY REVISING IT: the gap between a Rome-centred persecution and Revelation 13's scope language is a REAL OPEN PROBLEM for the Nero identification, not a detail.**

> ### ❗ **3. TACITUS IS NOT A CONTEMPORARY WITNESS.**
>
> **He wrote roughly half a century after the events. That does not discredit him — he is a serious source, hostile to Christians, which strengthens rather than weakens his testimony about their existence and punishment. BUT HE IS TIER C, NOT TIER A, AND MUST BE LABELLED SO.**

### 📌 Net result for the hypothesis

> ### **NERO'S PERSECUTION IS HISTORICALLY REAL AND WELL ATTESTED. ITS DURATION IS NOT. ITS GEOGRAPHICAL EXTENT IN THE PRIMARY SOURCE IS NARROWER THAN REVELATION 13'S LANGUAGE.**
>
> ### **THE BRANCH DID WHAT IT WAS BUILT TO DO: IT CONSTRAINED RATHER THAN CONFIRMED.**

### Still required for Question 1

**The LATIN of *Annals* 15.44 · Suetonius *Nero* 16 and 38 · whether any source attests a persecution CONTINUING to Nero's death · the precise date of the fire · whether any source attests Neronian persecution OUTSIDE Rome · Pliny the Younger's letter to Trajan (later, but bears on how imperial action against Christians actually worked).** *(q172.)*
