## 4. 🔑🔑 The structural datum that constrains everything: WOMAN ≠ BEAST

**17:3 VERIFIED: "a woman SITTING ON a scarlet beast."** ✅ **17:7: "the beast… THAT CARRIES HER."** ✅

> **"the ten horns that you saw, THEY AND THE BEAST WILL HATE THE PROSTITUTE. They will make her DESOLATE AND NAKED, and DEVOUR HER FLESH and BURN HER UP WITH FIRE."**

> ### ❗❗ **SHE RIDES IT. IT LATER DESTROYS HER. SHE IS NOT SIMPLY IDENTICAL TO IT.**
>
> ### **THEREFORE ANY READING OF *woman = Rome* AND *beast = Rome* MUST EXPLAIN WHY ROME RIDES ROME AND THEN DESTROYS ROME.** **That is possible if these are distinguishable aspects of one imperial system — but it is NOT automatic, and it must be argued rather than assumed.**

> ### ⚠️ **CORRESPONDENCE, NOT IDENTITY. But unlike an arbitrary theory, this relationship has historical plausibility: the same Roman power on which Judean leadership depended is the power that destroyed the city.**

> **Perhaps one candidate will eventually win. Perhaps John's symbolism deliberately overlays covenant apostasy and imperial power. But Revelation itself has now given us the next test: identify the beast without using Babylon to identify it, then return and see which woman can actually ride it.**

**This was the cleanest falsification test the project has run, and it produced no winner. That is the correct outcome, and it was not engineered.**

**Three disciplines held under pressure.** **First, the retrieval's verse-numbering drift was reported rather than silently corrected — a citation that is right by luck is not verification. Second, the *epi tēs gēs* ambiguity was flagged against the project's own strongest datum: "all who have been slain ON EARTH" may read "in the land," and this project has spent forty stages on exactly that ambiguity, so it does not get to benefit from the looser rendering now. Third, the Composite-Symbol Possibility was written WITH its leash — if a candidate needs "it's composite" to survive its worst datum, it is failing, not layering.**

**The most valuable structural finding is that Babylon cannot be solved alone. She rides the beast and the beast destroys her. Identify the beast independently, and the woman is constrained. THE TEXT FORCED OUR BRANCHES BACK TOGETHER — and it did so without letting either one bootstrap the other.**
