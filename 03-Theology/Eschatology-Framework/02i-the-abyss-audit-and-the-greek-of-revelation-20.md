| | |
|---|---|
| **ἵνα** | so that / in order that |

**μὴ … ἔτι naturally conveys NOT ANY MORE / NO LONGER.**

## ✍️ Manuscript Voice — "Might" Wasn't in the Greek

> **A single English word nearly created another false branch. Revelation said Satan was bound "so that he might not deceive the nations," and the word "might" initially sounded as though it could leave open whether the restraint actually succeeded. But the Greek did not contain an independent equivalent of that English modal at all. John used a purpose construction: *hina mē planēsē eti ta ethnē* — so that he no longer deceive the nations. The English translator needed grammar; I had almost turned the translator's grammar into theology.**
>
> **The source language sharpened both sides of the problem. Satan was not merely inconvenienced. He was seized, bound, cast into the abyss, shut in, sealed over, and later described as released from his prison. Yet Revelation also told me exactly which function mattered to the vision: the deception of the nations ceased during that confinement and resumed when he was released.
