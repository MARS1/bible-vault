## 10. 📊 THE FOUR MODELS

### **MODEL A — BODILY FIRST RESURRECTION BEFORE THE MILLENNIUM**
> Revelation means what it strongly appears to mean: the faithful dead are bodily raised before the thousand-year reign.

**Fits:** *ezēsan* · *anastasis* · "the rest of the dead" later · the state distinction from Revelation 6.
**Challenge for a past millennium:** ❗ **WHERE AND WHEN DID THIS HAPPEN HISTORICALLY?**

### **MODEL B — HEAVENLY VINDICATION / ENTHRONEMENT RESURRECTION**
> "First resurrection" is symbolic for martyrs entering heavenly reign after death.

**Advantage:** historically much easier.
**Problems:** ⚠️ **Revelation 6 already shows slain souls conscious · the SAME *ezēsan* is used of the rest of the dead · explicit *anastasis* terminology · the 1 Cor 15 / 1 Thess 4 bodily framework.**

> ### **AT PRESENT MODEL B IS UNDER CONSIDERABLE TEXTUAL PRESSURE.**

### **MODEL C — BODILY/TRANSFORMED RESURRECTION PLUS LIVING GATHERING**
> Revelation 20 focuses on the dead component; Paul supplies the simultaneously transformed living faithful.

**Potentially harmonizes Revelation 20 with 1 Cor 15 + 1 Thess 4.**
**But:** ❗ **the historical burden becomes even larger — living first-century believers would ALSO have undergone extraordinary transformation or removal.**

### **MODEL D — THE MARTYRS REPRESENT A LARGER CORPORATE FAITHFUL POPULATION**
> Revelation 20 highlights martyr witnesses but represents the broader people belonging to Messiah who participate in Paul's resurrection/gathering.

**Possible. Needs the syntactical audit at q212 and symbolic evidence.**

> ### 📌 **NONE SELECTED. But the CURRENT TEXTUAL WEIGHT FAVOURS: *SOME FORM OF ACTUAL RESURRECTION OR CHANGE OF STATE SUBSTANTIALLY STRONGER THAN MERE POSTMORTEM HEAVENLY SURVIVAL.***

**15:42–44 VERIFIED:** ✅

**Greek: σῶμα ψυχικόν versus σῶμα πνευματικόν.** ⚠️ *(Greek reported — q229.)* **BOTH CONTAIN σῶμα, *BODY*.**

> ### **THEREFORE THE REAL ALTERNATIVE IS *NOT* "PHYSICAL CORPSE WALKING NORMALLY" VERSUS "PURELY SPIRITUAL METAPHOR." PAUL SUPPLIES A THIRD CATEGORY: **RESURRECTED, TRANSFORMED EMBODIMENT**. THAT IS LIKELY CRUCIAL.**

> **Paul's σῶμα πνευματικόν remains σῶμα. DO NOT EQUATE "SPIRITUAL BODY" WITH DISEMBODIED SPIRIT — and do not require resurrected saints to behave like the mortal body that entered death.**

**Yeshua after resurrection: APPEARS · DISAPPEARS from immediate perception · ENTERS despite closed settings · EATS · CAN BE TOUCHED · and ultimately ASCENDS.** ⚠️ *(not retrieved.)* **AND PAUL EXPLICITLY MAKES HIM THE FIRSTFRUITS — SO HE IS THE CONTROLLING PATTERN, NOT AN ESCAPE HATCH.**

## 12. 🛑🛑 THE BRAKE THAT KEEPS THIS FALSIFIABLE

**Transformed embodiment means we may not assume resurrected saints would interact with ordinary mortals in ordinary ways. So absence from ordinary records is not automatically fatal to a past first resurrection.**

> ### ❗ **BUT THAT IS NOT ENOUGH TO PROVE IT HAPPENED — AND IT IS EXACTLY THE POINT WHERE A HYPOTHESIS CAN QUIETLY BECOME UNFALSIFIABLE.**

### 🆕 SUPERNATURAL-POSSIBILITY BRAKE

> **A biblical precedent showing that an event COULD occur supernaturally may answer an objection of IMPOSSIBILITY — but it DOES NOT CONSTITUTE EVIDENCE that the proposed historical event ACTUALLY OCCURRED.**

> ### **WE MUST NOT TURN SUPERNATURAL POSSIBILITY INTO AN EVIDENCE-ERASING DEVICE.**

**And the corollary for the coming historical work:**

> ### **ABSENCE OF EVIDENCE HAS WEIGHT ONLY RELATIVE TO THE AMOUNT AND TYPE OF EVIDENCE WE SHOULD REASONABLY EXPECT TO SURVIVE. THAT MUST BE CAREFULLY ARGUED — NOT ASSERTED IN EITHER DIRECTION (q227).**

## 14. ✍️ Manuscript Voice — The Martyrs Were Already Alive

> **The strongest objection to a merely spiritual first resurrection came from Revelation itself. The martyrs did not need chapter 20 in order to exist consciously after death. John had already seen their souls beneath the altar in chapter 6, speaking, receiving robes and being told to wait. Revelation 7 similarly portrayed the faithful before the throne. When chapter 20 later said the slain "lived" and called this "the first resurrection," the language therefore seemed to describe something beyond ordinary postmortem survival.**
>
> **Paul made the possibility stranger rather than simpler. Resurrection did not mean merely returning to ordinary mortality. Messiah was the firstfruits of a transformed order. The dead would be raised incorruptible, the living changed in an instant, and those belonging to Messiah would be gathered to Him. A resurrected body could be genuinely bodily without behaving like the mortal body that entered death.**
>
> **That answered one objection to a past resurrection: I could no longer assume that raised saints would simply resume ordinary public life indefinitely. But it did not provide historical evidence that the resurrection had occurred. Supernatural possibility could explain how an event might escape ordinary expectations; it could not be used to make the hypothesis unfalsifiable.**
>
> ### **THE NEXT QUESTION THEREFORE BECAME UNAVOIDABLE: WERE PAUL'S RESURRECTION AND GATHERING, YESHUA'S GATHERING IN MATTHEW 24, AND JOHN'S FIRST RESURRECTION ACTUALLY DESCRIBING THE SAME EVENT?**
