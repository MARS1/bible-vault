## 3. 🆕 DEFEAT ≠ ABOLITION — and Revelation supplies both halves itself

✅ **Revelation 1:18** — καὶ **ἔχω τὰς κλεῖς τοῦ θανάτου καὶ τοῦ ᾅδου** — "and I **have** the keys of Death and Hades."

✅ **Revelation 20:6** — the second death has no **ἐξουσία** over the first-resurrection participants *(established Stage 45½)*.

✅ **Revelation 20:14** — Death and Hades finally thrown into the lake.

> ### **THREE MOMENTS, RIGIDLY SEPARATE:**
> **1. MESSIAH'S RESURRECTION** — victory and *authority* over Death and Hades. He holds their keys. **They still exist.**
> **2. THE FIRST RESURRECTION** — a specified population becomes immune to the *second* death. **Death itself still exists.**
> **3. THE GREAT WHITE THRONE** — Death and Hades empty out and are destroyed. **Then, and only then, "death shall be no more."**

✅ **And Paul says the same thing independently: 1 Corinthians 15:26 — ἔσχατος ἐχθρὸς καταργεῖται ὁ θάνατος.** Messiah already reigns, already possesses authority — **and Death remains an enemy awaiting abolition.**

> ### 📌 **CONSEQUENCE FOR THE HYPOTHESIS, STATED AT THE PRECISION THE EVIDENCE SUPPORTS — AND IT IS A CONSISTENCY CHECK, NOT A CONFIRMATION:**
>
> **A present-little-season model does NOT encounter the objection "Revelation says Death no longer exists, yet people presently die," because Revelation places the abolition of Death AFTER the little season, not before it.**
>
> ### 🛑 **THAT REMOVES A CONTRADICTION. IT DOES NOT SUPPLY EVIDENCE.** *(Compatibility–Evidence Distinction, v2.6: **COULD FIT ≠ DOES FIT ≠ PROVES**.)* **The observation that people still die is compatible with the hypothesis and equally compatible with the hypothesis being false. It has no discriminating power whatever and must not be quoted as though it did.**

**❗ AND HADES ≠ THE LAKE OF FIRE, PROVEN INTERNALLY:** Hades is *thrown into* the lake (20:14) — it cannot be thrown into itself.
**❗ AND γέεννα APPEARS NOWHERE IN REVELATION.** The book's only term for final judgment is **λίμνη τοῦ πυρός**. English "hell" glosses both γέεννα (Gospels) and the lake of fire (Revelation) and therefore **obscures a distinction Revelation itself maintains.**

✅ **All SBLGNT-verified.**

- **4:15** — ἡμεῖς οἱ ζῶντες οἱ περιλειπόμενοι **εἰς τὴν παρουσίαν τοῦ κυρίου**

- **4:17** — ἔπειτα ἡμεῖς οἱ ζῶντες … **ἁρπαγησόμεθα** ἐν νεφέλαις εἰς ἀπάντησιν τοῦ κυρίου εἰς ἀέρα

**Paul's sequence: faithful dead rise → faithful living joined to them → all with Messiah.**

> ### **AND THE POPULATION IS THE POINT: οἱ νεκροὶ ἐν Χριστῷ — EXPLICITLY RESTRICTED.**
>
> **Revelation 20:11–13's later judgment is NOT so restricted: τοὺς νεκρούς, τοὺς μεγάλους καὶ τοὺς μικρούς — "the dead, great and small."**
>
> ### **A RESTRICTED RESURRECTION OF MESSIAH'S OWN FITS REVELATION'S BLESSED-AND-HOLY FIRST RESURRECTION FAR MORE NATURALLY THAN IT FITS THE COMPREHENSIVE GREAT-WHITE-THRONE JUDGMENT.**
>
> ### 📐 **STATUS: CORRESPONDENCE — STRONG. IDENTITY — NOT ESTABLISHED.** *(Correspondence–Identity Distinction, Stage 42.)*

**⚠️ AND THE BRAKE FROM STAGE 47 STANDS UNCHANGED (q227):** 1 Thessalonians' **πρῶτον** is relative *to the living* — the dead rise *before* the living are caught up, **within that sequence.** It is **NOT** lexical identity with Revelation's **ἡ ἀνάστασις ἡ πρώτη**. Two different Greek words doing two different jobs.

**❗ A NOUN THAT IS NOT THERE:** 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18 contains **no** ἀνάστασις. Paul uses the **verb** ἀναστήσονται. The passage describes a rising; it does not use Revelation's noun.

### ✍️ And 5:1 does not start a new subject — the Greek forbids it

✅ **5:1 opens Περὶ δὲ τῶν χρόνων καὶ τῶν καιρῶν** — **δέ**, a connective. The modern chapter break is an editorial artifact, not a discourse boundary. Paul's resurrection-and-gathering teaching **flows directly into** ✅ 5:2's ἡμέρα κυρίου ὡς κλέπτης ἐν νυκτί and ✅ 5:3's Εἰρήνη καὶ ἀσφάλεια → αἰφνίδιος … ὄλεθρος.

**That matters because Matthew 24 contains the same cluster** — and because this project has already established, at Stage 33's Coming-Language Control, that ✅ **Revelation 3:3 uses thief-coming language conditionally to a single assembly.** **A thief-coming is not automatically the final consummation.**

|---|---|---|---|

## 5. 🔑🔑 1 CORINTHIANS 15 — Paul puts the reign BETWEEN the resurrection and Death's defeat

✅ **All SBLGNT-verified. The sequence markers are the finding.**

| v. | Greek | Element |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | **ἀπαρχὴ** τῶν κεκοιμημένων | Messiah the firstfruits |
| 23 | ἕκαστος δὲ ἐν τῷ ἰδίῳ **τάγματι**· ἀπαρχὴ Χριστός, **ἔπειτα** οἱ τοῦ Χριστοῦ **ἐν τῇ παρουσίᾳ αὐτοῦ** | *own order*; **then** those of Messiah at His parousia |
| 24 | **εἶτα τὸ τέλος**, ὅταν παραδιδῷ τὴν βασιλείαν … | **then** the end |
| 25 | **δεῖ** γὰρ αὐτὸν **βασιλεύειν ἄχρι οὗ** θῇ πάντας τοὺς ἐχθροὺς ὑπὸ τοὺς πόδας αὐτοῦ | he **must reign until** |
| 26 | **ἔσχατος ἐχθρὸς καταργεῖται ὁ θάνατος** | the last enemy — Death |

> ### ❗ **PAUL USES *TWO DISTINCT SEQUENCE WORDS* — ἔπειτα (23) AND εἶτα (24) — NOT ONE.** The tranche treated τάγμα as the only ordering mechanism. **It is not.** The passage is explicitly staged, in Paul's own vocabulary, before any harmonization with Revelation is attempted.

> ### **PAUL DOES NOT SAY THE RESURRECTION OF MESSIAH'S OWN AND THE DESTRUCTION OF DEATH OCCUR AT THE SAME MOMENT.** His syntax reads naturally as: **Messiah's people raised at the parousia → a reign during which enemies are subjected → Death destroyed last → the end.**
>
> ### **THAT IS STRUCTURALLY COMPATIBLE WITH: FIRST RESURRECTION → MESSIAH AND THE SAINTS REIGN → DEATH AND HADES FINALLY REMOVED.**
>
> ### 📐 **STATUS: ARCHITECTURAL CORRESPONDENCE — STRONG. IDENTITY — NOT ESTABLISHED. COMPATIBILITY IS NOT DEMONSTRATION.**

### ❗ καταργεῖται IS PRESENT, NOT FUTURE — recorded as an OBSERVATION

✅ **καταργεῖται is present passive indicative.** Paul did not write the future καταργηθήσεται. On a plain reading he says Death **is being abolished**, not **will be abolished**.

> ### ⚠️ **RECORDED AS AN OBSERVATION ONLY, NOT AS AN ARGUMENT.** Whether a Greek present here is durative, futuristic, gnomic, or a present-of-certainty **requires a grammar authority this project does not possess** (S14 precedent — *we do not parse disputed syntax ourselves*). ❗ **A theological conclusion built on this tense before that authority is obtained would be exactly the class of claim ADR-0010 forbids.**

**And it produces a translation divergence with theological weight — `ENGLISH-SPECIFIC`:** ESV holds the present ("The last enemy to be destroyed **is** death"); ✅ **CJB shifts it to future — "will be done away with."**

### 🛑 AND THE PROJECT'S OWN RULE CONSTRAINS THE PROJECT'S OWN ATTRACTIVE READING

The tranche leans on **ἄχρι οὗ** ("until") in 15:25 to build reign-interval-then-terminus. **This project already wrote the rule that limits that move**, at Stage 26, *before* it could matter here:

> **⏳ UNTIL-REVERSAL CAUTION (v2.5):** *a statement that something continues "until" a specified point guarantees continuation THROUGH that point. It does NOT by itself establish cessation or reversal afterward.*

> ### ❗ **APPLIED HONESTLY: ἄχρι ESTABLISHES THAT MESSIAH REIGNS UP TO THE SUBJECTION OF ALL ENEMIES. IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH THAT THE REIGN THEN STOPS — AND THIS PROJECT HAS ALREADY FOUND INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE THAT IT DOES NOT (q218: ✅ Revelation 22:5 — "they will reign FOREVER AND EVER," against 20:6's thousand years).**
>
> ### **THE INTERVAL IS PERMITTED BY THE GRAMMAR. THE TERMINUS IS NOT SUPPLIED BY ἄχρι.** *The rule was written two years of stages before the conclusion it now restrains, which is the only reason it can be trusted here.*

**⚠️ AND δεῖ IS WEAK EVIDENCE, SAID PLAINLY.** ✅ δεῖ appears at 15:25 and ✅ at Revelation 20:3 (δεῖ λυθῆναι). **δεῖ is among the most common verbs in the Greek New Testament.** Shared occurrence of an extremely common word is **not** a meaningful lexical link, and presenting it as one would be the *Equivalent-Scope Burden* error in a new costume. **Same for ἄχρι.** ❌ **Neither is offered as evidence here.**

## 7. 🔑 MATTHEW 24:29–31 — what it says, and what it conspicuously does not

✅ **All SBLGNT-verified.**

**24:29** — **Εὐθέως δὲ μετὰ τὴν θλῖψιν τῶν ἡμερῶν ἐκείνων** … ὁ ἥλιος σκοτισθήσεται … αἱ **δυνάμεις τῶν οὐρανῶν** σαλευθήσονται
**24:30** — τὸ **σημεῖον** τοῦ υἱοῦ τοῦ ἀνθρώπου … ἐρχόμενον **ἐπὶ τῶν νεφελῶν** τοῦ οὐρανοῦ
**24:31** — καὶ **ἀποστελεῖ τοὺς ἀγγέλους αὐτοῦ μετὰ σάλπιγγος μεγάλης**, καὶ **ἐπισυνάξουσιν τοὺς ἐκλεκτοὺς αὐτοῦ** ἐκ τῶν τεσσάρων ἀνέμων
**24:34** — οὐ μὴ παρέλθῃ **ἡ γενεὰ αὕτη** ἕως ἂν **πάντα ταῦτα** γένηται

### ❗ THE LOAD-BEARING NEGATIVE, SEARCHED EXHAUSTIVELY AND CONFIRMED

**Matthew 24:29–31 contains NO resurrection vocabulary of any kind.** Each of the following was searched for and is **ABSENT**: **ἀνάστασις · ἐγείρω · ἀνίστημι · νεκροί · μνημεῖα · ἐξανάστασις · θάνατος**.

> ### **MATTHEW GIVES A GATHERING. PAUL GIVES A RESURRECTION *AND* A GATHERING. THE RESURRECTION COMPONENT IS PAUL'S, NOT MATTHEW'S.**
>
> ### 🛑 **WE MAY NOT MAKE PAUL'S DETAILS APPEAR INSIDE MATTHEW BECAUSE IT WOULD BE CONVENIENT. If the identity is to be established, it must be established — not imported.**

### ❗ AND THE TRANCHE'S STRONGEST LEXICAL CLAIM DOES NOT SURVIVE RETRIEVAL

The tranche's §12 argued: *"Matthew and Paul don't merely share an English word 'coming.' They share **παρουσία**. That's much stronger."*

✅ **Every occurrence of παρουσία in Matthew 24 was retrieved: 24:3 · 24:27 · 24:37 · 24:39. FOUR — the tranche implied three.**

> ### 🛑 **παρουσία DOES NOT OCCUR IN MATTHEW 24:29–36 AT ALL. IT IS ABSENT FROM THE GATHERING PERICOPE, ABSENT FROM THE TRUMPET, AND ABSENT FROM THE GENERATION STATEMENT.**
>
> **It brackets the passage (24:27 before, 24:37 after). It does not appear inside it.**
>
> ### **SO THE παρουσία LINK BETWEEN *MATTHEW'S GATHERING* AND *PAUL'S PAROUSIA-GATHERING* IS NOT DIRECT. IT IS AN INFERENCE FROM CONTEXTUAL FRAMING — WHICH MAY WELL BE CORRECT, AND IS NOT THE SAME THING AS SHARED VOCABULARY AT THE VERSE IN QUESTION.**

### ❗ A TRANSLATION ARTIFACT AT THE TRUMPET — `ENGLISH-SPECIFIC`

✅ **SBLGNT reads simply μετὰ σάλπιγγος μεγάλης — "with a great trumpet."** **ESV prints "with a loud trumpet call."** ❗ **There is no word for "call" in the Greek**, and no *φωνῆς* in this reading.

## 8. 🔑🔑 THE FINDING THAT CAME FROM CORRECTING OURSELVES — Matthew's OWN use of ἐπισυνάγω

### ⚠️ FIRST, THE SELF-CORRECTION

✅ **Matthew 24:31 — ἀποστελεῖ τοὺς ἀγγέλους αὐτοῦ … καὶ ΕΠΙΣΥΝΑΞΟΥΣΙΝ** — from **ἐπισυνάγω**.

> ### **DIFFERENT VERBS.**
>
> ### ✅ **BUT STAGE 11'S CONCLUSION SURVIVES — ON BETTER EVIDENCE THAN IT CITED.** The **SENDING** clause is near-verbatim in both: **ἀποστελεῖ … τοὺς ἀγγέλους αὐτοῦ.** That parallel carries the *contextually-more-probable-supernatural* verdict perfectly well. **The verdict was right; one of its supporting reasons was wrong.**

### 🔑 AND NOW THE FINDING

**If ἐπισυνάγω is NOT Matthew's generic gathering verb, then where else does Matthew use it?**

> ### ❗❗ **EXACTLY ONCE MORE — MATTHEW 23:37, ONE CHAPTER BEFORE THE OLIVET DISCOURSE:**
>
> ✅ **Ἰερουσαλὴμ Ἰερουσαλήμ … ποσάκις ἠθέλησα ἐπισυναγαγεῖν τὰ τέκνα σου, ὃν τρόπον ὄρνις ἐπισυνάγει τὰ νοσσία αὐτῆς ὑπὸ τὰς πτέρυγας**
>
> ### **MATTHEW'S ONLY OTHER USE OF THIS VERB IS YESHUA'S LAMENT OVER *JERUSALEM* — A COVENANTAL, PROTECTIVE, CITY-DIRECTED GATHERING, SPOKEN IMMEDIATELY BEFORE HE LEAVES THE TEMPLE AND BEGINS MATTHEW 24.**

**This is Intratextual Evidence — point 4 of the 12-point structure — and it is the strongest kind, because it is the author's own usage rather than an imported category.**

> ### 📌 **WHAT IT DOES:** it materially **strengthens** the tranche's own §14 objection — that Matthew 24:31's gathering may be **covenantal restoration** rather than resurrection. **The tranche raised that objection and could not settle it. Matthew's own vocabulary now speaks to it.**

> ### 🛑 **WHAT IT DOES NOT DO:** it does **not** settle it. 23:37 and 24:31 differ in agent (Yeshua Himself / His messengers), in object (Jerusalem's children / the elect from the four winds), and in outcome (refused — *οὐκ ἠθελήσατε* / accomplished). **Two occurrences of a verb in one Gospel is a datum, not a semantic rule** — and building a dictionary from it is precisely what the Contextual Symbolism rule (46½) forbids.

> ### ⚖️ **NET EFFECT ON THIS STAGE'S QUESTION: THE COVENANTAL-GATHERING READING OF MATTHEW 24:31 IS NOW BETTER SUPPORTED THAN IT WAS AT STAGE 11 — AND THAT CUTS AGAINST IDENTIFYING MATTHEW'S GATHERING WITH PAUL'S RESURRECTION-GATHERING.**

## 9. 🛑 "LAST TRUMPET" IS NOT "SEVENTH TRUMPET" — and the seventh trumpet points somewhere else

**The tranche was right to refuse the ordinal arithmetic, and this stage upholds the refusal.**

✅ **1 Corinthians 15:52 — ἐν τῇ ἐσχάτῃ σάλπιγγι.** ✅ **Revelation 11:15 — ὁ ἕβδομος ἄγγελος ἐσάλπισεν.**

**2. ✅ 12:48 ATTACHES THE LAST DAY TO JUDGMENT, NOT RESURRECTION** — ὁ λόγος … **κρινεῖ** αὐτὸν ἐν τῇ ἐσχάτῃ ἡμέρᾳ.

>

## 14. 🔬 Researcher's Commentary — how this stage was actually earned

**The order of operations mattered, and it was nearly wrong.**

**1. The audit came before the ingestion.** The tranche arrived carrying an instruction to correct the archive's Death/Hades chronology. The instinct was to comply — the correction was *itself correct as a statement about Revelation*. Checking the files first revealed the archive already held the right sequence in four places. **Complying would have "corrected" four correct files into agreement with an error that existed only in a conversation.** This is the first live demonstration that the *verify-against-the-files-not-the-report* rule pays for itself.

**2. Retrieval was delegated in parallel, and two agents had to be overruled.** One rated an unretrieved LXX comparison at "9/10 — highly probable"; that was downgraded to NOT VERIFIED, because probability is not retrieval and *"highly probable"* is the exact phrasing by which contextually-favored becomes lexically-established. Another declared "nothing significant" that the tranche had missed at Revelation, having failed to notice that Revelation 11:18 and 20:12 share both the small-and-great merism and the judging of the dead. **A retrieval agent is a retriever. The comparison across retrievals is not delegable, and the stage's two best findings both came from that comparison rather than from any single report.**

**3. The failed explanation, preserved because the failure is the finding.** The attractive move was to build the Matthew–Paul identity on shared *parousia*. Retrieval killed it: four occurrences, none inside 24:29–36. **The bracket is real and may still matter; the shared-vocabulary claim at the disputed verse is simply false.** Recorded because it was believed, argued, and then refuted by the text — and because the weaker inferential version (*contextual framing*) is genuinely still available, which is exactly when the distinction is easiest to lose.

**4. The self-correction that paid.** Stage 11's "the same gathering verb" was wrong. Chasing the actual verb produced Matthew 23:37 — the finding that most sharpens the stage's central question, and it points *against* the project's preferred reading. **A correction made honestly returned better evidence than the error had supplied.**

**5. What was refused.** *δεῖ* and *ἄχρι* appear in both 1 Corinthians 15:25 and Revelation 20:3. It would have been easy to present that as a lexical bridge. **Both words are extremely common; the "link" is noise, and it is refused here in writing so it cannot quietly return in a later stage as though it had been established.**
