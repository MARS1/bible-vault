**❗ A subtlety inside the intertext:** ✅ **MT Zechariah 12:12 reads מִשְׁפָּחוֹת (*mishpachot*, FAMILIES/CLANS), not שְׁבָטִים (tribes). φυλάς is the LXX's OWN RENDERING CHOICE.** **Matthew inherits the Greek word, not a Hebrew "tribes."**

✅ **LXX Zechariah 12:10 — ἐπιβλέψονται πρὸς μὲ ἀνθ' ὧν ΚΑΤΩΡΧΗΣΑΝΤΟ** ("because they MOCKED / danced in triumph over")

✅ **Matthew 23:36 — ἈΜΗΝ ΛΕΓΩ ΥΜΙΝ, ΠΑΝΤΑ ΤΑΥΤΑ ἥξει ἐπὶ ΤΗΝ ΓΕΝΕΑΝ ΤΑΥΤΗΝ**

> ### ❗ **BUT A RETRIEVAL AGENT DREW THE WRONG INFERENCE FROM IT — that 1:17 makes the "race/people" reading of 24:34 less burdensome. ❌ NON SEQUITUR. GENEALOGICAL-COUNTING-UNIT IS NOT ETHNIC-RACE. 1:17 BROADENS THE RANGE; IT DOES NOT SUPPLY *THAT* ESCAPE ROUTE.**

## 11. 🆕 THE INTERTEXTUAL EXPANSION RULE — adopted, and its leash is part of it

**The tranche proposes:**

**ADOPTED — the underlying observation is sound, and the stage's own evidence supports it: Isaiah 27 supplies Matthew's trumpet without supplying his gathering verb, which is exactly what "same image, developed vocabulary" looks like.**

> ### 🛑 **BUT IT ARRIVES WITH TWO LEASHES, WITHOUT WHICH IT IS POISON — the same shape as the Composite-Symbol Possibility's leash (Stage 43) and Spiral Progression's (Stage 44):**
>
> ### **LEASH 1 — ANTI-RESCUE: IF A READING NEEDS "IT IS BOTH" TO SURVIVE ITS WORST DATUM, THAT IS A FAILING CANDIDATE, NOT A LAYERED ONE.** A rule permitting both readings simultaneously can absorb any counterevidence, and a rule that explains every arrangement explains none.
>
> ### **LEASH 2 — IT MAY NOT RUN INTERTEXTUAL PRIORITY BACKWARDS.** ❗ This project's Rule 2 already states: *"never run it backwards — a LATER text developing an earlier one is not evidence about what the EARLIER one meant."* **THEREFORE PAUL MAY *ELABORATE* MATTHEW; PAUL IS NEVER *EVIDENCE FOR WHAT MATTHEW MEANT*. The expansion runs forward only.**

**📌 The joining must be DEMONSTRATED, text by text. The rule licenses the question, never the answer.**

## 13. ✍️ Manuscript Voice — The Date Held. The Event Did Not Follow.

> **I expected this stage to be where the argument either broke or closed. It did neither, and what it actually did was more useful than either.**
>
> **Matthew's own chronology held under the microscope, and it held on evidence I had not been leaning on. I had been leaning on "immediately." The word that actually carried it was one chapter earlier: the same solemn formula, the same "all these things," the same "this generation," spoken over the scribes and Pharisees standing in front of Him, where nobody disputes who is meant. Matthew had already used the sentence once in a setting that admits no escape, and then used it again three verses after the gathering.**
>
> **And the Greek behind the cosmic language turned out to be closer than I had dared claim. The moon clause in Matthew is Isaiah's moon clause with one noun changed. "The powers of the heavens" is Isaiah's phrase exactly. Both oracles are about Babylon and Edom — real cities, real judgments, described as if the sky were coming apart. I had been saying the prophets talk like this. I can now show it in the same language Matthew wrote in.**
>
> **But the thing I most wanted did not arrive. Isaiah's great trumpet is verbally Matthew's great trumpet — and Isaiah's gathering is not verbally Matthew's gathering. Deuteronomy's is not either. Both say* synagō*; Matthew says *episynagō*. The one place Matthew uses that verb again, he is standing over Jerusalem grieving that she would not be gathered.**
>
> **So I have a date I did not have this morning, and I still do not have the event. Matthew tells me when Matthew's gathering happens. He does not tell me it is the moment the dead in Messiah rise. That sentence is Paul's, and I have not yet earned the right to lay it over this one.**

## 14. 🔬 Researcher's Commentary — three failures worth more than the successes

**1. The correction ran in both directions on the same day.** The tranche upgraded a contextual correspondence into a lexical pair (§0). This project's own summary upgraded *"identity not established"* into *"non-identity adopted"* (§0). **Same failure class, same twelve hours, both parties. Recorded together deliberately, because a discipline that only ever catches the other side is not a discipline.**

**2. A fabricated Greek parallel nearly entered the archive.** A retrieval agent placed **ἥξουσιν** inside Matthew 24:31 — a word that is not there — and graded it VERBAL, inside a table whose other rows were genuine. **Formatting vouched for it.** It was caught only by reading the table against the verse rather than against itself. **Two further mislabels from the same lane and the next (συνάγω graded "identical" to ἐπισυνάγω; ἐπιβλέπω graded "same root" as ὁράω) were caught the same way.** The methodology's new *Delegated Retrieval Is Retrieval, Not Judgement* rule was written one stage ago and paid for itself immediately.

**3. A clean rule was built and the text destroyed it eighteen verses later.** Matthew 13:30 splits συλλέγω for weeds and συνάγω for wheat, which would have tied 24:31's ἐπισυνάγω to the wheat. **13:48 uses συλλέγω for the good fish and 13:47 uses συνάγω for an indiscriminate net.** **Preserved rather than deleted — the attractive pattern was real for two verses and false for the chapter, and the record of the failed generalization is worth more than the generalization would have been.**

**4. What was refused.** The promotion in §9 is the conclusion this project most wants. **It therefore ships with four falsification conditions, and with the frank admission that the strongest counter-argument — 24:36's περὶ δέ — was not engaged by the tranche and was not exhaustively searched by us.** **A promotion announced without its counter-argument is advocacy.**
