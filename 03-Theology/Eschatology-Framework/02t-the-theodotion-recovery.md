2. ❌ **That "the LXX Daniel" is one text with Theodotion somewhere inside it.** ✅ **CCAT ships them as SEPARATE FILES:** `61.DanielOG.mlxx` and `62.DanielTh.mlxx` — plus `59/60.Bel(OG|Th)` and `63/64.Sus(OG|Th)`.

> ### ❗ **AND THE TELL WAS ALREADY IN THE ARCHIVE, UNREAD: Stage 48½ recorded that BibleHub's Daniel gave *"ὡς παλαιὸς ἡμερῶν παρῆν"* and identified it as OLD GREEK **from the text's own distinctive**. ✅ **RAHLFS PRINTS THEODOTION AS THE MAIN DANIEL TEXT. A BIBLEHUB PAGE GIVING OG WAS ALREADY EVIDENCE THAT A DIFFERENT EDITION TREE WOULD GIVE THEODOTION — AND NOBODY DREW IT.**

> ### 🆕 **THE RULE THIS FORCES, THIRD IN THE FAMILY:** *A failed request is not an unavailable source* (v4.9) · *the tool matters as much as the URL* (S48) · and now — **ASK WHAT *KIND* OF ARTIFACT THE TEXT IS. ## 3. ✅✅ q180 CONFIRMED — and Stage 48½ was right to refuse the agent

**The three witnesses at Daniel 7:13, side by side:**

| Witness | Preposition | The second clause |
|---|---|---|
| **Aramaic (MT)** | **עִם** — *with* | *"he came TO the Ancient of Days and was PRESENTED before him"* |
| **Theodotion** | **μετά** — *with* | ✅ **ἕως τοῦ παλαιοῦ τῶν ἡμερῶν ἔφθασεν … ἐνώπιον αὐτοῦ προσηνέχθη** |
| **Old Greek** | **ἐπί** — *on* | ⚠️ **ὡς παλαιὸς ἡμερῶν παρῆν** — *"and as an Ancient of Days was present"* |

>
> ### ✅ **THEREFORE q180 — that Daniel 7:13's son of man is PRESENTED BEFORE the Ancient of Days rather than descending to earth — IS CONFIRMED BY TWO OF THREE WITNESSES AND WAS NEVER IN REAL DOUBT.**
>
> ### 📌 **AND STAGE 48½'s HANDLING IS VINDICATED: a retrieval agent asserted the Old Greek said "he approaches the throne"; THE ARCHIVE REJECTED THE ASSERTION AND RECORDED THE DIVERGENCE UNRESOLVED RATHER THAN SMOOTHING IT. > ### **SO THE CHAIN IS NOW FULLY MAPPED AND PAUL MATCHES NO LINK IN IT:**
> ### **HEBREW יִתְרוֹמֵם / יִתְגַּדֵּל → OLD GREEK ὑψόω → THEODOTION ὑψόω + μεγαλύνω → PAUL ὑπεραίρω.**

> ### **q275's DOWNGRADE STANDS AND IS NOW EXHAUSTIVE RATHER THAN PROVISIONAL. "PAUL IS QUOTING DANIEL 11:36" IS REFUSED AGAINST *BOTH* GREEK RECENSIONS.**

### ⚠️ AND THE NEAR-MISS, RECORDED SO NOBODY LATER MISTAKES IT FOR A MATCH

✅ **Theodotion 11:36 has ΥΠΕΡΟΓΚΑ** *(ὑπέρογκος — "swollen," "arrogant," of speech)*, and Paul has **ὑπεραιρόμενος**.

> ### ❗ **THEY SHARE THE PREFIX ὑπερ- AND NOTHING ELSE. ὑπέρογκος IS FROM ὄγκος; ὑπεραίρω IS FROM αἴρω. DIFFERENT WORDS, DIFFERENT ROOTS, DIFFERENT PARTS OF SPEECH.**

## 6. ★ THEODOTION VARIES TOO — and it preserves the Hebrew's lexical distinction
