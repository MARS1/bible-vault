> ### **ENOUGH CORRESPONDENCES HAVE ACCUMULATED THAT IT WOULD NOW BE EASY TO SAY "OBVIOUSLY PAUL IS TALKING ABOUT MATTHEW 24." THE QUESTION INSTEAD IS HARDER:**

## 8. 🆕 THE BURDEN-OF-DISTINCTION RULE — adopted, with a leash

> **When two passages independently share an unusually dense combination of VOCABULARY, SOURCE-TEXT BACKGROUND, EVENT SEQUENCE, AUDIENCE HORIZON and THEOLOGICAL FUNCTION, maintaining that they describe DIFFERENT events requires POSITIVE GROUNDS for distinguishing them — not merely the theoretical possibility that they could differ.**

**Why it is legitimate: without it, skepticism becomes unfalsifiable.** *"They could still be different"* can be said of any two passages forever, and a method that permits an unanswerable objection at every step is not a method.

> ### 🛑 **AND ITS LEASH, WHICH IS PART OF THE RULE — because this rule is the most dangerous one this project has adopted:**
>
> ### **1. IT IS NOT "SIMILAR = SAME." It shifts a burden at a DENSITY THRESHOLD; it does not lower the standard of proof.**
> ### **2. THE DENSITY MUST BE *INDEPENDENT*. Correspondences inherited from a shared source (both drawing on Daniel) are ONE datum, not five — and §3 shows the sanctuary link is exactly that kind.**
> ### **3. IT MAY NEVER RETIRE AN ASYMMETRY. §6's three gaps are positive grounds for distinction and remain live.**
> ### **4. IT DOES NOT APPLY TO CORRESPONDENCES THIS PROJECT HAS ALREADY DOWNGRADED — the Daniel–Paul link is conceptual, not lexical (Stage 51 §5), and may not be counted at full weight here (q300).**

## 12. ✍️ Manuscript Voice — The Test I Wanted to Fail

> **By this point I had collected so many resemblances that I no longer trusted myself with them. Every new correspondence felt like confirmation, and that feeling is exactly what the method exists to distrust. So I tried to break it instead.**
>
> **The test was simple and unkind: lay both sequences out and see whether either had to be shoved out of order to fit the other. If Paul's coming had to be slid backward, or Matthew's lawlessness forward, the identity would be in trouble.**
>
> **Nothing had to move. Both run lawlessness, then crisis and deception, then Messiah. Neither runs it backward.**
>
> **And I have to be careful about what that means, because it is less than it feels like. Two different crises built on the same page of Daniel would also come out in the same order. Compatible sequence is what identity requires, not what identity proves.**
>
> **What I actually have is three genuine shared words, one shared prophet standing behind both, an order that refused to break, and three holes — a tribulation Paul never mentions, a sky Paul never darkens, and a man Paul says is destroyed whom Matthew never destroys.**
>
> **The last one is the honest one. Paul kills somebody. Matthew does not. I do not know what to do with that yet, and I would rather write that sentence down than fill the hole with something convenient.**

## 13. 🔬 Researcher's Commentary

**1. A debt was paid before the argument leaned on it.** ✅ Matthew 24:12's ἀνομία had been **REPORTED, NOT VERIFIED** since Stage 50, and it is the lexical spine of this entire stage. **Verifying it first cost one retrieval. Using it unverified would have put a load-bearing claim on a failed fetch — sitting among a dozen verified ones, where nobody would have looked again.**

**2. The stage was designed to fail and did not, which is the weaker of the two good outcomes.** A falsification test that passes tells you less than one that fails. **The passing result is recorded with its own limit attached — *compatible order is necessary, not sufficient* — because a bare "the sequence survives" would read as much stronger than it is.**

**3. The most dangerous thing adopted here is the Burden-of-Distinction Rule, and it was leashed accordingly.** It is genuinely needed — *"they could still be different"* is otherwise an unanswerable objection available at every step, and unanswerable objections are not evidence. ❗ **But it is one inversion away from "similar therefore same," and the leash's second clause does the real work: correspondences inherited from a shared source are ONE datum, not five. §3 shows the sanctuary link is exactly that.**

**4. What was refused.** The three asymmetries could have been dissolved — omission-is-not-contradiction handles two of them cleanly, and the third has plausible candidates sitting right there. ❌ **Paul's destroyed individual has no Matthean counterpart, the candidates were not written down, and the gap stays open.** *Building the bridge out of the gap it is supposed to cross is the specific failure this project has spent fifty stages avoiding.*
