## 3. ⚖️ THE 20:4 SYNTAX — data recorded, q212 NOT ruled on

✅ **Καὶ εἶδον θρόνους, καὶ ἐκάθισαν ἐπ' αὐτούς, καὶ κρίμα ἐδόθη αὐτοῖς, καὶ ΤΑΣ ΨΥΧΑΣ τῶν πεπελεκισμένων διὰ τὴν μαρτυρίαν Ἰησοῦ καὶ διὰ τὸν λόγον τοῦ θεοῦ, ΚΑΙ ΟΙΤΙΝΕΣ οὐ προσεκύνησαν τὸ θηρίον οὐδὲ τὴν εἰκόνα αὐτοῦ καὶ οὐκ ἔλαβον τὸ χάραγμα … καὶ ἔζησαν καὶ ἐβασίλευσαν μετὰ τοῦ χριστοῦ χίλια ἔτη.**

**THE GRAMMATICAL FACTS, AS DATA:**

| Element | Form |
|---|---|
| **τὰς ψυχάς** | ★ **ACCUSATIVE** plural — object of εἶδον |
| **τῶν πεπελεκισμένων** | genitive plural perfect passive participle |
| ★ **οἵτινες** | ★ **NOMINATIVE** plural relative pronoun |
| **article before οἵτινες** | ❌ **NONE** — joined by **καί** alone |

## 12. ✍️ Manuscript Voice — Not One Word in Common

> **I came to this comparison expecting it to be the easiest of the three. Paul says the dead in Messiah rise; John says they came to life and reigned. Same people, same event, surely.**
>
> **Then I ran the same test that had worked twice before — search each passage for the other's vocabulary. Matthew and Paul had shared two words. Paul's own letters had shared several. Paul and John share none. Not the trumpet, not the cloud, not the coming, not the meeting, not one word for incorruptible or immortal or changed. John writes two verbs, lived and reigned, and neither of them appears anywhere in Paul's resurrection passages.**
>
> **That does not mean they are different events. Two men can describe one thing in entirely different words. But it does mean I have been feeling a strength the words do not supply — the correspondence I keep sensing is structural, and structure is a real kind of evidence and a weaker one than vocabulary.**
>
> **And then the harder thing. Paul says not all of us will sleep. Some are alive and are changed without dying. John's word for what happens is that they came to life. People who never died cannot come to life. That is not John declining to mention them. It is that his sentence has no room for them.**
>
> **I still think these are the same event. The shape is too close — the same order, the same staging, the same last enemy. But I have to write down that I think it rather than that I have shown it.**
