> ### **THIS IS RECORDED AT FULL STRENGTH BECAUSE IT IS INCONVENIENT. THE PROJECT DOES NOT GET TO SKIP A LEXICAL TEST WHEN THE ANSWER IS COMFORTABLE AND RUN ONE WHEN IT IS NOT.**
