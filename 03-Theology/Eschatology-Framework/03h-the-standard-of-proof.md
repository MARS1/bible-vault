## 3. ❗ WHAT THIS ARCHIVE HAS ACTUALLY BEEN DOING — the misunderstanding, named precisely

**THE CHARGE: that the investigation demands proof before permitting belief.**

> ### ✅ **IT DOES NOT, AND THE FILES SHOW IT. EVERY LEDGER IN THIS ARCHIVE GRADES ITS FINDINGS: **ESTABLISHED · STRONGLY SUPPORTED · PROBABLE · OPEN · REFUSED.** THOSE ARE GRADED CONVICTIONS, RECORDED AS SUCH.**
>
> ### **THE ARCHIVE SAYS ***PROBABLE*** ABOUT MATTHEW ⟷ 2 THESSALONIANS. IT SAYS ***HIGH*** ABOUT PAUL'S INTERNAL CHAIN. IT SAYS ***VERY HIGH*** ABOUT THE SHARED THOUSAND-YEAR INTERVAL. **THOSE ARE BELIEFS WITH LABELS ON THEM.**

**❗ WHAT IT REFUSES IS NOT CONVICTION. IT IS ***UPGRADING THE LABEL*** — ADR-0010: *contextually favored must never become lexically established.***

> ### 📌 **AND THAT RULE ***PROTECTS*** THE CONVICTION RATHER THAN BLOCKING IT. A BELIEF LABELLED *PROBABLE* SURVIVES SOMEONE CHECKING IT. A BELIEF LABELLED *ESTABLISHED* THAT WAS ONLY EVER *PROBABLE* COLLAPSES THE MOMENT ANYONE CHECKS — AND TAKES EVERYTHING SHELVED BESIDE IT DOWN TOO.**
>
> ### ❗ **THE PROJECT'S REFUSALS HAVE NEVER BEEN "you may not believe this." THEY HAVE BEEN "this is not the KIND of evidence you just called it" (q386).**

- ❌ **a specific commencement year**

- ❌ **a historical terminus, or that we are presently in the little season**

> ### ❗ **THAT IS THE PRACTICAL PAYOFF OF GRADED CONVICTION: YOU CAN HOLD **A** AT *SUPPORTS* WHILE **B**, **C** AND **D** STAY OPEN. THE UNCERTAINTY DOWNSTREAM DOES NOT LEAK UPSTREAM.**
