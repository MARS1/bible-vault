# Scripture Index

Every verse used across this framework, grouped by theme rather than book order, with **full quoted text in both translations** — CJB (Complete Jewish Bible) primary per your reading preference, ESV as the standard cross-reference. All text pulled from a live source (biblegateway.com) on 2026-08-08, not composed from memory — an appendix, not a fifth box. Cross-check here before assuming a verse was dropped between tranches.

## Seeking Wisdom & Testing

**Proverbs 18:15**
CJB: "The mind of a person with discernment gets knowledge, and the ear of the wise seeks knowledge."
ESV: "An intelligent heart acquires knowledge, and the ear of the wise seeks knowledge."

**Proverbs 18:17**
CJB: "The first to state his case seems right, till the other one comes and cross-examines."
ESV: "The one who states his case first seems right, until the other comes and examines him."

**1 Thessalonians 5:19–21** — the opening verse for this whole framework and any write-up of it (see [[00-index]])
CJB: "Don't quench the Spirit, don't despise inspired messages. But do test everything — hold onto what is good,"
ESV: "Do not quench the Spirit. Do not despise prophecies, but test everything; hold fast what is good."

**Romans 11:34**
CJB: "For, 'Who has known the mind of the Lord? Who has been his counselor?'"
ESV: "For who has known the mind of the Lord, or who has been his counselor?"

**Ecclesiastes 1:13** — the Solomon "search/seek all things" verse, located 2026-08-08 (see [[04-open-questions]] item 11 for the correction history)
CJB: "I wisely applied myself to seek out and investigate everything done under heaven. What a bothersome task God has given humanity to keep us occupied!"
ESV: "And I applied my heart to seek and to search out by wisdom all that is done under heaven. It is an unhappy business that God has given to the children of man to be busy with."

**Ecclesiastes 7:25**
CJB: "So I turned myself and my thoughts to know, search out and seek wisdom and the reasons behind things, also to know how foolish it is to be wicked and how stupid to act like a fool."
ESV: "I turned my heart to know and to search out and to seek wisdom and the scheme of things, and to know the wickedness of folly and the foolishness that is madness."

*Note: "test all things" (1 Thessalonians 5:21, above) is Paul's instruction, not Solomon's — the two get blended in memory but are distinct verses from distinct authors making a related but different point (Solomon: search/seek wisdom into all things; Paul: test claims/prophecies specifically).*

**Proverbs 18:13** — CJB: "To answer someone before hearing him out is both stupid and embarrassing." / ESV: "If one gives an answer before he hears, it is his folly and shame."
**Proverbs 2:1–6** — CJB: "if you seek it as you would silver and search for it as for hidden treasure — then you will understand the fear of Adonai and find knowledge of God." / ESV: "if you seek it like silver and search for it as for hidden treasures, then you will understand the fear of the Lord and find the knowledge of God."
**Proverbs 4:7** — CJB: "The beginning of wisdom is: get wisdom! And along with all your getting, get insight!" / ESV: "The beginning of wisdom is this: Get wisdom, and whatever you get, get insight."
**Proverbs 14:15** — CJB: "One who doesn't think believes every word, but the cautious understands his steps." / ESV: "The simple believes everything, but the prudent gives thought to his steps."
**Isaiah 40:13** — CJB: "Who has measured the Spirit of Adonai? Who has been his counselor, instructing him?" / ESV: "Who has measured the Spirit of the Lord, or what man shows him his counsel?"
**Romans 11:33–36** — CJB: "O the depth of the riches and the wisdom and knowledge of God! How inscrutable are his judgments! How unsearchable are his ways!... For from him and through him and to him are all things. To him be the glory forever! Amen." / ESV: "Oh, the depth of the riches and wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable are his judgments and how inscrutable his ways!... For from him and through him and to him are all things. To him be glory forever. Amen."
**1 John 4:1** — CJB: "don't trust every spirit. On the contrary, test the spirits to see whether they are from God." / ESV: "do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from God."
**1 John 2:27** — CJB: "the Messianic anointing you received from the Father remains in you... as his Messianic anointing continues to teach you about all things." / ESV: "the anointing that you received from him abides in you... his anointing teaches you about everything."
**1 Corinthians 14:29** — CJB: "Let two or three prophets speak, while the others weigh what is said." / ESV: "Let two or three prophets speak, and let the others weigh what is said."

## Worship — Directed to the Father / Received by the Son (open tension, see [[04-open-questions]] item 10)

Researched 2026-08-08. This is a genuine, two-sided textual tension, not resolved here — see the open-questions entry for how to hold it.

**Texts reading as Yeshua directing worship/honor toward the Father:**
