# Deferred Historical-Hypotheses Register

> # 🛑 **DEFERRED — DO NOT USE AS BIBLICAL EVIDENCE**
>
> ### **Nothing in this register may be cited in support of any interpretation of any biblical text. Not as corroboration, not as illustration, not as "interesting parallel." Not yet, and not without independent historical evidence established first.**

---

## Why This File Exists

**Established at Side Branch 27½ (2026-08-16).** A question about Revelation 20's post-millennial nations arrived together with a set of claims about the recent past. **The biblical question turned out to have a biblical answer** — Revelation 20:3 already places nations inside the thousand years — **but the episode exposed a live methodological risk:**

> ### **External historical anomalies were being reached for to solve a biblical problem BEFORE it had been verified that the problem existed.**

**Reversing that order is how a research project quietly becomes an apologetic for a pre-existing theory.**

**This register is the alternative to both dismissal and premature use.** Per **ADR-0010**, nothing substantive is discarded — **preservation without promotion.**

---

## The Required Order of Operations

> ### **Bible → textual model** *(established independently)*
> ### **then: historical evidence → independent test**
>
> ### **NEVER: interesting historical theory → search Scripture for verses that resemble it.**

**Once the biblical chronology is established as far as it can be established on its own, the legitimate question becomes:**

> **Does external history CORROBORATE, CONTRADICT, or remain NEUTRAL toward the model?**

**All three are real answers. "Neutral" is the most likely and is not a disappointment.**

---

## The Governing Rule

> ### **Compatibility–Evidence Distinction** *(methodology v2.6)*
>
> **A hypothesis being COMPATIBLE with Scripture does not constitute EVIDENCE that the hypothesis occurred.** Biblical texts may rule possibilities in or out where they speak directly; historical claims require independent historical evidence. **Never convert "Scripture does not prohibit X" into "Scripture predicts or confirms X."**
>
> ### **COULD FIT ≠ DOES FIT ≠ PROVES** — three distinct categories, never merged.

## Standing Instructions

1. **Add to this register freely.** Nothing is lost by recording an item here.
2. **Never cite an item from this register in a biblical stage file.** If one seems relevant, that is a signal to re-check whether the biblical problem is real — **which is exactly what happened at Side Branch 27½, where it was not.**
3. **To move an item out of this register**, it must be independently researched to the standard of [[02-historical-observations]] — primary sources named, mainstream assessment recorded, overstated portions of the original framing identified.
4. **Moving an item out of the register still does not make it biblical evidence.** It makes it a historical finding that may later be compared against a model derived independently from the texts.

> ### **The register is not a waiting room for confirmation. It is a holding area for questions that have not yet been asked properly.**
