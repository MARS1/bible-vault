# AUDIT 1 — Research machinery leaking into reader-facing prose

**Governing rule: `SCRIPTURE-COMPLETE-001`** *(methodology, retrieval family)*.

## Classification scheme

| | Category | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **MACHINERY LEAK** — a tool, agent, lane, API or retrieval is narrated as an actor or as an event in the story | ❗ **Must be repaired** |
| **B** | **INFRASTRUCTURE VOCABULARY** — a legitimate evidence status *(this is reported, not verified)* stated in retrieval words | ⚠️ **Reword, do not delete.** The content is the book's spine; only the register is wrong |
| **C** | **LEGITIMATE** — metaphor *(the investigation's "favourite tool")*, ancient documents genuinely being partial, or evidence status in ordinary language | ✅ **Keep** |

## The flagship case, and it is the one that was predicted

> ### ❗❗❗ **EN `ch40` opens with a section titled *"Three retrievals came back broken, and I am saying so before I argue"* — followed by a table whose columns are `Passage | What came back | What was missing`.**

**That is a table of API output in a book about Revelation.**

★★ **And the passage involved is the exact example the rule was written around.** `ch40` L179:

> *"The cry, the robes and* rest a little longer *are in verses 10–11, which is the part of the retrieval that came back truncated. They are reported, not verified."*

❗ **Revelation 6:9–11 was requested. Verses 10 and 11 did not come back. The chapter argues from 6:9 and tells the reader about the truncation instead of retrieving 6:10 and 6:11 individually.** ✅ **This is precisely the failure `SCRIPTURE-COMPLETE-001` step 2 exists to prevent, and the repair is not to delete the sentence — it is to retrieve the verses.**

⚠️ **ES `ch40` carries the identical section**, *"Tres recuperaciones volvieron rotas"*, with the same table.

## ⚠️ The judgement call I am flagging rather than deciding

**Some A-category instances are carrying real intellectual content that shaped the investigation.**

- EN `ch41`: *the comparison across retrievals is not delegable* — a genuine methodological finding, and both of that chapter's best results came from it.
- EN `ch45`: a source's conclusion contradicted its own data table twelve lines earlier, and catching that mattered.
- EN `ch38`: a versification discrepancy shifted every label in the chapter — that **did** materially change the reasoning.

📐 **`SCRIPTURE-COMPLETE-001` already answers this in principle: narrate the intellectual correction, never the software malfunction.** *"I compared two sources side by side and found what neither report contained"* keeps the finding and drops the machinery.

> ### ❗ **BUT THE QUESTION UNDERNEATH IS THE AUTHOR'S, NOT MINE: Part VII's character is an adversarial trial in which the investigator audits his own apparatus. How much of that is part of the book's identity, and how much is plumbing?** A full scrub keeps the reader inside the argument. It also removes a visible reason to trust the investigator. **I have not decided this, and the repair pass should not start until it is decided.**

## Repair ordering, when authorized

### **EN VI → ES VI → EN VII → ES VII**

🛑 **And for every affected Scripture passage the repair is NOT deleting the embarrassing sentence.** It is: **retrieve the complete passage · verify it into the Evidence Vault · confirm the manuscript's conclusion still follows from the complete text.** ❗ **If the complete verses change the argument, the argument changes.**

⚠️ **Completeness is verified per translation witness.** A complete English passage does not establish a complete RVR1960 one.

# AUDIT 2 — First-appearance person orientation

**Governing rule: `PERSON-INTRO-001`** *([[EDITORIAL-RULES]])*.

## The finding that explains the rest

> ### ❗❗ **The `person` component already exists in the design system. It has been used FOUR times in the entire manuscript — all in Parts I–II, all for *biblical* figures: John the Baptist and Ezekiel, in both editions.**
>
> ### 🛑 **It has never once been used for an extra-biblical witness — the category where a reader most needs it, and where the testimony actually carries evidentiary weight.**

📐 **The tool was built, applied to the easy cases early, and abandoned exactly where it was needed.** That is worth recording as a pattern, not just a gap.

## Priority

### **① `ch34` — Irenaeus, Nero, Domitian arrive together and carry the dating argument.**
### **② `ch36` — Eusebius, who is quoted *about* Irenaeus. Both need capsules for the sentence to mean anything.**
### **③ `ch41` — Theodotion. 39 uses, zero orientation, and it is a text-tradition rather than a person, which compounds the problem.**
### **④ Everything else.**

⚠️ **`Clement` and `Titus` need disambiguation before a capsule is written, not after.**

## Constraints on the repair

✅ **Track globally** — Josephus is introduced once, in Part V, and not again in Part VI.
🛑 **Capsules orient; they do not argue.** No capsule may pre-decide the evidentiary weight its chapter is about to assign — which matters most for Irenaeus and Eusebius, whose weight is precisely what `ch36` is adjudicating.
⚠️ **Both editions, together.** A capsule added to EN without its ES sibling is a new component-parity divergence.
