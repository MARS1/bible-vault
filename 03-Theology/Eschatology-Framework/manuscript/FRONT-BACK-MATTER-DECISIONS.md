# Front and back matter — decision sheet

> **How to read this sheet**
>
> 1. **Skim Tables 1 and 2** (about 2 minutes). They show every piece that goes around the chapters, in printed order, and whether it exists yet. Nothing to decide there. Just check that nothing important is missing.
> 2. **Answer the 11 decisions at the end** (about 10 minutes). Each one has my recommendation. Write **✓** to accept it, **✗** to reject it, or a short note. A one-word reply per number is enough.
> 3. **Ignore everything marked "later".** Title page, copyright page, ISBN and the table of contents are produced during publishing prep and layout, not now.

## Table 1 — Front matter (before Chapter 1)

| # | Piece | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| i | Title page | Book title, author | later (layout) |
| ii | Copyright page | Legal notice plus Bible-translation permissions | later (publishing prep) |
| iii | Dedication | One line, or none | **your call: D1** |
| iv | Foreword: why this began | How the question started | ✅ written, EN + ES |

**Why vi–ix-b matter:** the Note to the Reader already tells readers these pages exist. If the book ships without them, it breaks a promise on its second page.

## Table 2 — Back matter (after the Coda)

The architecture's governing rule: **an appendix may never hide evidence a reader needs to judge a chapter.** Appendices hold the *exhaustive* version; the chapter keeps what decides the argument.

| | Appendix | What goes in it | Source exists? |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Open questions | Questions the book deliberately leaves open | ✅ `04-open-questions` (441 items; must be filtered: **D6**) |

| D | Word study index | Every word studied: what it establishes, permits, does not establish | ✅ in the chapters' word-study boxes |
