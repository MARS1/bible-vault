# About the author

<img class="author-photo" src="../img/author-headshot-circle.png" alt="Marcelo Mario Berta Silva">

**Marcelo Mario Berta Silva** first read Revelation in Washington, DC, around 2001, and spent the next two decades coming back to the questions it raised. This book is the investigation those questions eventually demanded.

He describes himself as a follower of YahuShua HaMashiach, a Gentile grafted into Israel's covenant olive tree.

He lives in Montevideo, Uruguay, where he builds software applications, writes and sings. He has been married to his wife, Guisela, for twenty-four years, and they have two children, Fabiana and Elias. He speaks English and Spanish, and he loves to travel, to learn new things, to write code, and to walk along La Rambla, Montevideo's long waterfront.

He is also the author of *The Idea Vault* (2026), a practical guide to building a personal system for capturing and keeping your ideas, using open, plain-text tools so that nothing worth keeping gets lost. It is available at [kollabs.ai/tools/idea-vault](https://kollabs.ai/tools/idea-vault).

**Contact:** [kuality.design](https://kuality.design) · [kollabs.ai](https://kollabs.ai) · [mars@kuality.design](mailto:mars@kuality.design)
