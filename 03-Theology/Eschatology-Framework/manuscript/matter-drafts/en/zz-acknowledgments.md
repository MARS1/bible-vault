# Acknowledgments

This book did not begin with me, and it was not finished by me alone.

It began with a relative who would not let a question go. He pressed me for days, and when I could not show my work, he was right to keep asking.

I could not have read the texts the way this book reads them without the people who made them freely available: the Society of Biblical Literature, for the SBL Greek New Testament; the MorphGNT project, which made it possible to count every word in it; and Sefaria, for the Hebrew Scriptures. Scholars spent lifetimes on the editions I opened in seconds.

To my wife, Guisela, and to our children, Fabiana and Elias: thank you for the patience this book asked of you, through the long evenings when I was somewhere in the first century.

The mistakes that remain are mine. Several earlier ones are recorded in Appendix G, because finding them was part of the work.
